# Student quality tradeoff R1

This folder holds a source-bound, not-yet-admitted comparison of continued label supervision against continued clean-T3 distillation from the same warm H256×2 student parent. The proposed intervention changes only `--distill-alpha`; it does not test cold-start distillation or remove the initializer's earlier distillation ancestry.

- [Matched warm-parent alpha-0 packet](MATCHED_WARMPARENT_ALPHA0_PACKET_R1.md) specifies the exact checkpoint/cache/split/source pins, the single argument difference, two-epoch recoverable stages, fixed checkpoint evaluations at epochs 2/4/6, and the known resource bound. It authorizes no fit and requires fresh review/admission.
- [Machine-readable packet](MATCHED_WARMPARENT_ALPHA0_PACKET_R1.json) gives the frozen source/weight/membership/cache hashes, current alpha-.9 reference checkpoint hashes, stage boundary mapping, and resource estimates for later launch construction.
- [Quality bottleneck evidence synthesis](../../unified/ideas/QUALITY_BOTTLENECK_AND_STUDENT_TRADEOFFS_20261015_R1.md) is the campaign's current interpretation of the exposed-F0 student and teacher evidence; the [independent review](../../unified/reviews/CLEAN_T3_TEACHER_STUDENT_BOTTLENECK_REVIEW_20261015_R1.md) records the critic's source and comparison checks.

- [P1 fit contract](ALPHA0_P1_FIT_CONTRACT_R2.json), [P2 fit contract](ALPHA0_P2_FIT_CONTRACT_R2.json), and [P3 fit contract](ALPHA0_P3_FIT_CONTRACT_R2.json) clone the exact successful alpha-.9 worker vectors with `--distill-alpha 0.0`, unique run names, and the matching alpha-0 predecessor state for P2/P3. Each remains unlaunched and requires an independent delta review, fresh root release/admission, resume-state validation, and output-absence recheck.
- [Launch templates](ALPHA0_LAUNCH_TEMPLATES_R2.json) give the exact controller and worker argument vectors for the three sequential stages under the pinned one-GPU resource envelope. They are templates, not admission or launch authorization.
- [Post-fit export and grade commands](ALPHA0_POSTFIT_EXPORT_GRADE_COMMANDS_R1.json) bind six feature-only FP32 exports and official-metric grades at epochs 2/4/6 for both arms. Intermediate checkpoints are descriptive; epoch 6 is the fixed primary endpoint. Every command requires a fresh resource release and output preflight, and no command has been run.
- [Source-bound materialization validation](ALPHA0_SOURCEBOUND_VALIDATION_R2.json) verifies the frozen trainer/source snapshot, split, parent, cache metadata, all three terminal alpha-.9 attempts, exact alpha argument diffs, stage paths, and six planned post-fit snapshots. Result: `PASS_SOURCE_PINS_AND_COMMAND_CONSTRUCTION; NO_FIT_NO_LAUNCH`.
- [Materializer](materialize_alpha0_contracts.py) is a task-specific builder for these contracts. The [preserved first builder failure](ALPHA0_MATERIALIZER_FAILURE_R1.json) records a `StopIteration` caused by assuming the P2 control binding was in the env-prefixed P3 receipt; it was a builder lookup error, not a source or recipe failure. The corrected builder uses the separate successful P2 attempt.

Status: prepared from metadata, source and existing receipts only. No new fit, prediction, grade, provider action or resource admission was performed.
