[execute.py](execute.py) uses the original immutable panel and canonical trainer, the separately corrected `runtime/pr15-host-v2` delivery adapters, and frozen R01 process/resource helpers. It excludes the 16 already trained cells, validates their recovered weights, requests, update counts, device identities and official scores, and requires the original PR15 unit and all its workers to have drained. Two PR15 lanes per GPU keep their original CPU pairs and source/recipe/seed/fold bindings. The original absolute campaign deadline is inherited; each fit remains bounded at 12 hours. Admission failures drain healthy workers rather than cancelling them; failed fits are never silently retried.
