# Bounded reporting triggers

Two oneshot services share a nonblocking publication lock. `tabdata-github-updates.timer` checks every10minutes with persistence; `tabdata-github-completions.path` watches completion-only receipt directories and terminal summary paths. The completion service waits20seconds to coalesce a cohort. Neither watches two-second heartbeat journals. Services have8GiB memory, swap disabled,128tasks, CPU affinity72/74, CUDA hidden and300second timeout. They stop only their own timer/path after successful terminal reporting or the expiry horizon. Inspect with `systemctl --user list-timers tabdata-github-updates.timer` and `systemctl --user status tabdata-github-completions.path`; publication receipts and source/config hashes are kept in the parent packet.
