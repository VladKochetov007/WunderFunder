**Status: proposed design only — unadmitted, not preregistered, not runnable, and not an exact public-TL3 replica.** This packet gives root a concrete scientific recipe to criticize and, if appropriate, turn into a separately reviewed draft. It does not change the frozen rank-1 files, authorize code implementation or a fit, or establish public transfer.

The machine-readable settings, stage graph, complete 30-cell matrix, family mapping and source pins are in [`recipe.json`](recipe.json). Its evidence pins bind the incumbent analysis, closure/readiness receipts, metadata-only checkpoint records, archived source method and the existing CP30 authority.

## Question and comparison

The proposed question is whether a clean-fold, fixed-weight three-member TL3-like bundle improves the official local score over the frozen rank-1 `deep` H384×2 labels-only endpoint, across four source-qualified folds and 30 paired fold/seed cells. The candidate is a package-level comparison: it uses three final models, a learned propensity path, a multi-stage teacher graph, a family cache and a tied-lead branch; `deep` is one labels-only model. Any difference is not an isolated estimate of distillation, features, optimizer or tiedlead, and candidate training/inference costs are larger. Every future comparison must report actual work and latency; this memo makes no throughput estimate.

Within each fold, fit one clean teacher/propensity graph using fixed role seeds 0, 3, 4 and 5, then reuse that graph for the fold’s eight or seven final-student seed cells. This makes the CP30 estimate conditional on those fixed ancestors. It does not estimate propensity/teacher-seed variation. The final student seeds for cell seed `S` are `S`, `S+10000`, and `S+20000` for the distilled, tiedlead-A and tiedlead-B members. The comparison control remains the existing rank-1 `deep` run at seed `S`; nothing in rank-1 is edited or replaced.

The exact cell allocation is the frozen 8/8/7/7 matrix: F0 and F1 use seeds 1001–1008; F2 and F3 use 1001–1007. Pair the candidate and `deep` on the same fold manifest, ordered evaluation rows, provider and runtime. A cell that cannot meet that pairing rule is not part of the paired primary result. Use `global_wp_clip2_v1`; average seed deltas within each fold and then average the four folds equally. Preserve paired seed identities in 10,000 bootstrap draws, with folds fixed, plus the conditional whole-component intervals. The existing +0.001 practical screen is copied only as a proposal from the rank-1 decision addendum; because this TL3 design is written after rank-1 outcomes have begun, it is not a prospectively frozen rule for this study. Freeze or replace it before any candidate outcome is read.

The official metric retains its prescribed scoring mask, ±2 prediction/label clipping and clipped-label-magnitude weights. This proposed study is empirical Tier B evidence, but the four folds are exposed: it can support development ranking and robustness on this panel, not untouched confirmation. Public leaderboard transfer remains a separate outcome.

All four folds are exposed from prior research. This is discovery/robustness evidence, not a fresh confirmation set, and does not estimate a public score. Do not tune weights, pick members or select another cache variant using the public leaderboard.

## Tied-lead implementation requirement

The two 0.33 members are scratch 263-input GRU256×2 students trained for four epochs on `train+valid`, WMSE, propensity power 2/epsilon 0.03, batch 32/chunk 1000, alpha 0.5, bootstrap horizon 8, Muon 0.001×6, weight decay 0.01, OneCycle (5% warmup, final division factor 100), seeded epoch order, fixed epoch 3, no EMA. Their teacher target is the equal mean of clean `fin256ft_s3/s4@ep1`. Their seeds are the per-cell offsets above.

A focused canonical adapter is missing. The concrete proposal ports the pinned archived `tiedlead` semantics: base 211-channel projection plus a shared `104→128→64` GELU book encoder over each instrument’s 52 raw channels and row-to-row delta, a zero-initialized `128→256` residual from the two instrument embeddings, and a zero-initialized `64→256` lead residual computed with the same encoder from the second instrument’s 52-channel block and its difference from that instrument’s last first-instrument event. Use `RS_LEAD=1`, archived `RS_TIED_DIMS=128,64`, and the exact 52-channel order from the pinned feature map. The source snapshot is an architecture lead, not proof of the bytes executed in the historical jobs. Before preregistration, test causality, feature ordering, initialization, checkpoint/export shape and numerical behavior; until that adapter exists and passes review, the recipe cannot run.

## Failure handling and evidence

Strict FP32 means no autocast, `NVIDIA_TF32_OVERRIDE=0`, `allow_tf32=false` for both CUDA backends, and highest FP32 matmul precision. Record observed runtime flags. Every stage and cache must carry source/config hashes, split SHA, ordered fit/evaluation group digests, row/component mapping, preprocessing identity, optimizer/scheduler configuration, seed, full epoch/update/skip counts and selected fixed epoch. Cache generation must reject mismatched source rows before arithmetic. Final predictions are FP32 two-column arrays with exact eval row digests. Compute the fixed blend only after all three members align; use the official grader and retain member and mixture metrics, component deltas, failed attempts and exact outputs.

A cell fails its quality gate if any ancestor includes evaluation groups, source/cache identities are incomplete, a target has nonmatching rows, the adapter fails its checks, a prediction is nonfinite, or successful/skipped update accounting is not resolved. Preserve failed evidence; do not silently retry or count interrupted work as a fit. A positive result supports only this whole bundle on the exposed panel. A zero-crossing interval is inconclusive, not a universal stop. No local delta predicts a public score.
