SCALE / CLIP / REALIZED STEP / METRIC — DISTINCT CONDITIONAL OBJECTS
Source online order: scale(total).backward -> unscale_(optimizer) -> observe stats
-> GLOBAL clip_grad_norm -> scaler.step -> scaler.update -> scheduler/EMA clocks.
Component producer differentiates scale(term) separately and divides by scale.
Therefore both intended gradients are preclip/unscaled, but that algebraic unit
match does not remove finite-precision operation differences. Never compare
component sum to CLIPPED gradients or divide captured components by scale twice.
Exact pre/post scale and skip clocks must come from accepted payload, not this toy.
A common positive clipping coefficient scales total force and all putative component
vectors together; cannot separately clip each component then sum. The toy clip
[3,4] with clip1 gives coefficient.199999960000008 under the declared1e-6 stabilizer;
this illustrates source operation, not measured actual clipping or per-loss steps.

For supported ACTIVE MuonAdamW/AdamW parameters, source applies
  theta_after=(1-lr*wd)*theta_before + nonlinear_data_update.
Thus delta+lr*wd*theta_before removes decoupled decay in exact arithmetic. This
requires actual group lr/decay, pre-weight and executed parameter update; observed
fp32 delta/correction retains numerical roundoff. Unused/skipped states do not
satisfy that active-update identity. First-order g_k dot observed delta is a local
Taylor diagnostic, not exact loss change; curvature, momentum, clipping and
orthogonalization prevent additive per-loss optimizer attribution.
Frozen incoming Adam diagonal1/(sqrt(v/(1-beta2^step))+eps) is correct as a labeled
positive diagnostic metric. Actual NEXT update uses updated m/v and step+1. Toy
surrogate-.0013624656 versus actual Adam formula-.0021030597 shows the distinction.
Muon has no analogous Adam diagonal; do not invent one or sum hypothetical Muon
component steps. Positive component normalization preserves pairwise cosine sign,
not necessarily cancellation/total direction under unequal scaling.
The adapter's adam_frozen_metric aggregates ALL Adam parameters, not a shared-only
Adam metric. A shared Euclidean witness plus aggregate Adam witness cannot prove
preregistered shared-conflict survival in the Adam metric. Scope outputs explicitly;
if that shared metric criterion is required, its matched subtotal is unavailable.
