The apparent conflict is resolved by separating controls and mechanisms. Event
history beating a current-only ridge says recent information helps; it does not
show event history beats row history, survives a recurrent strong baseline, or
reveals an exact target horizon. The four analyses agree on that distinction.

The strongest shared narrow lead is causal state/activity/age conditioning, with
different effects on t0 and t1. It overlaps the nonlinear offset candidate. Test
that cheap candidate once against the strong fold-bound student before adding
new architectures or allocating GPU time. Preserve all frozen negative gates;
post-score regime diagnostics are proposals, not confirmations.
