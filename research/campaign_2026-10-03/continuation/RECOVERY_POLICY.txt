Periodic crash recovery policy

The persistent watcher polls once per minute. It consumes journal cursors,
reconciles GPU running/waiting records with systemd, and watches the aggregate
slice's cumulative OOM counter. CPU wrappers retain terminal scope journals.
Failures and malformed metadata stop admissions; monitoring permission/bus
errors are visible as degraded coverage. Independent service inspection also
fails closed at launch. Incident identities and acknowledgement reasons survive
watcher restarts. No failed output is silently relabelled successful.

Controller response while this research session is active:
1. Save incident, traceback, unit result, scope memory events, effective limits,
   last completed epoch, prediction .partial path, and current source hashes.
2. Distinguish admission/controller failure from actual training failure, GPU
   allocator exhaustion, host OOM, timeout, bad data, or a scientific-code bug.
3. Correct the observed cause. For memory pressure: reduce loader workers and
   prefetch first; stream/chunk arrays; lower batch only if needed and preregister
   the resulting scientific change. Keep global limits unchanged.
4. Preserve original record and partial artifacts. Use a fresh job/model tag;
   resume only from a complete, validated checkpoint with matching provenance.
   Never overwrite a successful export or truncate a failed log.
5. Record the revised command/conditions and rationale. Acknowledge only the
   diagnosed incident IDs, then release the owned pause. Repeated failure of the
   revised attempt closes the arm until a new diagnosis is available.

The watchdog itself diagnoses evidence and pauses; it does not invent a retry
configuration or restart arbitrary failed experiments. CPU admission exit75
before Python starts may be retried after controller repair: no fit ran.
Service restart-on-failure is used only for the lightweight watcher. Hardware
or data errors are never treated as a successful negative scientific result.
