QUALIFYING 'ANY HORIZON'
1. Entry inclusion is essential. The actor's trailing rule initializes at entry;
   its fixed16-event rule excludes entry. The algebra does NOT reject every
   entry-excluding fixed-extrema rule at every horizon. The actor's H16 mismatch
   is a bounded empirical result only. The independent fixture with entry
   B0=100,A0=101 and future B=97,A=98 gives entry-inclusive returns[-1,2], but
   entry-excluding/execution returns[-4,2]. Only the latter violates the bound.
2. Raw versus clipped: every417 replayed mapped outside violation survives
   clipping target to[-2,2] against the SAME raw bound. This is panel-specific.
   The synthetic raw label-4 with bound-3 violates before clipping but not at-2.
   Clipping bound/prediction too, affine recalibration, fees or nonlinear target
   transforms are different hypotheses. The registered raw algebra cannot be
   applied to those without proving the return/scale convention.
3. Positive quantum and spread-coordinate identification matter. Table class
   gating is input-code-only; missing maps cannot be filled using target scale.
   Interior sum agreement is supervised coordinate transport, not identification
   of physical spread in outside states or evidence of stable future references.
4. Censoring does not qualify the LOWER bound for a mapped entry-inclusive
   return, but does qualify empirical future-path reconstruction and touch claims.
   Of417 mapped outside origins, complete/unresolved pairs are302/115 for fixed,
   279/138 for barriers,260/157 for trailing. Every unresolved mapped outside
   origin also violates. The157 trailing unresolved include unknown transitions,
   cap censorship and sequence end. No unknown gap was bridged or future stop
   inferred in this audit; reasons were checked from frozen origin metadata,
   not independently rerun through the actor's path engine.
