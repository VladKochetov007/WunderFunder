PRIMARY SOURCES ACTUALLY READ (no unread citation relied on)
1. Neelakantan et al., Adding Gradient Noise Improves Learning for Very Deep
Networks (2015), PDF section3/page3 equation1 and neighboring discussion:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.06807
The tested variance law follows their annealed-noise method. Prior success on deep
optimization tasks is not evidence of benefit for this already distilled GRU.
2. Loshchilov & Hutter, SGDR (2016/2017), full HTML section3 equation5 and discussion:
https://arxiv.org/html/1608.03983v5
Cosine reheating retains model state. Applying that schedule to Adam/Muon here is
an experimental extension; no published GRU speedup is inferred.
3. Krueger et al., Zoneout (2016), primary arXiv abstract:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01305
Zoneout preserves previous per-timestep hidden values. Inspection of RNNModel's
nn.GRU(z,h) call shows no access to internal timestep states. Thus exact fused
zoneout is explicitly unsupported; cell-loop speed penalty is unmeasured.
4. Owen, On dropping the first Sobol' point, PDF introduction/section2 and returned
smooth additive example discussion; sections inspected via web open/find:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.08051
Scrambling preserves uniform marginals with dependent samples; dyadic nets and
smooth/simple effective structure matter. Our discrete adaptive SGD mapping has
no proved QMC rate. Avoid dropping/thinning nets; truncated non-dyadic tensor rows
lose exact balance. Inference: finite strata help when they explain gradients,
and order reversal helps only if paired training responses have negative covariance.
5. SciPy Sobol official implementation docs, API/scramble/power-of-two warnings:
https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol.html
Used scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol scramble=True, random_base2, private per-tensor seeds.
The docs' LMS+shift implementation is not asserted to be full nested Owen scramble.
Owen1997 publisher page was inaccessible; Stanford landing page had metadata only.
No theorem was attributed to those unread texts. An initial mistaken zoneout arXiv
ID opened unrelated caching literature; discarded, correct primary source above.
