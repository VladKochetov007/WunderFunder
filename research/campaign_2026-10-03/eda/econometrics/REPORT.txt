METHOD AND CAUSALITY
48 training sequences, 24 validation sequences, uniformly seeded selection.
Train groups exclude lattice300, train0, and inferred seed2 ACF120; the NPZ has
120 sequences but does not store original group IDs. Unknown event-delta training
exposure prevents claiming fully fresh training. ALL validation was already
exposed in own valid_seq_summary.csv. Followup uses the same now score-exposed IDs.
Each sequence is streamed independently; only its current/past 112 raw features
enter models. 22 i0 price columns are used without assuming depth ordering.
Rows 0–98 update history; all state resets at sequence boundaries. No targets,
mask, full-sequence lattice, or IDs are inputs. Global feature scaling is fit on
training only. Prefix/full feature parity at 777 rows: maximum difference 0.
Training: every tenth required row, 95,568 samples. Validation: 31,658 exact
need_prediction AND is_scored rows. Predict every required row regardless of mask.
Target-specific ridge minimizes abs(clipped target)-weighted squared error;
penalty = 0.01 mean non-intercept standardized Gram diagonal. Intercept unpenalized.
All scoring clips finite targets/predictions to +/-2 and pools additive moments
across sequences before computing target-wise correlations and their equal mean.
No row bootstrap, confidence interval, independent replication, or public forecast.

METRIC-OPTIMAL ESTIMAND
Ignoring prediction clipping, population weighted Pearson is ordinary Pearson
under the tilted measure dQ proportional to |clip(Y)| dP. Its optimal predictor
is affine to E_Q[clip(Y)|X] = E[|clip(Y)|clip(Y)|X]/E[|clip(Y)||X]. Weighted MSE
estimates this ratio; ordinary MSE need not. Prediction clipping and restricted
linear function classes break the unrestricted affine optimum. This is a model
argument, not evidence that every weighted fit beats an unweighted control.
