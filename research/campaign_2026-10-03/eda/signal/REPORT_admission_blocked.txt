FROZEN MATCHED EXPERIMENT READY FOR CONTROLLER REVIEW
clock_probe.py compares shared i0_p, own group, every-row, and prefix-rate clocks.
All use 112 level inputs + 112 fast-band residuals + 3 sentinel indicators +
6 identical clock covariates + intercept (234 columns), alpha .25, ridge .001.
Own groups: i0p, i0v, i0d, i1p, i1v, i1d, a. Change indicators use current and
previous input rows. Prefix-rate periodic control estimates group event rates
from rows 1..98; rows 0..98 use the shared clock. Thus it is causal, approximately
rate matched, but not exactly event-count matched over the scored interval.
Weighted ridge heads fit rows 99..19999 stride 4 of 64 streamed train groups.
All selected valid is_scored rows >=99 are pooled for target-weighted Pearson,
with targets and predictions clipped to +/-2 before computing target weights.
No parameter sweep. Models share training rows, dimension and regularization.
Features receive input arrays only; targets and scoring flags are separate reads.
Prefix equivalence is checked at endings 98, 99, 255, 1023 in two sequences.
Acceptance: own-shared mean WP >.002 and positive in both fixed valid halves.
Timing-specific support additionally needs own-prefix_rate >.002.
Raw additive per-sequence score moments, models, rates, and provenance are saved
by the script for controller reproduction; no target arrays enter inference.
