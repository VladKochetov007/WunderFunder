UNCERTAINTY AND CONTRADICTIONS
400 paired bootstrap resamples over 64 observable exact-prefix components.
Descriptive t1 offset-minus-affine 95% intervals: train [0.004544,0.040622],
scored-valid [-0.003620,0.077863]. These assume independence of observable
components; campaign connected components are unavailable. Do not interpret
them as campaign-valid confidence intervals. No row-independence claim.
The scored-valid interval includes zero despite passing the frozen half test.
Coeff figure: t1 log-age coefficient differs sharply between sources
(approximately -0.020 vs -0.259 per source-standardized unit); individual
coefficients are correlated and do not identify structural causality.
Thus the result weakens my initial preference for nonlinear regime models:
reweighting an existing linear predictor's state coefficients is sufficient
for the observed local gain. Threshold nonlinearity was not needed here.
