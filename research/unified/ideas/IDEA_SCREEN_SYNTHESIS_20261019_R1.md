## Exact negatives and mechanism premises

The tested [R11 fixed initial sampler](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r11/RESULTS.md), [R12 finite correlation-loss schedule](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r12/RESULTS.md) and [R13 immutable mean99 context](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r13/RESULTS.md) lost their primary matched comparisons. Their exact recipes remain closed without outer-driven retuning. These outcomes do not reject their entire mechanism families.

Preserve both sides of the selection evidence: R3 fixed-alpha1 absolute EMA gains+.029060 over raw alpha1, while the fitting-selected endpoint loses .004323 to selected raw. [R4](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r4/RESULTS.md) selected tanh loses to identity, while fixed-alpha1 history retains+.002015. The mismatch is an unresolved transfer/selection issue, not permission to substitute the outer winner. Smaller inner fits, training/evaluation source and mask composition, and regularization remain unseparated.

[R6 gradients](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r6/RESULTS.md), [R9 carry intervention](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r9/RESULTS.md) and [R10 support geometry](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r10/RESULTS.md) establish narrow checkpoint or input premises. They do not measure optimization benefit or label usefulness. Input covariance is not a substitute for gradients or recurrent state.

## Remaining questions and the next-test rule

The [25-card map](../../campaign_2026-10-19/idea_screens_r1/STATUS_25.md) retains NOT_TESTED for the full reflection, sparse-factor, conditional-graph, prior/correction, energy-constraint, matched pole-span basis, covariance-preconditioner, stale-weight state, mixed-curvature penalty and uncertainty-damping mechanisms where appropriate. Learned end-to-end moment heads and several original mechanisms also remain untested beyond their stated premises. Counting screens is not coverage of those mechanisms.

For example, a low-rank cross-product ridge could test the usefulness of adding interaction features; it would not test whether a mixed-curvature penalty removes fragile interactions (#24). It is not admitted here or relabeled as that mechanism. Similarly, a dense covariance rotation is not sparse-factor recovery (#9), and a different-pole EMA is not a same-span conditioning test (#18).

The next useful CPU test needs a faithful intervention and its decisive control, a frozen endpoint/mask/budget and a reason its outcome changes allocation. Selection-alignment analysis may be more valuable than another model, but it must distinguish hypotheses rather than merely redescribe the known ranking mismatch. No automatic context/loss/variance/sampling/carry series, ridge sweep or new framework follows from this note. No additional human permission is introduced for work already within the authorized CPU scope.
