# Groupwise fast/slow Kalman innovation states

**Status: the exposed train-row ridge proxy and a matched two-seed recurrent interaction screen are complete. The additive ridge arm is negative; the recurrent paired effect is tiny and changes sign across seeds on exposed F0. Keep the feature family open without promoting a stable gain.**

## Mechanism

The all-channel Kalman screen found that innovation magnitude varies by feature family and sequence position, while its scalar CUSUM becomes saturated early. This motivates a narrower representation test: retain every raw input, then add three causal statistics for each of the 13 predeclared state groups—fast innovation energy, slow innovation energy and the fast-to-slow log ratio.

The intended mechanism is conditional state information. A fast increase can mark a local change; the slow level can describe the sequence's current background noise; their ratio can indicate a transient departure from that background. The features are computed from input-only, pre-update standardized innovations. All Kalman and EMA state resets at each sequence boundary. No future rows, targets, scoring masks or target feedback enter the extractor.

## Test

The [experiment packet](../../campaign_2026-10-18/online_features_r1/group_fast_slow_r1/README.md) predeclares three matched representations: raw 112 inputs, raw plus the existing scalar clock, and raw plus 39 groupwise fast/slow state features. A two-output weighted ridge screen uses same-row `x[t] → y[t]` alignment after the 99-row warmup; features use every post-warmup row and the fit uses a fixed stride of 10. Components, not rows, define four cross-fit folds. The label-blind predictions are frozen before any held-out labels are opened. Because `train.parquet` has no `is_scored` column, the initial official-mask grader correctly stopped. The separate [train-row proxy grade recipe](../../campaign_2026-10-18/online_features_r1/group_fast_slow_r1/grade_train_proxy_r1.json) uses every target-bearing row from step 99 onward and applies the official metric formula with a different, explicitly nonofficial selection mask.

The source sample contains 32 components previously selected from `fold0.fit.train` for the innovation distribution screen. They are already exposed development data. A one-sequence exact-path profile used 1.122 GB worker memory under a 2 GB cap; the full 32-component label-blind fit/prediction used 1.253 GB under the same cap. Both had zero OOM/max deltas; these are resource receipts, not quality evidence. Component cross-fitting is useful for an allocation decision but does not create a clean validation result or capture seed variation. Any proxy score uses all post-warmup train rows, not an official validation mask, and position bins and paired component resampling remain conditional on these four fixed fits.

## Decision rule

A stable, practical increment over raw that appears across folds can justify a matched recurrent screen. A small or uncertain linear delta does not close Kalman feature research; it only defers this exact fast/slow encoding. A separate B300 draft requires a larger direction: a recurrent interaction with the state features that is materially distinct from existing causal-innovation and learned-Kalman handoffs. No such handoff follows from the earlier distribution screen alone.

## Result and interpretation

The frozen label-blind cross-fit was graded separately as an all-post-warmup train-row proxy because the source has no official `is_scored` mask. Raw scored 0.26885894 WP; raw plus the scalar clock scored 0.26875090 (Δ −0.00010803, paired component-bootstrap 95% interval [−0.00243463, +0.00189382]); raw plus the 39 group states scored 0.25576581 (Δ −0.01309313, interval [−0.03369300, +0.00595564]). The group-state arm lost in every one of the four cross-fit folds and each of the four position bins; its largest bin loss was −0.062789 WP at steps 500–1,999. The scalar clock is effectively tied on this proxy.

This is evidence against using these 39 summaries as a simple additive feature block in the frozen weighted-ridge treatment. It does not establish that the group states lack nonlinear or recurrent conditional value. The 32 components were selected for earlier feature diagnostics and are exposed; the bootstrap conditions on four fixed fitted models and does not include fit-seed or selection uncertainty. The score uses every train target row from step 99 onward under the official metric formula, but this is not the official validation mask or score. Data identity pins the source footer/metadata, not the complete Parquet payload. See the experiment packet and the independent [result review](../reviews/GROUPWISE_FAST_SLOW_PROXY_RESULT_REVIEW_20261018_R1.md).

**Allocation:** defer this exact additive ridge arm. Keep the broader causal Kalman direction open. The recurrent teacher/student version is already covered by [B300 draft PR #17](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/pull/17); no separate draft follows from this screen. The [existing innovation draft #14](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/pull/14) was updated to include the group-state result as adjacent evidence and state that the proxy does not test its projected-innovation arm. If a matched recurrent raw-versus-state interaction is allocated, treat the exposed screen as exploratory evidence and do not use it to claim clean validation or long-sequence online-learning value.

## Recurrent interaction follow-up

The [recurrent packet](../../campaign_2026-10-18/online_features_r1/kalman_recurrent_groupfast_slow_r1/README.md) then tested whether the GRU can use the states nonlinearly while retaining all raw features. It compared computed 39-state inputs against an equal-width all-zero block, with a shared parent checkpoint, fit manifest, seed/order, normalization, and 210 FP32 updates. Active scored `0.67986413` WP versus `0.67981250` for the zero control (`+0.00005163`). A paired 141-component bootstrap on exposed F0 gives conditional 95% interval `[−0.00013143,+0.00027018]`; temporal-bin effects change sign. The independent [recurrent screen review](../reviews/GROUPWISE_FAST_SLOW_RECURRENT_SCREEN_REVIEW_20261018_R1.md) found no score-changing mismatch and recommends no separate B300 draft.

This does not close recurrent use: the result is a one-seed incremental fine-tune over an already trained parent, and its conditional interval excludes neither a small loss nor a useful gain. It also does not validate long-horizon online learning. The feature family can be revisited with a fit-only scaling/bounding variant or an independent fit cell if the expected information value merits another pair. Existing [draft #17](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/pull/17) remains the larger retained-raw Kalman teacher/student handoff; the screen is adjacent local evidence, not a new large direction.

### Seed-4 optimizer sensitivity

The [seed-4 supplement](../../campaign_2026-10-18/online_features_r1/kalman_recurrent_groupfast_slow_r1/RESULTS_SEED4_R1.md) repeats the same complete-component fit and F0 evaluation membership, shared initializer, active-versus-zero treatment, and 210-update budget under a distinct stochastic seed. Active WP is `0.68037958`; zero control is `0.68039346` (Δ `−0.00001387`). Its conditional 141-component 95% interval is `[−0.00017548,+0.00012404]`; post-hoc time-bin deltas are mixed. Together with seed3 (`+0.00005163`), the arithmetic mean paired delta is `+0.00001888`, purely descriptive. The sign flip and small effect relative to roughly `0.0005–0.0006` between-seed endpoint movement argue against claiming a stable feature increment. With two seeds, one exposed fold, one parent, and common fit components, this does not estimate training variance robustly or close the conditional/recurrent family.

The two local feature treatments do not motivate a new large-run handoff on their own. A separate [innovation-conditioned latent gain/update direction](KALMAN_INNOVATION_GAIN_STATE_GRU_20261018_R1.md) is being developed from primary KalmanNet literature and the contrast between the weak fixed-feature effects and harmful output smoothing. That proposal changes the recurrent state-update mechanism; it remains unmeasured, and its B300 result, if any, must stand on its own paired teacher/student matrix.
