# Final stationary-teacher blend grader R4 compatibility review

R4 is a narrow input-schema successor to the predeclared R3 grade. R3's five fixed blends, member order, `float32` arithmetic, official metric, F0 panel, and selected members remain unchanged. The review found that R3 expected the older top-level `jobs` admission shape, while the actual stationary-teacher exporter admission uses `vectors`/`commands` with source, native, split, data and panel identities nested under `frozen_inputs` and `evaluation`.

R4 consumes the actual producer records directly. It binds original native-export admission SHA `5aaa036b…04bcb` and the one-time CPU-admission correction SHA `8b4559ed…8ef30`. The correction preserves the prior PTQ-W4 launch refusal as `not_started` with no worker/output, uses a new unique attempt ID, and keeps the exact exporter argv, input archive, packing mode, manifest and output path. The grader checks that relation without synthesizing or weakening the root admission.

The frozen [R4 recipe](../../campaign_2026-10-13/qat_renewable_r1/final_release_r1/FINAL_BLEND_GRADE_RECIPE_R4.json) changes only compatibility pins, the bounded output location, and the PTQ-W4 attempt binding required by that one-time correction. The independent [R4 grader](../../../scripts/grade_qat_final_blends_r4.py) accepts the old and actual R2 admission formats while checking all input/source bindings before member reads. The R3 schema-rejection [receipt](receipts/QAT_FINAL_SELF_DISTILL_BLEND_GRADE_R3_SCHEMA_REJECTION_R2.json) preserves the pre-result failure diagnosis. The new R4 compatibility [receipt](receipts/QAT_FINAL_SELF_DISTILL_BLEND_GRADE_R4_COMPATIBILITY.json) records an actual-admission check with no score JSON or prediction array read.

R4 is not a quality grade. All three native exports were admitted under the root's frozen CPU-only attempt policy; the full R4 grader must wait until all result/prediction outputs are terminal and then independently verify the ordered 468-group panels, member score receipts, reset replay, prediction hashes, official mask, and five fixed blend scores. F0 ancestry includes the fitted final parent and remains exposed adaptation evidence, not clean CV or a public forecast. Root retains package callback parity and submission selection.
