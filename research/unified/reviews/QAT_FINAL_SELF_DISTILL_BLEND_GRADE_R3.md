# Final stationary-teacher QAT blend grade R3 predeclaration

This note freezes the five fixed four-member mixtures for the next full-F0 blend grade before the stationary-teacher R2 native prediction panels are opened. It does not report a score, choose a ZIP, or authorize external submission.

The comparisons are the original `xteam_h4` baseline; the previous final-adaptation R1 seed-3 W4 QAT blend; and three R2 stationary-teacher replacements using (1) the matched FP32 checkpoint exported to native Q8, (2) same-checkpoint W4 post-training quantization, and (3) learned-W4 QAT. Each mixture keeps common members 0, 1, and 2 at 0.25 and replaces only member 3 at 0.25. The prior R1 seed-4 QAT blend is omitted because the already graded R1 seed-3 blend is the stronger prior QAT reference. No coefficients are tuned.

The predeclaration fixes the ordered blend members, float32 0.25 multiply-and-add order, official `global_wp_clip2_v1` accumulator and mask, post-blend clipping, eight member score checks, and per-group output arrays needed for root-owned ZIP callback parity. Delivery has supplied the planned source artifacts: FP32 `parent.npz` for native-Q8, FP32 `recurrent_w4.npz` for same-checkpoint W4 PTQ, and learned-W4 `recurrent_w4.npz`. Their archive hashes, exact native export admission path/hash, prediction receipts, and independent fit-provenance audit remain pending because root has not yet frozen the post-fit native export admission. Those pins must be filled from that receipt and the corresponding weights before the executable R3 recipe is hashed; after that, the score files are read and graded without changing the five blends.

The machine recipe predeclaration is [FINAL_BLEND_GRADE_PREDECLARATION_R3.json](../../campaign_2026-10-13/qat_renewable_r1/final_release_r1/FINAL_BLEND_GRADE_PREDECLARATION_R3.json). The independent streaming/memmap consumer is [grade_qat_final_blends_r3.py](../../../scripts/grade_qat_final_blends_r3.py).
