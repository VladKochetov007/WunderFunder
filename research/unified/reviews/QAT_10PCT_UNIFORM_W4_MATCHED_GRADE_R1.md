## Limits

This grade conditions on fixed predictions and component resampling. F0 has prior research exposure; the 10% subset is nested under CP5 and clean-T3 fitting ancestry; only one fitting seed was evaluated. The archive stores six legacy raw sums, not the centered moments used by `GlobalAccumulator.result`, so intervals approximate the exact centered-moment bootstrap even though the pooled point scores agree with stored scores to about `1e-15`. Component target/mask statistics do not constitute a row-level mask digest. The result supports only this matched endpoint comparison; it does not establish public transfer, cross-seed robustness, or a general quality claim.
