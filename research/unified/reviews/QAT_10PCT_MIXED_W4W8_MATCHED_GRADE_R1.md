## Limits

Its intervals use the official `wp_from_sums` function on six legacy raw sums; the centered moments used by `GlobalAccumulator.result()` are absent, so these are descriptive raw-sum approximations to an exact centered-moment bootstrap. F0 has prior research exposure, and the 10% adaptation subset inherits prior CP5/teacher/cache exposure. One fitting seed cannot establish training variation or cross-seed robustness. The score supports only this matched deployment comparison; it does not establish public transfer or general QAT quality.
