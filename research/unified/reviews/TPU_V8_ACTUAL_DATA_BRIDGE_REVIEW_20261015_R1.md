# TPU V8 actual-data bridge review R1

This read-only review covers the terminal V8 TPU bridge collected on 2026-10-15. I inspected the collection manifest, small terminal JSON/source/log payloads, the frozen packet and source-bound sampling/update path. Thirty-one small JSON/source/log files match the collection manifest. I did not load or rehash NPZ payloads; root reports that it rehashed the complete 17,504,339-byte collection. No fit was launched or graded by this reviewer.

- [Actual-data update receipt](../../../artifacts/kaggle_tpu_v8_terminal_20261015_r1/cloud_preflight_tpu_v8/realdata_update_receipt.json) binds the H256×2, 211-input/23-output epoch-3 combo parent and F0 split. The worker verified the mounted train/valid Parquet whole-file and footer identities and the 17-file source tree before execution. The Kaggle mount does not expose a dataset version, so these byte hashes are the input identity.
- The update code takes the deterministic seed-3, epoch-0 first B16 batch over source-qualified F0 fit-VALID members. The receipt records 16 unique members, zero fold-evaluation overlap and zero members outside the parent fit set. All eight TPUv5e replicas completed one finite C2000 update; model, EMA and fresh AdamW state/moments reloaded bitwise. The exported updated-EMA prediction is finite FP32 with shape 1×20,000×23 for fit member `(valid, 254)`, after state reset and ten 2,000-row chunks. Future-tail perturbation leaves the first 256 rows unchanged, and omitted state matches explicit zero state.
- The independent synthetic reduction fixture agrees with a concatenated full-batch loss and gradient (relative L2 gradient error `9.95e-8`); its deliberately incorrect local-normalization control differs. That validates the all-replica reduction mechanism on the fixture, not real-batch parity against a separate producer. The full-shape synthetic path separately ran four finite updates. Worker-level HBM telemetry reports a maximum of 417,804,288 bytes on device 0 and 118–120 MB on the others against 16.91 GB/device capacity; it spans synthetic and real-data phases rather than isolating the one real update. The terminal host-headroom check also passed. These are bounded smoke measurements, not a full-fit peak or throughput estimate.

## What this does and does not establish

This is successful actual-data backend, sampler-member, save/reload, causal-state and export plumbing. It is not a completed training recipe: the actual update uses only the first 2,000 rows of one B16, with a fresh optimizer and OneCycle step 0 of 7,460. The sampler input is constructed from the 1,405 fit-VALID members only; it does not exercise the complete 11,940-member `train + valid_fit` fitting stream. The mounted inputs include valid-side SBD teacher caches, while the full training stream still needs its complete source-qualified targets and producer-order contract. Thus the first update is deterministic and inside fit membership, but it does not prove that the production sampler or later scheduled updates are correct.

The probe's declared recipe is the E465 global pooled four-term combo objective with stopped epoch-3 EMA predictions, an equal mean of the two pinned since teachers, SBD alpha `.5`/horizon `8`, valid F0 propensity/true-mask and since weighting, fresh FP32 AdamW, global norm clipping and OneCycle. This is explicit experiment scope, but the local epoch-3 parent itself was produced with B32/C1000/FP16/Muon. Before a longer run, freeze a separate consumer/producer contract and compare the full input, target, weighting, objective, sampler, optimizer, EMA and schedule semantics to the intended E465 producer. The synthetic reduction parity does not establish that end-to-end recipe equivalence on the real batch.

The saved JAX NPZ and one-sequence prediction have not been loaded by the competition C++ inference path. A native consumer check must verify the parameter names/layout and 23-output handling, then compare full-sequence predictions and reset behavior before a TPU-trained artifact can be treated as a deployable endpoint. All V8 fit-VALID members are already exposed; no fresh holdout, quality score, public forecast or candidate ranking follows from this run. The 558.6-second worker includes about 478 seconds hashing raw Parquet files, so it is not a steady training-rate measurement. A nonfatal TPU metric-port warning appears in the worker log; the eight-device job still completed.

The collection manifest's final `interpretation` string says that the update/reload/causality/export checks are pending. That was collection-time text and is superseded by the later terminal receipts and the updated V8 README; preserve the manifest unchanged and use those terminal artifacts for status.

**Verdict: PASS for the bounded actual-data TPU bridge and its stated compatibility checks.** Meaningful multi-update TPU work still requires full fitting membership/targets and sampler coverage, a frozen producer-matched E465 recipe, and native C++ import/prediction compatibility. This receipt supports no model-quality or submission claim.
