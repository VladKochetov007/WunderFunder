# Research value comes from completed contrasts and usable endpoints

This independent review asks which small changes would turn the existing research into better competition decisions. It is tier C source-based judgment, with historical measurements attributed to their original receipts. No fits, imports, tests, profiles, array reads, exports, provider jobs or artifact recovery were performed. The [October 23 stop](../../../experiment_drafts/b300-fast-local-kaggle-20261023/USER_STOP_R1.json) remains binding. Every test below is proposed and unexecuted; its cost is a planning estimate, never new authority.

The strongest opportunity is to change the unit of search from “implemented idea” or “entire matrix” to **the smallest complete comparison that changes the next competition decision**. This means exploiting already completed contrasts, measuring incremental value in a declared endpoint, and charging the remaining path through export and serving. It does not mean proving every mechanism, requiring significance, abandoning expensive teachers, or rewarding completion of an unpromising project merely because it has sunk costs.

## Five findings

### 1. The process can deliver useful comparisons, but several recent investments are stranded between implementation and inference

The [i1 grade](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/i1/runtime/GRADE_R1.json) is an instructive success. Six fits on F0/1001 and F2/1002 tested three arms. History C lost standalone to B by −0.00353711 and −0.00390342, yet fixed 90B/10C improved over B by +0.00035953 and +0.00027145. The conditional component intervals for the mixtures are approximately [−0.0000119,+0.0007089] and [+0.0000822,+0.0004340]. This supports a bounded complementarity follow-up despite standalone inferiority. It does not establish significance, independent fold and seed effects, strong-parent transfer, or public improvement.

The [accounting snapshot](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/CYCLE_ACCOUNTING_POSTBOOT_R1.json) charges 15,296.58 seconds to that completed screen. In contrast, the warm-history stage spent 12,564.23 seconds on engineering, one control fit and its cancelled/accepted exports, with no matched treatment result. Its remaining three fits were [deferred intact](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/WARM_REMAINDER_COST_DEFERRAL_R1.json). Dynamics consumed 550.67 profile seconds, but its four-fit projection exceeded the stage allowance: [12,118.92 versus 10,800 seconds](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/DYNAMICS_PROFILE_RESULT_R2.json).

The online panel then [timed out after 7,800.45 service seconds](../../../experiment_drafts/online-relationships-20261022/PANEL_TERMINAL_R1.json), saving all six F0 heads and F1/lag1/A. One interrupted head lost two unsaved epochs; four heads never started. These records diagnose incomplete delivery, not failed scientific mechanisms.

**Inference:** protecting complete panels is sensible for the original claim, but making every smaller contrast unusable until a larger panel finishes can destroy decision value. A separately frozen exploratory analysis of an already complete cell can inform whether to fund completion. Preserve the original panel as incomplete; never silently weaken its claim or rewrite its grader.

### 2. Full-path cost, resource feasibility and budget accounting answer different questions

The [30-hour plan](../../../../specs/plans/2026-10-21-next-local-kaggle-cycle.md) explicitly includes profiles, exports and failed attempts and protects 12 hours for production. That discipline is valuable. Nevertheless, “zero GPU cost” is not zero laptop cost: the accounting receipt treats CPU-only native recovery as zero GPU charge, while the later online work competes for the same CPU, memory, disk and cooling.

The online worker's CPU quota fell from 190% to 100% after 93–94°C package readings, invalidating earlier throughput assumptions. The [rolling review](../../../experiment_drafts/online-relationships-20261022/ROLLING_COST_REVIEW_R2.json) observed later epoch intervals ranging from 129.299 to 188.073 seconds. Its mean-rate full-path scenarios exceeded both the original limit and the earlier proposed extension. A faster feature kernel did not eliminate parent inference, normalization, fitting, writing and grading.

The age screen demonstrates an even more concrete delivery gap. A [1.14 GB portable bundle](../../../experiment_drafts/b300-fast-local-kaggle-20261023/KAGGLE_PROFILE_TERMINAL_R2.json) reached Kaggle but failed before scientific fitting. The [resource amendment](../../../experiment_drafts/b300-fast-local-kaggle-20261023/RESOURCE_AND_DELIVERY_AMENDMENT_R2.json) charges an unbounded checkout conservatively at 1,080 seconds and raises engineering allowance from 1,200 to 2,400 within the unchanged 7,200-second screen. At the stop, R3 had never launched.

**Inference:** a proposal's cheap head or zero-fit label is insufficient to rank it. Use marginal *delivery* cost on the intended device, plus a separate host-feasibility judgment. The latest quarter-core/1-GiB limit is far below the historical 3–4 GB working sets. These historical rates cannot authorize local execution now. No new scheduler is needed: one existing receipt can state setup, artifact validation, fit, export, grade, transfer and eventual serving costs, including unknowns.

### 3. Some repeated controls add no information; others are exactly the missing scientific comparison

The [mean-only identity receipt](../../../experiment_drafts/online-relationships-20261022/MEAN_ONLY_LAG_IDENTITY_R1.json) reports identical prediction digests for F0 A at lags 1 and 16. Source inspection attributes this to zero slope removing the lagged regressor. Both fits actually ran; their equality is not an independent replication. Independent payload verification and F1 identity remain outstanding.

The second lag was explicitly requested by the user, so expanding the original six-fit design was authorized. The lesson is narrower: future designs can retain both requested lag questions while sharing a proven lag-independent A within each fold/seed. Ten unique fits could represent twelve named endpoints; recovery might need four new B/C fits instead of five missing files. This requires a prospective amendment and exact identity/coverage checks, not an assumption from matching names.

Conversely, an additional independent B model in the i1 experiment would be informative. The current 90B/10C gain has not been compared with 90B/10B′, where B′ is another ordinary model trained with matched resources. That contrast asks whether history buys more competition value than ordinary seed diversity. Do not demand a mechanism proof before using C, but do price its claimed advantage against the simplest equally costly alternative.

### 4. Export scope and serving expansion can dominate the apparent cost of a small improvement

The accepted [warm control export](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/i1_warm_history_extension_r1/F0_CONTROL_EXPORT_ACCEPTANCE_R4.json) covered all 1,873 valid groups, cost 1,936.84 seconds and used roughly 3.7 GiB. The [warm grader](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/i1_warm_history_extension_r1/README.md#conditional-paired-grade) evaluates source-qualified F0/F1 comparisons. A future evaluation-only export retaining every row of each required evaluation sequence could avoid predictions on unused groups. The i1 screen already demonstrates complete-fold export as a viable scientific unit. This would amend artifact scope, not shorten sequences or silently alter fitting.

The [projected checkpoint mean](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/weight_mean_projected_r1/README.md) took only 9.86 seconds to derive, with exact scored-output projection parity, yet export and grading remain pending. It is a zero-fit candidate, not a zero-cost result.

Serving matters as well: a 10% mixture coefficient does not reduce the additional member's execution work to 10%. The [model catalog](../../models/CURRENT_MODEL_CATALOG.md) records TL3 at 39:12 and TL4 at 50:07, with different members; this is evidence that ensemble choices have material costs, not an isolated marginal-runtime benchmark. Native value must be assessed for the whole endpoint. The recovered [CP5/SWA comparison](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/NATIVE_GRADE_R3.json) also found that an H512 replacement worsened TL3 by about 0.00736 on its exposed diagnostic despite standalone promise. Better individual score is an inadequate allocation target.

### 5. Preserved history is strong; current artifact usability and status are weaker

The [25-idea portfolio](../../../campaign_2026-10-17/research_reset_r1/IDEAS_25.md) is explicitly a proposal archive. Its CP30 reference and student-centered gates belong to that date; later fast-screen and teacher-priority amendments must govern new decisions. Reopening a card should retrieve its mechanism and counterevidence, not automatically resurrect its old matrix.

There are concrete navigation lags: the [ideas index](../../ideas/README.md) retains an i1 entry saying execution is live and no quality result exists, alongside a later complete-grade entry. The projected-mean README retains unexecuted command language above its completed derivation. These are historical layers, not evidence of dishonesty, but they increase the chance that a new reviewer repeats work or misunderstands what remains.

Artifact pointers likewise do not establish availability. The [storage map](../../operations/STORAGE_MAP.md) records 463 prediction caches removed in an earlier authorized pruning operation and warns that valid historical receipts may reference absent files. I did not inspect payloads or current remote access; I cannot identify further missing artifacts. A future zero-fit proposal should first distinguish “measured historically,” “manifest present,” and “usable predictions actually available.” Regenerating an unavailable cache is a new cost, not free reuse. Preserve all existing records and files; this review proposes no cleanup.

## Disposition of the older ideas

Retain the i1 lineage, especially [H-RR17-05](../../../campaign_2026-10-17/research_reset_r1/IDEAS_25.md#h-rr17-05--asynchronous-i1-lag-reliability-gate-with-a-no-i1-expert), but change its next question from general lag plausibility to incremental endpoint value over an equal-cost diversity control. Retain age, soft clocks and covariance-state ideas as distinct representations; their mathematical or descriptive appeal does not replace target comparisons.

Modify H-RR17-08/18 dynamics proposals toward one affordable complete objective contrast. Their deferred fits are not negative results, and additional auxiliary prediction accuracy alone would not earn production resources. Modify H-RR17-20 calibration toward the exact intended model's available predictions, with fitting/evaluation separation and no coefficient search on the final grade. It is low-dimensional, but its artifact availability and clipping-dependent value remain questions.

Defer H-RR17-13/24/25 retrieval, fast-memory teachers and world-model rollouts within a laptop-bound cycle unless a credible full-path execution venue and discriminating screen exist. This is an opportunity-cost decision, not rejection of long memory or large teachers. Preserve the large-teacher direction under its separate authority. Avoid implementing another unfamiliar family merely to increase the proposal count while existing low-cost comparisons remain unanswered.

## Seven questions that would change an allocation

1. **Do completed online C heads beat same-lag B heads on F0?** If neither lag shows useful incremental value, completing every missing head becomes less urgent; a positive result makes F1 recovery more valuable.
2. **Does 90B/10C beat 90B/10B′ at equal member cost?** If not, ordinary seed diversity may be the better production investment.
3. **Can the three remaining warm fits, evaluation-only exports and grade fit a safe venue and remaining allocation?** If not, preserve the strong-parent question without treating its spent control as a reason to overspend.
4. **Which intended “reused” predictions are physically usable with exact source membership and provenance?** The answer determines whether a zero-fit candidate is minutes away or requires substantial reconstruction.
5. **Does the already derived projected mean improve its fixed oldZ4/WMSE comparator?** A cheap negative would close this specific averaging attempt; a positive would justify a matched robustness extension.
6. **Which candidate actually fits the remaining serving time and memory after the baseline is included?** If a full extra model is unaffordable, a small head or student path can have higher expected competition value despite a smaller research gain.
7. **What result would justify spending the protected production reserve?** Require a specific locally supported endpoint and a feasible completion estimate, not a public forecast or accumulated engineering effort.

## Four hypothesis cards

### H-ASTRA23-09-A — Recover the continual-versus-warmup relationship contrast

**Family/change:** causal adaptive readouts; analyze the completed F0 contrasts under a new exploratory specification. **Assumptions/mechanism hypothesis:** continual input-only slope/intercept updates preserve useful changing relationships beyond the frozen warmup state. Residual-head training can exploit this information; in-sample parent residuals may instead overfit.

**Predicted observable:** C−B is positive in the unchanged official score at both fixed lags, or one predeclared lag shows a sufficiently useful, interpretable contribution to justify finishing its missing robustness evidence. Report both lags without selecting a hidden winner.

**Cheapest test/matched controls:** zero new fits. Independently validate the six existing F0 heads and parent; freeze C−B for lag1 and lag16 before target access, with A and parent comparisons as diagnostics. Preserve the original twelve-head panel and grader as incomplete.

**Falsifier:** C fails to add useful endpoint value beyond B at both lags; this narrows this readout recipe, not all online learning. **Future full-path cost:** propose 600–1,200 CPU seconds for validated streaming grade/collection, guided by the historical 600-second whole-panel grade reservation; no throughput at the current cap is established. Conditional recovery needs four new B/C heads, zero GRU/teacher fits, only if F1 A lag equivalence is verified. Twelve epochs at historical 129–188 seconds imply roughly 1,550–2,260 fit seconds, plus validation, normalization, exports and grade; plan roughly 2,600–3,600 seconds before remote transport or cache reconstruction. Native adaptation and production remain separately unmeasured.

**Reuse/ancestry/exposure/serving:** both parent caches, seven saved heads, exact source pins; F0/F1 exposed, readout seeds do not create new parent replications. Serving needs a causal native state/head implementation before release. **Status:** `not_tested`. Related: H-RR17-05/24 and [online relationship amendment](../../ideas/ONLINE_RELATIONSHIPS_20261022_R1.md).

### H-ASTRA23-09-B — History must earn its cost against ordinary model diversity

**Family/change:** ensemble complementarity; replace the absent diversity comparator with matched B′/C′ fits. **Assumptions/mechanism hypothesis:** history induces useful errors beyond what another ordinary initialization supplies.

**Predicted observable:** fixed 90B/10C′ improves more than fixed 90B/10B′ in both exposed cells. **Cheapest matched test:** retain original B anchors; train B′ and C′ with the same new seed within F0 and within F2, using the same small-screen recipe. Two fits per arm, four total, zero teachers; no blend-weight search.

**Falsifier:** C′ has no useful advantage over equally costly B′, especially if ordinary diversity consistently wins. Do not demand that C′ win standalone. **Future full-path cost:** approximately 3–4.5 worker hours for four fits, exports and grade under historical safe throughput, derived from the six-fit 4.25-hour packet; new venue/resource conditions require measurement. Strong-parent follow-up, final training and native comparison are additional, unpriced work. A successful two-member endpoint costs a full additional member; distillation would add explicit student fits rather than erase that bill.

**Reuse/ancestry/exposure/serving:** original B predictions, fitting subsets, source-qualified splits and fixed metric. New seeds assess training sensitivity; they do not unexpose folds. Related old idea H-RR17-05; retain the actual i1 result as the parent evidence. **Status:** `not_tested`.

### H-ASTRA23-09-C — Complete evaluation sequences can be the export unit

**Family/change:** scientific delivery fidelity; export only a comparison's required evaluation groups while retaining every row and reset. **Assumptions/mechanism hypothesis:** unused valid groups account for substantial inference cost, and sequence independence makes their omission irrelevant to retained predictions.

**Predicted observable:** evaluation-only predictions match the corresponding rows of the saved full-valid export under the declared numerical tolerance, while wall time and artifact size fall. **Cheapest matched test:** zero fits; one bounded export from the saved warm F0 control versus its accepted full-valid reference. Membership/order, prefix state and resets remain explicit.

**Falsifier:** prediction mismatch, hidden cross-sequence coupling, or little wall-time reduction because setup dominates. **Future full-path cost:** optimistic row scaling gives about 484 seconds from 1,936.84 × 468/1,873, not a forecast. Reserve roughly 900–1,500 seconds for export, comparison and collection on a capable venue; transfer/setup may dominate. Even 500-second exports plus three historical 4,744-second fits leave little room inside the 16,235.77-second warm remainder, so this cannot establish affordability by arithmetic alone.

**Reuse/ancestry/exposure/serving:** saved control/checkpoint and exact full-valid predictions; no quality claim, new fitting or fresh holdout. Serving behavior is unchanged. Related H-RR17-01/05 and the [warm packet](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/i1_warm_history_extension_r1/README.md). **Status:** `not_tested`.

### H-ASTRA23-09-D — Resolve one saved trajectory mean before inventing another

**Family/change:** fixed checkpoint averaging; finish the already derived serving-projected mean's declared comparison. **Assumptions/mechanism hypothesis:** averaging related trajectory points can improve the fixed member's error contribution without another inference branch. Projection parity proves representational compatibility, not averaging quality.

**Predicted observable:** the fixed oldZ4/mean endpoint beats oldZ4/WMSE CP1, with both target scores and standalone mean reported. **Cheapest matched test:** zero new fits; one export and the already specified fixed score screen. No fresh mean coefficients or checkpoint selection.

**Falsifier:** no useful complete-endpoint benefit over the frozen comparator. **Future full-path cost:** roughly 1,000–3,000 seconds for one export, validated grade and collection using historical exports as loose anchors; actual device/availability can invalidate this range. Exact native endpoint replay, transfer and any production training are additional unmeasured costs. The inference architecture adds no extra ensemble member.

**Reuse/ancestry/exposure/serving:** saved projected mean and three source checkpoints, normalization/provenance receipts and existing comparator predictions if still available. Exposed F0 only; historical parent trainer-source metadata is missing and must remain disclosed. Related H-RR17-01 and the [projected-mean amendment](../../../campaign_2026-10-21/next_cycle_r1/weight_mean_projected_r1/README.md). **Status:** `not_tested`.

## Counterarguments and best next decision

Recovery can become a sunk-cost trap. The i1 mixture gain is small and exposed; an entirely new representation could have higher upside than another marginal comparison. A cheap F0 grade also increases adaptive exposure and could tempt premature rejection of a mechanism that transfers differently to F1. Shared deterministic controls reduce cost but not uncertainty; they correlate comparisons. Evaluation-only exports may require more engineering than they save for a single remaining packet.

These are serious objections. Rank the tests by their *remaining* cost and what each possible answer changes, not by money already spent or certainty of producing a report. Preserve a bounded exploration allocation after resume; do not turn recovery into a universal prerequisite for new ideas. The 12-hour production reserve was correctly protected, but its existence does not prove that any current production route is feasible.

**Best next test, proposed and unexecuted:** H-ASTRA23-09-A's zero-fit F0 continual-versus-warmup grade. It directly tests a still unanswered scientific contrast using completed outputs. Freeze both lag comparisons and limits before opening targets; record it as exploratory and leave the original full panel incomplete. A convincing incremental signal raises the value of four missing unique F1 fits; an unhelpful signal lowers that spending priority without refuting the family. Artifact validation failure instead redirects effort to recovery and supplies no scientific conclusion.

This recommendation is conditional on an explicit resume and a safe feasible venue. Original TL3 public 0.66832 remains the historical reported best in the stop receipt. No reviewed result supplies a calibrated public forecast or a new submission. No external literature was needed for these process claims: the primary evidence is the project's actual allocations, terminal receipts, grades and artifact records. The review did not verify live payload availability, benchmark any proposed shortcut, or inspect sibling audit reports.
