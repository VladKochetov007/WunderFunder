# All 70 decision-changing questions

The questions below are preserved from the independent reports and grouped by
reviewer. Repetition is visible deliberately. They have not been converted into
70 mandatory tasks. Root's [synthesis](SYNTHESIS.md) selects the decisions that
matter first; [reconciliation](RECONCILIATION.md) corrects stale or inadequate
premises, including the already completed projected-mean grade.

## [01 — Seven decision-changing questions](01_evidence_and_selection.md)

1. Does fixed 90B/10C beat fixed 90B/10B-prime at the same additional-fit and serving cost? If not, allocate to ordinary ensembling or compression instead of history engineering.
2. Does history still contribute when its introduction preserves the initial function, and does that contribution survive a strong parent? The stopped warm panel does not answer this.
3. Which purported clean reference has complete source-qualified fitting, teacher, initializer, and propensity ancestry? If none matches TL3, distinguish the deployable incumbent from the research comparator instead of using one scalar for both.
4. Would the i1 mixture rank change after removing one connected component, or under modest predefined component reweighting? Concentration would lower the value of another small aggregate gain.
5. What do all six saved F0 online heads say relative to their fixed parent, mean-only control, and frozen-after-warmup control? A null result there could save completion costs; a useful contribution could justify a bounded second cell.
6. Are tiny gains from short cold training durable, or do they disappear as the control converges? The meaningful object is a treatment advantage along a matched learning budget, not the best selected checkpoint.
7. Can the strong i1 dependence be regularized into one standard-input model rather than purchasing a second temporal model? This changes both the modeling direction and the serving burden.

## [02 — Seven questions whose answers would change allocation](02_representation_and_hidden_structure.md)

1. **Where does i1's value survive?** Does a current-row nonlinear i1 representation improve the matched frozen endpoint without extra temporal features? A positive answer raises encoding/interactions above longer memory.
2. **Is field age useful conditional on row position and aggregate activity?** If not, a small nuisance-state summary may replace 112 clocks and their serving cost.
3. **Are long ages mostly recurrent atoms or ordinary quantized excursions?** Different behavior at known atoms versus other levels would favor a mixed categorical/numerical representation over generic decay.
4. **How much official weight lies before the first observed change of a field?** The fitting fixture's 0.368% unweighted field-row figure is insufficient. A negligible weighted footprint lowers censor-aware modeling priority; concentration in early failures raises it.
5. **Does same-index i0/i1 pairing beat a fixed within-family derangement inside a restricted shared encoder?** Failure would weaken the current 52-pair prior while preserving i1's overall value.
6. **Does a row-lag effect remain stable when sequences have very different event rates?** If not, a change-count clock may be more transferable; if yes, imposing event-time invariance may erase useful duration information.
7. **Can an apparent feature gain survive an initial-function-preserving comparison and the complete endpoint?** If only altered initialization or a weak cold model benefits, prioritize ordinary ensemble diversity or capacity rather than attributing the gain to hidden structure.

## [03 — Seven questions that would change allocation](03_memory_and_time.md)

1. How much useful prediction depends on recurrent context older than 99, 512 or 2,000 rows, after the existing causal feature clocks are held identical?
2. Is the persistent state-decay direction target-relevant across components, or mostly harmless initialization sensitivity? Which layers and positions account for the difference?
3. Does a small correction conditioned on current parent h improve the endpoint before any extra history is supplied? Does history still add value conditional on h?
4. Does history adaptation lose quality by changing the original recurrence, despite passing initial parity? Would freezing it retain the complementary signal?
5. Does error track row distance, event count or the disagreement between them? Is a per-field clock transferable across activity regimes, or a proxy for familiar sequence identity?
6. Do matched detach horizons change late target quality at fixed optimizer cadence, or is the apparent benefit explained by state refresh, loss normalization or more updates?
7. For online relationships and future-input auxiliaries, do improvements survive controls for estimator maturity and predictable input nuisance, and reach the fixed target endpoint?

## [04 — Questions whose answers would change an allocation](04_objectives_and_metric.md)

1. For the exact strong-parent recipe, how different are distributions of **scored metric mass, actual training mass, and normalized main-gradient mass** across targets, position bins, and dependency components?
2. Are teacher-mixture residual variance and clamp occupancy primarily associated with label magnitude, teacher disagreement, target type, or recurrent approximation error? Existing aggregate head norms cannot answer this.
3. Does NLL change recurrent **AdamW update direction and size**, after separating weight decay, or mainly change a preclip diagnostic that the optimizer largely absorbs?
4. Does the i1 mixture correction retain useful covariance after a low-complexity, component-held-out nonlinear calibration of B, in each cell?
5. Do moment/history gains live mostly in within-sequence dynamics or between-sequence location/scale, and is that decomposition stable under predeclared component-composition sensitivities?
6. Which proposed objective/normalizer switches actually reach the teacher-cache branch, and which are currently bypassed? Is every future recipe bound to the historical implementation it claims to extend?
7. Does a raw-target or robust-loss proposal seek better official mean prediction, useful representation regularization, or ensemble diversity? Which fixed endpoint would make that proposed value observable?

## [05 — Seven questions whose answers change the plan](05_teacher_scaling_and_capacity.md)

1. Which fixed fitting-probe and evaluation curves show that a strong recurrent teacher has exhausted useful optimization, rather than merely reached its allocated endpoint?
2. Does detach2000 improve over detach500 when update frequency, loss denominators and forward memory are identical?
3. Is the first supervised Transformer learning the two targets at all before auxiliary tasks, numerical embeddings and Muon are added?
4. At a common cost, does a larger model beat a longer-trained compact one after comparable cooldown?
5. Does additional fitting-component diversity help more than repeated presentations, with the entire initializer/teacher ancestry restricted correctly?
6. Does useful long-history information survive compression into completed-block summaries, and which rows lose when it does not?
7. What measured incremental teacher quality and complete-fit cost would justify one 0.68B or 1.65B allocation, even if a smaller exploratory rung disappoints?

## [06 — Questions whose answers would change allocation](06_ensembles_and_distillation.md)

1. Does a fixed 90/10 history mixture beat a fixed 90/10 mixture with an ordinary second B seed at equal fitting cost? If not, simplify the endpoint before adding a history serving path.
2. Which proposed complement remains useful after accounting for the fixed incumbent, separately for both targets and broad component strata? This determines whether to improve, compress or omit that component.
3. Does the current KD target improve on a frozen initializer mainly by retaining its behavior, or does a genuinely different teacher add a reproducible correction? The answer selects anchoring versus new-teacher work.
4. Do fit-side and excluded-component residuals differ in useful correction direction, not only RMSE or variance? A positive answer changes residual-head training membership before more feature screens.
5. Can a proposed clean endpoint's entire teacher/initializer/propensity ancestry exclude its evaluation components? If unavailable, should that study remain a fitted diagnostic or fund new parents explicitly?
6. Does a cached-parent gain survive replacement by the exact native parent and callback implementation at a fixed coefficient? If not, fix that interface before another scientific feature arm.
7. How much of a teacher's useful correction is retained by a matched student, and at what complete fitting/serving cost compared with keeping the teacher or a second ordinary model? A lower teacher score is not an automatic veto; a higher teacher score is not a transfer result.

## [07 — Seven questions whose answers change a decision](07_causal_identification_and_controls.md)

1. Does the repeated i1 mixture benefit exceed the contribution of a comparably costly second standard-input member? If not, diversity may be the cheaper route.
2. Does warm history improve after initial-function and normalization matching? If not, defer this warm recipe while preserving the cold complementary endpoint evidence.
3. What fraction of any online gain survives equal estimator age and weighting mass? This determines adaptation versus initialization work.
4. Does age identity add value beyond its within-group distribution? This determines whether 112 clocks need individual model connections.
5. Do residual corrections fail mainly where in-sample parent residuals differ from parent-held-out residuals? This decides whether head-training membership deserves a bounded change.
6. Does a future auxiliary beat an analogous past auxiliary, and does either improve the actual endpoint? This distinguishes anticipation investment from representation supervision.
7. Which results survive within-cell comparisons when F0/F1 parents, teachers and fit seeds differ? This decides whether to generalize a recipe or retain a lineage-specific option.

## [08 — Seven decision-changing questions](08_transfer_and_information_limits.md)

1. Does the completed history mixture survive the existing component-frequency stresses, and does its gain remain after separating within-domain covariance from between-domain offsets?
2. Which independently documented target properties establish maturity or horizon? Without them, which online proposals remain valid using observable-input outcomes only?
3. Are propensity errors concentrated by feature support, sequence position, or source, and do lower weighting powers change official endpoint ranking?
4. Do apparent i1 distribution differences persist under comparable component composition, rather than equal label-class sampling?
5. Are exact feature atoms stable across components, or does useful occupancy survive when a fitting dictionary reports unknown values?
6. How much scored magnitude weight occurs before a field's first observed change, and can an existing recurrent state already distinguish those intervals?
7. Which local/public disagreement has a completely matched package, inference path, mask protocol, and known ancestry, sufficient to isolate transfer rather than implementation or selection?

## [09 — Seven questions that would change an allocation](09_research_value_and_delivery.md)

1. **Do completed online C heads beat same-lag B heads on F0?** If neither lag shows useful incremental value, completing every missing head becomes less urgent; a positive result makes F1 recovery more valuable.
2. **Does 90B/10C beat 90B/10B′ at equal member cost?** If not, ordinary seed diversity may be the better production investment.
3. **Can the three remaining warm fits, evaluation-only exports and grade fit a safe venue and remaining allocation?** If not, preserve the strong-parent question without treating its spent control as a reason to overspend.
4. **Which intended “reused” predictions are physically usable with exact source membership and provenance?** The answer determines whether a zero-fit candidate is minutes away or requires substantial reconstruction.
5. **Does the already derived projected mean improve its fixed oldZ4/WMSE comparator?** A cheap negative would close this specific averaging attempt; a positive would justify a matched robustness extension.
6. **Which candidate actually fits the remaining serving time and memory after the baseline is included?** If a full extra model is unaffordable, a small head or student path can have higher expected competition value despite a smaller research gain.
7. **What result would justify spending the protected production reserve?** Require a specific locally supported endpoint and a feasible completion estimate, not a public forecast or accumulated engineering effort.

## [10 — Seven questions whose answers change a decision](10_unrestricted_red_team.md)

1. Does a fixed causal lead correction improve a complete endpoint beyond a gain-only change? A yes directs effort toward response dynamics; a no removes this cheap explanation.
2. Is the history mixture's useful correction slow, fast, or inseparable? That decides whether the next representation should preserve levels, transitions, or both.
3. Does another ordinary seed buy at least as much fixed-mixture gain per full training-and-serving cost? A yes can change allocation without disproving history.
4. Do warm history gains survive the already planned function-preserving initialization? A failure weakens information-retention explanations while leaving cold optimization diversity open.
5. Which target and score-weight mass actually account for a proposed improvement? Broad average accuracy can hide damage to the heavily weighted observations that determine WP.
6. Does a new head beat the untouched parent, or only a newly trained control that has forgotten it? E421 makes this a concrete risk, not a hypothetical warning.
7. What would distinguish a genuinely different distributional experiment from H-111/E421/E537-F? Without that answer, another literature citation should not reopen spending.
