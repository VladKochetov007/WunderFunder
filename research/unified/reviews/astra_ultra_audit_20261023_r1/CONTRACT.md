The user requests ten independent **gpt-6-astra / ultra** subagents to read the
current research state and ideas, identify ways to add value, ask better
questions, seek nearby directions, and expose missing or misleading assumptions.
Their follow-up is: “We maybe losing something or some ideas are misleading.”
This is an adversarial scientific review and proposal task, not permission to
resume experiments. Objective mode is **competition**: improve the expected
unseen performance of a valid deterministic Challenge 2 endpoint. Novelty and
agent agreement are not success metrics.

## Independence and deliverables

Each role receives this neutral contract, its own lens and primary-path
navigation, with `fork_turns=none`. It must not read any sibling report,
assignment, progress summary or synthesis, nor discuss conclusions with a
sibling. It saves only its assigned report, then gives root its path and a
short completion summary. Root waits for all ten before comparative synthesis.
Reports are retained even when they disagree. Do not save private reasoning
transcripts; save conclusions, evidence, uncertainty and discriminating tests.

Every report follows the unified note front matter and includes:

1. Three to five strongest findings, with measured facts separated from
   inferences and untested proposals; cite original repository evidence.
2. A critical assessment of current ideas: retain, modify, defer or deprioritize
   with scope. An incomplete run is not a negative mechanism result.
3. Five to eight questions whose answers would change a decision.
4. Three to five hypothesis cards: ID, family, change, assumptions, mechanism
   hypothesis, predicted observable, cheapest test, matched controls,
   falsifier, estimated future cost, reused artifacts, ancestry/exposure,
   serving implications, status `not_tested`, and links to related old ideas.
5. At least one serious counterargument to the report's own recommendation.
6. Primary literature claim–source pairs if useful, identifying which part
   was actually inspected and the transfer limitation. Use web browsing for
   uncertain/niche claims; do not invent citations or run downloaded code.
7. One best next decision/test, explicitly **proposed and unexecuted**.

Desired depth is approximately 2,000–3,500 words per report, adjusted to useful
evidence. Root delivers ten separate reports, a large comprehensive synthesis,
a source/claim map, a run manifest with requested model/effort and actual
completion state, source/report hashes, and descriptive unified index links.
