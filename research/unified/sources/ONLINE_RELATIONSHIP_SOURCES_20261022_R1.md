# Sources for the online relationship amendment

These sources motivate components of [H-ONLINE-RELATIONSHIPS](../ideas/ONLINE_RELATIONSHIPS_20261022_R1.md), [per-field age](../ideas/PER_FIELD_AGE_20261022_R1.md), and [burstiness](../ideas/BURSTINESS_20261022_R1.md). They establish no Challenge2 gain.

- Sun et al., [Test-Time Training with Self-Supervision for Generalization under Distribution Shifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.13231), ICML2020. The inspected primary abstract describes self-supervised updates using unlabeled test samples and an online extension, evaluated on image classification. The local proposal instead fits a fixed task readout over an adapting scalar input model; it is not a replication of that method.
- Che et al., [Recurrent Neural Networks for Multivariate Time Series with Missing Values](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01865),2016. The primary abstract describes masks and time intervals in GRU-D for informative missingness. Timing motivates the separate age representation; unchanged fields here are not known missing observations.
- Barndorff-Nielsen and Shephard, [Power and Bipower Variation with Stochastic Volatility and Jumps](https://shephard.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/power-and-bipower-variation-stochastic-volatility-and-jumps),2004. The author's publication record and [Oxford archive](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3Af927c62c-1717-4a27-8518-d272b0459d85) support the source identity. The originally supplied Nuffield PDF URL was inaccessible to the browser; no claim of reading that full PDF. Transfer is only the descriptive adjacent-product component, not the asymptotic jump interpretation.
