## Verified external basis and limits

- [AIRA-dojo](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02554v1), abstract/introduction, explicitly separates search policy, candidate operators and proxy evaluation. It reports that changing search alone was limited by the original operators and observes a validation/test selection gap. This motivates varying mechanisms and auditing evaluation; it is not evidence that our current loop or any particular model has plateaued.
- [AutoResearch at Production Scale](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30541), abstract, reports 220+ experiments and recurring infrastructure, memory, search-direction, cost-asymmetry and metric-fixation failures. Its recommendation systems and sequential campaign evidence do not establish transfer gains for our competition.
- [JANUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13194), primary abstract checked October15, proposes forecasting improvement, information gain and exploration utility before execution, then updating experience from measured feedback. We adopt the small decision-card question, not a trained world model or its reported performance claims. Other numerical claims and links in the user's long writeup have not all been independently audited here.
