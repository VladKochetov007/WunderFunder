# Independent review: fixed same-member-count replacement

**Disposition: statically coherent; empirical replacement result not tested.** This was a read-only audit: no fit, Torch import, GPU, network, or launch. The target is the predeclared substitution of the original TL3C `event211` member at coefficient 0.34 with `l14_kd90_s3`, keeping the two tiedlead members and their 0.33 coefficients fixed.

## Findings

1. **The package expresses the stated intervention.** The original TL3C archive has three members at `[0.34, 0.33, 0.33]`. The replacement archive records `l14_kd90_s3` at 0.34 and retains `tl3c_fttall_tlead_s5/s6` at 0.33 each. Its SHA-256 is `34b12c214f054941b3f833a494085acdaa46c84f3ad973dec8db1bce8cb568d6` (12,540,686 bytes, below the 20 MB limit); the frozen source archive is `8f93972655cf3c0858376b7d53daba72745a6f17f81fc6c45a54053fdeb5cac8`. The retained tiedlead weight hashes match the source, as do `solution.py` and all three packaged native libraries. KD90's packaged weight hash is `f8ff85238df8e9c91f15bddae4900f789b29c39e4eeb095a60d5b0aa15480e09`. The one-member diagnostic ZIP retains the exact original fbc weight hash `1863883d4006e50b355ec6fed9d84174f281bce1866fdb87d0606dbdf3196f28`. These checks support byte identity for the stated weights/runtime; they do not establish executed parity. See [build_replacement.py](build_replacement.py), [replacement_receipt.json](replacement_receipt.json), and [receipt.json](receipt.json).

2. **The proposed proxy arithmetic and row alignment are appropriate, with a material numerical limit.** The receiver loads each coefficient and sums three `dynamic_int8` members; the scorer clips the final predictions at ±2 under `global_wp_clip2_v1`. `grade_replacement.py` aligns native arrays to the frozen target row groups before applying the scored mask, checks full 1,873-group coverage, and forms `TL3C − 0.34 × original_fbc + 0.34 × KD90` before scoring. The original fbc input is produced by extracting the exact member and changing only its inventory/coefficient. However, KD90 in this formula is cached Torch output, while the candidate ZIP serves native `dynamic_int8`; float accumulation and native execution can also differ from subtracting separately captured arrays. Thus this is a proxy for the candidate, not an exact identity or candidate score.

3. **The immediate blocker is unfinished original-fbc replay.** At review time `full_valid/predictions.npy` was still being written, with no `full_valid/report.json` or `REPLACEMENT_PROXY_R1.json`. The root controller reported approximately 1,093/1,873 sequences complete. `grade_replacement.py` now binds both native reports to the frozen package hashes and rejects incomplete coverage, wrong data identity, or mismatched KD90 checkpoint/prediction digest. It cannot produce a grade until the original-fbc replay completes and its report validates. The baseline native report is already complete and binds the exact TL3C ZIP; its exposed full-valid score is **0.6923359001** over 3,528,273 selected rows. After the proxy gate, the decision-changing measurement is a full-valid receiver run of the actual `kd90_tiedlead_pair.zip`, with the same data/split and native level, then direct comparison to that baseline. No such replacement run was launched or verified in this audit.

4. **Claims must remain conditional and exposed.** The README correctly labels full valid as exposed development evidence and says original TL3C training-membership equality with the local reproduction is unverified. The candidate contains one KD90 seed, not a seed-averaged treatment; its provenance names teacher `tt29rep_fbc1k_s5` on `train` and records “FP32 model kernels, legacy BF16 Newton-Schulz.” The component bootstrap in the grader measures uncertainty conditional on these fixed predictions and exposed components; it does not provide fresh validation, training-seed robustness, or a public-score forecast. No improvement claim is supported before the exact ZIP run.

## Gate summary

Static ZIP structure, declared coefficients, weight/runtime byte identities, and package size: **supported**. Raw-array alignment and official scoring path: **supported by code inspection**. Original-member native score: **in progress**. Replacement native quality, ABI/state-reset behavior, runtime, and numerical parity: **not tested**. Keep the outcome at `not_tested` until those measurements land.
