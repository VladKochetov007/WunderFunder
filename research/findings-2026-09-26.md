## Data structure
- Targets are two trade PnLs against one common future price F: t0 = F - ask, t1 = bid - F, with a
  fixed -(spread+2) ticks for the losing side. For class 3 F = round(mean mid over the next 4 book
  events) (97.5% exact). The law is exact only for class 3 (E-042, E-074, E-079).
- Sequences are consecutive 20k-row chunks of continuous streams: 3,288 chains (E-095). Train and
  valid are blocks cut from the same streams; old dev halves leaked neighbours.
- Train vs valid sequences separate with AUC 0.99 within every class (E-094): different
  instruments/regimes. Within valid, "train-unlike" sequences are only mildly harder (E-114).
- a6 ~ real time since the last book change, a5 ~ since the last trade, a2/a3 book intensities
  (E-091); a0/a4 encode the per-row time increment and dp0-3 the last trade price relative to the
  quotes (opus-micro). Volume codes are full-rank mixings of the depth (E-090).
- is_scored depends on activity (a3), i1 activity, the a5 clock and book state (E-087); it barely
  depends on the labels (+0.002 AUC, E-103). prop_v1 over-predicts class-3 scoring 1.42x (E-099).

## What moved the score
- Output scale (clip at +-2): baseline x0.25 +0.056 (E-061).
- |y|-weighted MSE over correlation/Huber/CE heads (E-071).
- Two epochs (+0.004), train+valid data, blending with a differently built model (+0.002 LB).
- Mask robustness: a floor on propensity^2 weights (+0.0020) and a next-target-change aux head
  (+0.0014) stack to +0.0030 on full valid, +0.0046 on the stress panel (E-097, E-102, E-111).
- Blend GRU 0.7 + baseline x0.25 0.3 is the stress-optimal structure (E-111, E-113).

## Explanations
Four-term symbolic formulas per class reach 84-92% of the GRU's direction correlation (E-100,
E-101): recent trade-volume flow in every class, book-shape codes in classes 0/1, queue imbalance
in class 3, i1 trades in class 2. Partial-dependence plots show the GRU already tracks every main
effect (E-106). Our GRU variants have residual correlation 0.99+ with each other; the organizer
baseline 0.956 (opus-arch): diversity must come from other views or model families.

## Validation protocol now in use

same-sign-on-every-fold rule, softened-mask lambda, and `--stress` (2/3 least train-like sequences
per class; orders all three known LB comparisons correctly at lambda 0.05, E-110).
