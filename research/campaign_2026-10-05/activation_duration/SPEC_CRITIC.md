The registered question is coherent and bounded: for three paired initializations, does the fixed sine candidate's effect change between early snapshots and later finite training under one fixed H128 Muon/AdamW recipe? It does not estimate asymptotic equivalence, initialization uncertainty from three draws, an unseen-component effect, or public performance. The design correctly treats the 18 saved states as snapshots from six continuous fits, and keeps per-seed paired component uncertainty separate.

The canonical-order clarification is essential. Optimizer moments, step counter, and parameter values must continue across all four epochs; the epoch snapshots are not restarts.
