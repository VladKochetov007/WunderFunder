## Then: transfer mechanisms into the complete GRU256 recipe

4. **Fixed GELU/LeakyReLU readout transfer (highest-value model test).** E488 is a repeated small-panel lead, not a complete-model result. Fit the fixed readout mixture against an unchanged readout on the real GRU256 recipe, with identical teacher/cache lineage, data-order RNG, fitting-only normalization, schedule, parent/head procedure and held-component endpoint. Use two seeds or reserve a confirmation seed before opening the screen.
5. **Original-label reanchor in stopped bootstrap.** E494 is code/spec preparation only. Compare the single preregistered small reanchor with unchanged bootstrap and bootstrap-off; hold teacher caches, seeds, fold, parent/head endpoint fixed. The training target must retain the origin row’s clipped label, weight and mask treatment. This directly tests the proposed champion-specific failure mode.
6. **Event-conditioned belief updates.** E492 establishes raw clock behavior, not model benefit. Test a zero-initialized causal residual with learned event and quiet-row innovation updates against an equal-capacity unrestricted recurrent residual and stale/shuffled-event controls. Include age/activity controls so an apparent event effect cannot be explained by duration. Process every row with correct warmup and sequence reset; do not turn the event clock into output holding.
7. **Cadence and gradient credit.** E495 has independent preparation GO, but zero profiles and fits. Its matched arms compare update/detach rows 1000/1000, 2000/1000, and 2000/2000 with row-matched EMA. Run each resource profile before its fixed screen and confirmations.

## Larger mechanism changes, after the bounded transfer tests

8. **Origin-target privileged teacher (H-098).** This is untested as the registered teacher/student experiment. A training-only teacher may read through `t+8` but must predict `Y_t` with the origin row’s weights and masks. First test held-component teacher value against a matched causal teacher; then test a component-cross-fitted causal student. Never deploy future access or a label-derived survival gate.
9. **Residual memory.** E497 is independently approved only as an isolated prototype. It has no trainer integration, native support, resource profile or predictive result. Integrate a causal state reset/detach contract and event-index binding before profiling; compare the fixed 0.999 additive memory against delta erase/write while preserving the strong GRU parent.
10. **Joint sign-and-amplitude transition model.** This attacks the documented class-3 wrong-sign error. The old frozen coarse-codebook trial is a narrow negative with a weakened regression anchor. A new test needs a parent-preserving continuous residual/control, train-only support and the complete head procedure.
11. **Metric-aligned objective and selector diagnostics.** The review calls for a small predeclared loss comparison and a cheap label-aware selector diagnostic on calibration and metric-weighted residual moments before changing training weights. Keep this distinct from class-mask AUC and do not start a broad weight sweep.
12. **The E431–E445 intervention tranche.** Reviews say its 38 planned fits remain unrun. Either re-cost and execute it as one preregistered block after headroom/resource checks, or record a dated retirement; partial cherry-picked starts would leave the same evidence gap. The original ordering prioritizes activity/event-age and i1 innovations, then branch readout and target-specific clocks. E495 is a separate prepared cadence study, not completion of this tranche.
