## What the critics corrected

- **E413 is a lead, not a win.** Volume-history features exceeded current-volume features by +0.000962 pooled WP, but the paired component interval crosses zero. It is one exposed fold/seed, and the two T4 workers had 13 versus 12 skipped optimizer updates.
- **E418 and E421 tested frozen readouts.** Their negatives apply to the sampled, frozen-trunk heads used there. They do not test end-to-end conditional recurrent learning.
- **E422 tested one scalar matured-gain estimate across one selected gap.** It does not close vector corrections, other maturities, or state-based retrieval.
- **E423 does not isolate an EMA clock.** Its intervention changed the consistency teacher and evaluation weights together. Its −0.000574 delta has an interval that includes a small gain.
- **E424 needed operational corrections.** Its old controller could launch a job with less time remaining than the sealed timeout, did not kill a live worker at the final deadline, and bound one baseline prediction sidecar to `model_ep0.pt` while naming `model.pt` in the scorer inputs. The v2 specs still use the amended 24-GiB cap, although the original preregistration retains the superseded 30-GiB text.

The active E424 controller was already running when its review finished. The source now requires each newly launched job's full sealed timeout plus a three-minute stop margin to fit before 20:00 Kyiv, terminates a live runner attempt when its deadline expires, and binds cached baseline predictions to the exact checkpoint SHA in their sidecars. The scorer now verifies that binding. The existing active controller cannot load edits made after startup, so `checks/e424_hard_stop.sh` is armed through user systemd for **20:00 Kyiv** and will stop E424 units then. `py_compile` and `bash -n` pass. The verified baseline sidecars match `c20261004_combo_s3_final_r1/model_ep3.pt` and `c20261004_combo_head_s3_final/model_ep0.pt` respectively.
