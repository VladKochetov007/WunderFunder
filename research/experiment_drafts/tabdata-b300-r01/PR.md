2. **Why it should work.** A deeper recurrent composition may represent temporal interactions more efficiently than width alone. Predicted observation: deep-minus-wide gains across matched seeds and folds, rather than only one favorable fold. Additional optimization difficulty or unmatched arithmetic cost are competing explanations. This experiment estimates a fixed-recipe capacity contrast; it cannot establish a general depth scaling law.

3. **Compute advantage.** Eight B300s provide 30 independent paired fitting cells per arm. Four measured concurrent actors per GPU share capacity with the separate PR15 experiment.
