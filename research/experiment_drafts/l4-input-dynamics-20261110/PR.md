2. **Why it should work.** The supervised GRU may learn a representation dominated by noisy task labels. Predicting input changes at several future offsets may teach useful dynamics before fitting those labels. The pretext targets are `x[t+h]-x[t]` jointly for h16/64/256, with separate fitting-only population moments per horizon/feature. Future samples enter only the training target; the causal GRU receives current/past inputs. The idea is motivated by predictive representation learning ([CPC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03748)); our objective is regression of input innovations, with no contrastive loss or negatives. A strong alternative is that predictable feature variation is irrelevant to the two scored targets. Falling pretext loss without better task WP supports that alternative for this tested recipe.
