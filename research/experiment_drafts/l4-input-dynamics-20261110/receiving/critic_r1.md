## Blocking checks before any code is admitted to a fit

1. **Exact split and ancestry gate.** Bind data file/source hashes, official train/valid membership, whole-sequence disjointness, and the receiver's split manifest. Assert every scaler/normalizer statistic and every fit, SSL target, initialization, teacher, and selection object is derived from train members only. Reject `seq_ix`/Parquet ordering as chronology. 2. **SSL no-label/no-future-input-path property.** Build a tiny deterministic batch; perturb `y` and label masks while holding inputs fixed and assert every SSL loss/gradient/update is unchanged. Trace the encoder input path to prove future `x[t+h]` appears only as a detached prediction target, never as encoder input or a fed-back token. Verify causal masks at each h in {16,64,256}, h-unit semantics against actual sequence order, and valid-position/end masks (including short sequences and all-masked batches). Fit target standardization on train targets only; exclude validation rows in the cache builder.
3. **Fine-tune lineage/reset.** For each paired SSL parent, compare parent/checkpoint hashes and training membership to the supervised child; assert model weights are the only inherited state and optimizer, scheduler, scaler, counters, and RNG progression are reset exactly as specified. Control/specialist branches must not silently inherit SSL state. Bind the 2E+4E budget and log actual updates and valid SSL target counts.
4. **Metric denominator invariant.** On synthetic prediction/mask fixtures, verify specialists apply the intended selected numerator mask while retaining the exact same two-target denominator and global official scoring mask as controls; a missing/poor unselected component must not improve score by shrinking its denominator. Check target ordering, NaNs, padding, and empty masks.
5. **Recipe/pair integrity and staged accounting.** Verify exact seeds, raw112 feature source, batch32/chunk1000, FP32 AdamW3e-4/WD.01, clipping, warmup=.05 and cosine-v2 floor=.1 at actual optimizer updates; make every per-seed pair differ only by declared treatment. Report 2 shared controls once, both specialist contrasts, and SSL-vs-control per seed. Fail closed on checkpoint/parent/config/source mismatch. Explicitly distinguish 8 initial branches, 2 adaptation stages, 10 training phases, and their update/compute totals.
6. **No post-hoc primary tuning.** Freeze endpoint/checkpoint rule, scoring code/mask, specialist numerator selection, and comparison table before opening full validation scores. Record prior validation exposure and every tried arm/readout. Do not fit blend/calibration/coefficients on validation. Treat estimates as exposed development evidence; report paired seed deltas separately from any fixed-model, sequence-unit uncertainty.

## Highest-risk interpretation gaps to resolve from source/receipts

- “all-fit normalization” must resolve to the train fitting partition, not train+valid; source/data receipt must prove it.
- “future-input SSL” is safe only if targets are future input values from train sequences, detached from the causal encoder path, with masks grounded in sequence timestamps/order. If the samples do not carry real order evidence, h=16/64/256 is not yet a well-defined chronological horizon.
- `8 initial / 10 stages` accounting is easy to misstate: the SSL adapters are additional train phases, and nominal epochs may not imply equal update or compute budgets because horizon/end masking changes usable examples.
- A two-target denominator can mean target averaging versus row-weighted/global scoring; the implementation must match the official scorer exactly. A specialized numerator must not redefine the scored target universe.
- Predecessors H-RR17-17/18 and H393 limit novelty claims. The mechanism should be framed as a scoped recombination/retest unless the code/source comparison demonstrates a material difference.
