# [Experiment] Teacher scale × GRU transfer: explicit raw112 runtime amendment

1. **Status, lineage and owner.** This is a prospective amendment to the published design [PR #28](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/pull/28), associated with `H-TEACHER-TRANSFER-FRONTIER-20261019` and `E-TEACHER-TRANSFER-FRONTIER-20261019`. The original CP30 body is preserved byte-for-byte in [PR_CP30_FROZEN.md](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/blob/experiment/teacher-transfer-frontier/research/experiment_drafts/teacher-transfer-frontier/PR_CP30_FROZEN.md); [recipe.json](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/blob/experiment/teacher-transfer-frontier/research/experiment_drafts/teacher-transfer-frontier/recipe.json) also remains unchanged and describes that historical CP30 design. This prospective panel depends on the implemented but unexecuted shared runtime in [PR #32](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/pull/32), pinned to source commit [`d2e6263af3a0dcb2d35badec8c3fa15da0d91dc6`](https://github.com/VladKochetov007/WunderFunder/commit/d2e6263af3a0dcb2d35badec8c3fa15da0d91dc6). Runtime code is not copied into this packet. The scientific panel is unrun: no receiving-host checks, profiles, fits, grades or measured B300 result are claimed. Root owns the design; the receiving operator must bind the data/splits, claim cells and own execution.

2. **Why it should work.** A stronger causal teacher may improve a compact student, but teacher quality alone does not establish transfer. Compare a matched GRU trained on labels with the same GRU trained using the frozen teacher’s outputs. This bounded screen tests transfer for the T768×24 teacher and GRU256 student in two paired cells; it does not test the wider historical scale frontier.

3. **Compute advantage.** The amended first panel costs six new fits: two T768×24 teachers, two GRU256 label controls and two GRU256 KD students. Teacher fits create reusable same-fold caches; student fits test their downstream value. The teacher is not counted as an independent replication of its students. This allocation is not an eight-GPU-hour reservation or a throughput claim. Further teacher/student scale work remains a separately admitted follow-up.

4. **Frozen comparison.** Use two cells: F0/student seed 1001/teacher seed 101001 and F2/student seed 1002/teacher seed 101002. Each cell fits one teacher, one GRU256 label control and one GRU256 KD student. The teacher is a raw112 causal Transformer, width 768, 24 layers, 12 heads, FF multiplier 4, 512-row per-layer sliding KV context, zero dropout and activation checkpointing. GRU students use the canonical GRU adapter, hidden 256×2, raw112 input and recurrent state that resets at each sequence boundary. All fits use strict FP32 parameters, activations, states, gradients and optimizer; autocast, GradScaler, TF32 and quantization are off. They train three fixed epochs, full 20,000-row sequences in 256-row chunks, TBPTT state detach at chunk boundaries and AdamW with cosine schedule, gradient clip 1.0, weight decay 0.01 and 5% warmup. Teacher LR is 1e-4; student LR is 3e-4. Normalization is fitted on the first 32 fitting sequences only; score/loss mask is `is_scored & row>=99`. The additive objective is `WMSE(clipped labels) + kd_weight*WMSE(clipped teacher outputs) + hint_weight*MSE(hints)`; label and KD terms use absolute clipped-label weights. Teacher has KD/hint weights zero; label student has KD/hint weights zero; KD student has KD weight 1.0 and hint weight zero. No alpha=0.9 mixture or anchored4096 procedure carries forward.

5. **Bounded fitting panel.** The six new fits are:

   | Cell | Teacher | Label control | KD student |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | F0 / s1001 | `teacher_t768_l24_f0_s1001.json` | `gru256_labels_f0_s1001.json` | `gru256_kd_f0_s1001.json` |
   | F2 / s1002 | `teacher_t768_l24_f2_s1002.json` | `gru256_labels_f2_s1002.json` | `gru256_kd_f2_s1002.json` |

   Total: 2 teacher fits + 4 student fits = 6 new fits; no control or cache reuse is claimed for this first panel. These two cells confound fold and fitting seed and are a descriptive screen, not CP30. Hints and streaming are separate four-fit panels that may reuse only these exact hash-bound teacher caches/endpoints after this panel is completed and independently checked. No follow-up launches automatically.

6. **Validation and uncertainty.** Score with the canonical pooled `global_wp_clip2_v1` on exact held-out component membership; report both targets and each cell. Primary transfer contrast is GRU256 KD minus GRU256 labels within each cell; teacher score is reported separately and cannot substitute for student transfer. Two cells do not estimate training variance, support significance, or establish generalization. A descriptive mean student gain of at least 0.001 with both cells nonnegative is only a useful follow-up signal under the shared runtime decision rule, not a claim of reliable effect.

7. **Leakage and coverage.** Bind source-qualified data, split/component manifests, fitting-only normalizers, teacher ancestry, cache membership, config and environment hashes before admission. Keep all rows causal and reset state per sequence; warm-up rows advance state but are excluded from loss/scoring through row 98. Teacher targets are fitting-only and must match the same fold, source membership and checkpoint. No future labels, teacher outputs or hidden hints are student inference inputs. Existing folds are exposed and temporal coverage is unknown.

8. **Decision-changing result.** A descriptive mean student gain of at least 0.001 with both cells nonnegative, alongside acceptable measured FP32 cost and a plausible CPU serving path, is a useful follow-up signal. Smaller or mixed effects may still justify targeted diagnosis of source, target, fold, teacher quality or cost; they are not an automatic rejection. Any promotion requires stronger evidence than this two-cell screen. This panel is not a public-score forecast, and a confidence interval crossing zero alone is not a kill rule.
