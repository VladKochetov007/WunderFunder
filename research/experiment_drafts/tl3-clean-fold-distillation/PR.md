# [Experiment] E-TL3-CLEAN-KD-20261023-R1: paired TL3 student labels versus clean teacher distillation

1. **Status, lineage and owner.** Implemented, unrun; preparation owned by Ivan Vorfolomeev, GPU receiver/operator unassigned. Student/teacher connection experiment H-TL3-CLEAN-KD-20261023 → E-TL3-CLEAN-KD-20261023-R1. Base `research-team` at `b4365e01adef57f4a5c9313faecf26952f0f5287`. Extends the canonical trainer with a causal tiedlead encoder and prepares a bounded receiver, source/data binding, teacher-cache export, independent fit audit, complete-mixture grader and evidence bundles. Zero completed fits, GPU profiles or measured gain. Local tests, inference and training were explicitly forbidden and were not run.

2. **Why it should work.** An ensemble target may reduce idiosyncratic label noise and transfer book/event structure into the smaller plain TL3 student. Predicted observation: KD improves the student's official score and its marginal contribution to the fixed mixture. Alternative: any historical gain came from warm ancestry, auxiliary heads or a different teacher family; or fresh teachers/students underfit. The labels-only arm uses identical caches, fitting pool, loss code, initialization, normalization and sample order, differing only in alpha 0 versus 0.5. [Hinton et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531) motivates ensemble-to-student transfer, not a forecast for this task.

3. **Compute advantage.** Two new tiedlead teachers per fold receive four full epochs; students receive two. Extra B300 compute buys independent teacher fits and their two-seed average without admitting unverifiable production ancestry. The student remains the 211-input H256×2 GELU-head GRU architecture. Teacher GPU cost is explicit and can be amortized over later separately approved transfer experiments. No scaling law, latency, occupancy or public-score improvement is claimed before measurement. A positive student result still needs separate one-CPU inference/size admission before a submission.

4. **Frozen comparison.** F0/1001 and F2/1002 students, cold and paired. Each fold's two teachers are cold, label-only, 263-input tiedlead H256×2. Complete eligible train+valid component pools, fitting-only canonical normalization, deterministic seeded batches, strict FP32, no autocast/TF32/quantization. AdamW LR .001/WD .01, OneCycle, batch32, chunk/update/detach2000, full20000-row sequences, clip1. Per-window canonical WMSE uses clipped-label absolute weights; KD target is .5 clipped labels + .5 clipped mean of two teachers. No auxiliary targets, future-label bootstrap or propensity. Four teacher epochs/fixed ep3; two student epochs/fixed ep1. No fit-time outer evaluation or checkpoint selection. State resets at sequence boundaries; encoder book deltas explicitly carry across chunks. This repairs the archived boundary reset, so historical numerical parity is not claimed. Explicit epoch-state resume preserves source/runtime/optimizer/sampler/RNG contract and original cold-start receipt.

5. **Bounded fitting panel.** **8 new scientific fits:** F0 teachers2001/2002 + labels1001 + KD1001; F2 teachers2003/2004 + labels1002 + KD1002. Four teacher prerequisites, two control students, two KD students, zero reused fits. This dated panel explicitly amends the usual 4–6 initial-fit screen: two teachers on two clean folds need four prerequisite fits because production ancestry is uncertified. Six short engineering profiles are separate and are not scientific replications. No search, coefficient tuning, automatic retries or expansion. No remote allocation or fit is started by this PR.

Intervals condition on fixed fits; two fold/seed cells cannot estimate training-seed variance, prove significance or remove development exposure. No best-seed selection.

7. **Leakage and coverage.** F0/F2 split hashes are frozen in `recipe.json`; the canonical splitter recomputes feature-only connected components and excludes them across sources. No production initializer, teacher/cache, propensity or auxiliary learned feature. Teacher cache exports use fitting features only, exact source-qualified ordering and zero padding outside fitting groups; checkpoint/source/sidecar hashes and FP32 provenance are checked on both creation and reuse. New lead features only gather from the current/past sequence prefix. These folds are exposed development evidence; ancestry exclusion is not a pristine or proven future-regime holdout. Dataset anonymity and residual cross-component dependence limit representativeness claims.

8. **Decision-changing result.** Both complete-mixture cell deltas nonnegative and mean ≥+.0003 justify a separately budgeted confirmation, not a submission claim. Mixed signs or a zero-crossing interval remain uncertain. Negative complete-mixture results constrain this exact alpha/teacher/budget recipe, with standalone and teacher-only results distinguishing weak teachers, student underfitting and ensemble redundancy. Never add a local delta to a leaderboard score. No adaptive teacher/mixture/checkpoint selection after seeing outcomes.
