2. **Why it should work.** The official weighted-Pearson objective uses scored rows, while the control fits on all valid prediction rows. Restricting the head loss to the true fitting-side `is_scored` rows aligns the readout update with the scored population while holding the recurrent representation fixed. Prediction: positive paired weighted-Pearson delta. Strong alternatives are fold/seed variation, parent quality, and row-exposure changes; the matched unweighted head is the direct ablation. The public [MarkBrezina repository](https://github.com/MarkBrezina/WunderConnectome/tree/5d086fc90ea0c11a39391dde702e2a4f0a47a869) reports +0.000802 locally after scored-row head FT; this is author-reported, unverified, and not imported as evidence.
