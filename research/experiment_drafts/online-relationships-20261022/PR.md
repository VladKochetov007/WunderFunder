2. **Why it should work.** A causal state of input-only forecasts, pre-update innovations and slopes may reveal residual structure that the frozen parent misses. Arm A adapts the mean only and fixes slope to zero; B adapts slope/intercept through row 98, then freezes; C continues updating slope/intercept. C−A asks whether relationships add value beyond mean adaptation; C−B asks whether continued adaptation helps after warmup. A lower input-prediction loss alone is not evidence of endpoint value. The strongest alternatives are ordinary calibration, fold/seed variation and a learned residual distribution that does not transfer from in-sample parent predictions.

3. **Compute advantage.** Each candidate is a 64-unit tanh residual readout on top of a reused frozen parent endpoint. This screen adds no GRU or teacher fits and does not train a new backbone. The resource decision is an inclusive 8,311.440-second CPU panel within the 9,000-second online ceiling, including cache/I/O and separate grading reserves; it preserves the 43,200-second production reserve. B300 is deferred. If the small-head endpoint earns further work, the next step is a targeted, source-qualified follow-up or native-serving test; no scale law, public-score gain or production benefit is assumed.

4. **Frozen comparison.** The admitted immutable recipe uses 419 inputs: canonical `standard211` plus matured i0 changes, forecasts, pre-update errors and slopes (52 each). The online state uses raw observations only, updates causally, resets at each sequence, and never reads labels, scoring masks or prediction flags. Separate lag-1 and lag-16 panels are used. The residual is `parent + 0.1 * head`; head output initializes to zero. All arms use strict FP32 with autocast and TF32 off; canonical fitting row weights and WMSE; AdamW at 0.001, weight decay 0.01, gradient clip 1, batches of 8,192 rows, three epochs and fixed epoch-2 checkpoint selection. Fit-only standardization uses a 0.001 standard-deviation floor and clips standardized differences to ±8. Means/covariance start at zero, input variance at one; slope ridge is 0.1 and the state half-life is 512. The exact optimizer, target, parent, sequence and reset contracts are pinned in `EXECUTION_RECIPE_R1.json` and `PANEL_ADMISSION_R1.json`.

6. **Validation and uncertainty.** The unchanged official metric is `global_wp_clip2_v1`, with its fixed scored/prediction mask, ±2 clipping and original clipped-label magnitude weights. The grader evaluates both targets and four fixed position bins on both source-qualified folds. Planned primary contrasts are C−A, C−B and each arm against its matched parent within each fold/lag; lag-16 minus lag-1 per arm/cell is secondary (26 contrasts total). The paired dependency-component bootstrap is conditional on these fixed endpoints and excludes fitting variation and public transfer. Both folds are exposed; the two fold/seed cells confound those axes and cannot estimate training variance or establish significance. Report every paired delta and interval; do not promote on an isolated positive component interval.

7. **Leakage and coverage.** Online updates are past-only and use raw inputs, not targets, masks or request/score flags. Unavailable backward differences are zero placeholders and excluded from fitting standardization until mature; warmup spans rows 16–98, with 82 real lag-1 and 67 real lag-16 regressor pairs. Sequence state resets on boundaries. Exact cache headers, source-qualified membership, parent normalization and coverage are checked before grading. Parent training predictions are in-sample rather than OOF, so the learned residual distribution may differ on held-out components. All folds used here are exposed; this screen does not create a fresh holdout.

8. **Decision-changing result.** A coherent endpoint improvement for C over A and B supports continued relationship adaptation in this readout; improvement only over A is consistent with calibration rather than ongoing adaptation. An input-loss improvement without official endpoint value earns no expansion. The screen is small, so mixed or imprecise results remain uncertain and can justify only a bounded information-seeking follow-up when its expected value exceeds its cost. Negative evidence narrows this exact readout and panel; it does not reject online learning generally. Any release also requires actual native endpoint quality and runtime while preserving `PredictionModel.predict`; neither is established here.
