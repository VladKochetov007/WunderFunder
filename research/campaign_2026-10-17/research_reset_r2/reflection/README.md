# Independent mechanism reflection

This folder contains an independent bottleneck framework written before
reviewing the Round 1 idea cards, followed by a critical synthesis of those
cards, their reviews, the historical negatives, and the selected primary
literature. It is an analysis artifact only: it contains no new model run,
fit, optimizer or calibrator step, or independent data scan.

- [ROUND1_FRAMEWORK.md](ROUND1_FRAMEWORK.md): problem decomposition and
  evidence limits, with a dated addendum preserving the original draft and
  correcting two overstatements about the rare clock exceptions.
- [DEEP_REFLECTION.md](DEEP_REFLECTION.md): mechanism equivalences,
  counter-hypotheses, prior evidence, priority-changing observations, and
  three high-risk variants.
- [SOURCE_MANIFEST.md](SOURCE_MANIFEST.md): primary-paper claims checked,
  source links, and transfer limits.
- [ROUND2_PRIOR_ATTACK.md](ROUND2_PRIOR_ATTACK.md): source-level novelty audit
  against Belief v2, target-defined hazard, Wave38 bootstrap, current temporal
  teacher paths, future-moment transfer, and the existing observable-query
  auxiliaries. It records which variants remain conditional or closed.
- [ROUND3_EDA_DECISIONS.md](ROUND3_EDA_DECISIONS.md): finalized descriptive
  interpretation of the corrected exception census, canonical saved-output
  transforms, exact target/mask alignment receipt, sequence decomposition,
  cross-model prediction/residual/run-fluctuation correlations, age-by-class
  MSE limits, and revised priorities for all 25 cards. Historical
  prediction-cache producer provenance remains unresolved, so this is not a
  current-model causal result. The underlying follow-up statistics are in
  `../prediction_audit/FOLLOWUP_DIAGNOSTICS.json` and the age-variance field
  caveat in `../prediction_audit/AGE_CLASS_VARIANCE_CLARIFICATION.json`.
- [IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md](IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md): read-only review of the
  audit implementation and the later validation/corrected-presentation
  results, with remaining source-lineage limits.

The campaign-level index is maintained by the research-reset owner.
