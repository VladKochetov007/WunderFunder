# Coordinator decisions after four stages

Two independent proposal/source sets preceded cross-review R2. R3 audited data
claims and metric geometry. R4 critiqued the full25 list and then reconciled it
with final cards. The result is [IDEAS_25](../IDEAS_25.md), not a poll or a count
of agent votes. No trained candidate was graded by these reviews.

1. **Accept the event-clock falsification.** Full census E-RR17-EVENT-CENSUS
   refutes H-001's universal form. Preserve the original ledger record and append
   the correction. Update feature documentation and remove the false label-law
   assertion from the real-data feature test; retain streaming equivalence.
   Leave existing event features numerically unchanged.
2. **Do not promote i1 from the bug or the lag table.** The fixed frozen-model
   replay proves a preprocessing failure. Heterogeneous shifted-control phi
   is not predictive gain, causal impact or a legal retrospective-class router.
   Card05 remains conditional, below the baseline/transfer spine in allocation.
3. **Keep real distinctions, merge names into mechanism families.** 02/04/07
   share a transfer endpoint but differ in supervisory information. 03/08/12
   distinguish state write, auxiliary event target and change alarm. 14/15/16
   compare different memory operators; none inherits language-model gains.
   Their first future comparisons should remain separate, not an all-loss stack.
4. **Resolve physical semantics against official documentation.** Bid/ask group
   membership is supplied. Component index is not depth rank, best quote, queue
   position or a guaranteed physical trade event. R2's blanket side-semantics
   rejection was too strong; card22 keeps within-side tying plus identity escape.
5. **Keep labels in supervision, never serving.** Permitted training labels may
   define a group-robust loss in future fits. This does not make retrospective
   class or scoring mask available at test time. Future observable-feature
   targets for SSL may supervise training; current-row inference remains causal.
6. **Accept the weighted-geometry correction, not novelty by algebra.** Clip Y
   before magnitude weighting. The optimal direction is conditional under the
   selected weighted measure; weighted MSE already estimates that direction.
   Linear output rotation adds no function class. Nonlinear sharing/calibration
   are distinct hypotheses and must face old histogram/CE/ridge negatives.
7. **Correct TTT terminology and admission.** Card24 is causal current-sequence
   fast-weight memory, not a privileged future-input teacher. No TTT adaptation,
   calibrator/probe fitting or synthetic optimizer-based TTT demonstration was
   run. Proposed future update/runtime checks require that future fit scope;
   current static/prefix/reset checks must not silently execute learning updates.
8. **Do not turn no-fit gates into universal scientific rejection.** Causality,
   valid input availability, identity/reset and source coverage are hard
   requirements. Weak input-only persistence, JS, signature or lag statistics
   can lower priority but cannot rule out a nonlinear forecast mechanism.
   A conditional CI crossing zero is not a universal mechanism rejection.
9. **Choose information value, not invented improvement estimates.** Use01 as
   endpoint anchor, 02/04 as transfer family, then06 and03. Conditional05/20 have
   bounded diagnostic value; architecture/TTT/world-model reserves retain
   diversity. No local delta is added to the public score.
10. **Separate model research from engineering fixtures.** No competition model
    was fitted. The required `make training-tests` suite includes tiny synthetic
    CPU trainer/resume fixtures (5 generated8-row sequences, hidden4), which
    perform optimizer steps to verify software. Its66passed/2skipped result is
    code validation, not zero optimizer operations across every unit test or a
    new scientific training result. Frozen-checkpoint replay itself had0steps.
