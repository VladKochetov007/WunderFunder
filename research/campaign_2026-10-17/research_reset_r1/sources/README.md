# Primary literature and official contract

The two scouts searched independently before seeing one another's proposals.
These are source-qualified mechanisms, not local gains or full reproductions.
The combined register has32distinct literature works; source claims were checked
at the recorded level, often abstract/primary metadata. No external market data
was imported.

- [Learning proposals R1](LEARNING_ROUND1.md) and
  [learning_sources.json](learning_sources.json): long-sequence state, Mamba-3,
  xLSTM/Griffin, SSL, distillation, truncation and adaptation. Each primary paper
  has a claim and transfer limitation; official code links are references, not
  assertions of local installation or benchmark success.
- [Microstructure proposals R1](MICROSTRUCTURE_ROUND1.md) and
  [microstructure_sources.json](microstructure_sources.json): duration/event
  traces, asynchronous relationships, signatures, conditional geometry,
  covariance regimes and robustness. Anonymous component indices constrain
  physical-market interpretations.
- [Coordinator source/rules checks](ROOT_SOURCES_AND_RULES.md): six additional
  primary works (weighted forecast targets, weighted scoring, scenario-conditioned
  book modeling, FitNets, CPC, DreamerV3), Mamba/xLSTM cross-checks, live contest
  rules and the correction that bid/ask sides are known but depth order is not.
- [arxiv_search_snapshot.sh](arxiv_search_snapshot.sh) and
  [arxiv_weighted_search.txt](arxiv_weighted_search.txt): frozen repository helper
  and bounded weighted-scoring query receipt. Search hits are not automatically
  counted as adopted or fully read papers.

Ideas proceed through [independent critiques](../reviews/README.md) into the
[25-card portfolio](../IDEAS_25.md). [Parent](../README.md).
