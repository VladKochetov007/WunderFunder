Predicted observation if the mechanism is useful: the candidate endpoint should improve paired official WP versus its declared control under the same complete-data exposure, with the intermediate mechanism-specific diagnostic moving in its predicted direction. Strong alternatives include capacity/optimization changes, source/sequence mix, preprocessing, exposure and selection noise. For the lag hypothesis, use predeclared input-only lag and phase-shift controls across sequence halves; never tune on labels or retrospective classes. The falsifier is: Replay is not an i1 result. If lag signs remain heterogeneous, controls fail, or paired endpoint adds no value, retain the no-i1 path.
