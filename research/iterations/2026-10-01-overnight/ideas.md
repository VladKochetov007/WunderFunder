## What the failed experiments actually constrain

The current four-slot ensemble already captures almost all of the historical model zoo: the September29–30 journal records0.70277 for22 head-tuned members versus0.70242 for C. More same-family capacity or simply another teacher is therefore a weak proposal. Causal attention stored instantaneous row projections and improved early convergence without final ensemble value; dual-clock recurrence also consumed the instantaneous projection, not a contextual interval representation. Those negatives substantially weaken generic “add memory”, but leave the exact content written into memory unsettled.

Direction/magnitude headroom is an oracle observation, not an accessible predictor. E-241's sign×positive-magnitude head lost; E-244's sign auxiliary missed its pair gate; E-245's readout did not rescue it. E-234's spread projection harmed all-row scoring despite promising event snapshots. Atom/CE heads and quote-path auxiliaries also failed. A proposal built around target geometry must change the statistical estimation problem, rather than repeat sign/atom supervision or impose the retrospective target law on every row.

Inference resets2k/4k, volume-level offsets/drop, sequence V-REx, validation repetition3×, and honest matured-label feedback all failed their respective comparisons. Training-origin augmentation has only been proposed, not tested, and older warm-up diagnostics show no obvious cold-start deficit. It remains lower-priority than a concrete optimizer mismatch. No past true residual may be used before actual label maturity.

## Recommended next allocation: ENSEMBLE-HEAD01

The best remaining next step is **ensemble-aware head continuation with an affine control**, conditional on the first allocated results and remaining budget. It attacks a real difference between the training objective and the delivered ensemble without adding inference burden. The four existing predictions have correlations≈.989–.995; this is a reason to insist on small, controlled spending, not a reason to assert a large gain. Earlier scale sensitivities were weak or uncertain, so calibration alone is not a demonstrated lever.

### Fixed objects and treatment

Start every arm from the corresponding already-created two-epoch plain h128 checkpoint `on_ctl128s{3,4}_f{0,1}/model_ep1.pt`. Keep the samefold d256H2, c500H2 and other h128H2 predictions fixed as partners. This is a **continuation** study; it must not be compared to a fresh parent fit without disclosing the extra budget. Build three arms for each seed/fold:

- **S:** existing nonlinear head, standalone main objective, all backbone parameters frozen.
- **E:** the same nonlinear head, main objective on `e=.25*(p_candidate+p_fixed1+p_fixed2+p_fixed3)`.
- **A:** original head frozen, learn only per-target scale/intercept `p_candidate=a*p_initial+b`, initialized(1,0), with the same ensemble objective as E.

Twoepochs, same valid-training chains excluding the evaluatedfold, same masks, order, batch16, chunk2000, AdamW lr5e−4/wd.01 and new fixed OneCycle schedule; final epoch1 only. For affine parameters set no weight decay, because decay toward zero on an identityscale is an avoidable confound; report this predefined family-specific treatment. To keep the mechanistic contrast interpretable, run the first continuation study **main-loss only in all three arms**, irrespective of whether aux-off wins the preceding campaign. This is a new preregistered combination, not a posthoc inheritance of an outcome. No auxiliary head output is changed by A; in S/E shared head changes can change unused auxiliary logits without adding an auxiliary objective.

Keep the current **unclipped-prediction** weighted-correlation training surrogate in this first study. Final scoring still averages raw members then clips to±2. Training clipping would be another treatment; do not introduce it opportunistically. For E/A use `4*Lcorr(e,y)` so the derivative's explicitquarter-member coefficient is compensated. This sets a fixed optimizer scale, not a claim of full optimization equivalence to S. Do not retune that factor from outcomes. S uses `Lcorr(p,y)`.

Affines include intercepts although unclipped global correlation is invariant to a global intercept; here the training surrogate is chunked and heldout aggregate clipping can make intercepts operationally relevant. Do not count affine intercept fitting as proof of nonlinear extra information. For denominator near-zero or empty-scoring chunks, use the established loss guards.

### Data and leakage discipline

Current prediction files contain only held-out rows; **they cannot provide training-side partner forecasts**. Either replay all fixed partners under no_grad during training, or create a compact two-output-per-partner cache on the allowed training row-groups. Fourmembers×1873sequences×20000rows×2targets×4bytes is≈1.20GB perfold,≈2.40GB for twofolds, not a large hidden-feature cache. Save row-group mapping and checkpoint hashes. No hidden state or112feature cache is needed.

Partner checkpoints legitimately use training chains; their training predictions are in-sample. This is ordinary supervised fine-tuning with fixed features, but creates a stacker-overfit risk: candidate can learn around partner error that is artificially small on those chains. Do not call those partner forecasts OOF. The pure heldoutfold ensemble comparison is the spending evidence. If the E gain is large only on training, stop rather than allocate cross-fitting every partner overnight. No evaluatedfold data enter optimization, affine fitting, normalization or partner selection.

### Readout, cost and falsifier

Primary ensemble uses both Eseed3 and Eseed4 replacements together; compare to the same ensemble with both S replacements, the original V1-like two-epoch anchor, and both A replacements. Weights remain.25, no seed/slot cherry-picking or blend search. Require E−S pooled≥.0003 and eachfold≥0, E−original≥0 per fold and pooled, and E−A≥0 pooled to justify choosing the nonlinear artifact. If A wins robustly, choose the simpler affine-folded artifact under its own predeclared anchor gate; do not rebrand it a representation gain. Report single-slot deltas as diagnostics, not alternative promotion paths.

Allocation:12continuation fits including4tiny affine fits; eight neural head fits are comparable to the current F/T allocation. First cap implementation+preflight at45minutes and all12fits/inference at90minutes on root-managed resources; stop if the remaining native/package reserve would be consumed. Relative training-forward work is about3–4× one branch if partners are replayed repeatedly, but partner prediction caching amortizes it. These are engineering estimates, not measured runtime. Native inference is exactly the existing cost because only ordinary head weights change. Final training must reproduce the accepted continuation on all valid chains with the corresponding fixed final partners, then verify full export parity.

Cheapest hard falsifiers before real training: check the per-row ensemble-gradient quarter factor against autograd finite differences; prove candidate receives no gradient through partners; assert identity affine initialization reproduces original forecasts; demonstrate partner-cache row-group/sequence alignment and exclusion of held-outfold; compare one optimizerstep with a literal tiny four-model ensemble. Evaluate all predeclared seeds/folds on exposed pure chains using the root guarded grader. A failed gate closes this recipe; it does not establish that all ensemble-aware learning is futile.
