## Independent synthetic numerical result and required domain correction

The independent critic oracle uses a literal nearest-even tie rule and int64 dot accumulation, rather than the actor's scalar implementation as its reference. It passed3,024 unit-scale half-tie/neighbor cases and12,096 signed dot cases across scalar, AVX2 and VNNI. Outputs are preserved in quant3-independent-kernel-result.json.

An inherited domain flaw was then exposed. For a vector with maximum equal to one float32 subnormal unit, max/127 underflows to zero. Scalar integer conversion and VNNI saturation disagree. Root approved rejecting nonpositive/nonfinite computed scales, but a further boundary test shows that restriction alone is insufficient: maximum128 subnormal units yields scale1 unit and quotient128; scalar int8 converts to -128 while VNNI saturates to +127. Maximum190 units gives -66 versus +127. The failure is preserved in quant3-subnormal-boundary-result.jsonl. This does not establish that actual trained model activations enter this domain.

Recommended supported domain is an all-zero vector, retaining scale1, or a nonzero vector with finite normal computed scale >= FLT_MIN. Reject a smaller/nonfinite computed scale consistently for all activation backends and weight rows; do not clamp, rescale, or tune. This leaves every normal-scale quantizer output unchanged while making the declared code-range bound true. Root controls that domain amendment. Repeat zero/normal/lower-bound/rejection tests and final complete-model synthetic checks on the exact rebuilt binaries before freeze.

Final approval remains pending the domain correction, final source/build/ZIP receipts, cross-ISA screens, independently generated-weight complete-model checks, and the actual frozen empirical timing harness/plan. No empirical allocation is granted by this note.
