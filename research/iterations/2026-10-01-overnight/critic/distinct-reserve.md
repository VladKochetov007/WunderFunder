# Distinct reserve: training-only random-origin state exposure

Proposal only; no configuration allocated, fit launched, readout requested or endpoint changed.

The prior history has negative level augmentation/drop, periodic inference resets, several event-memory architectures and full-network fine-tuning. A different hypothesis is that the frozen readout is over-specialized to hidden states produced from the dataset's particular sequence starts. A randomly shifted training origin exposes shorter available context at ordinary rows while preserving raw values and deployment behavior. This is not the rejected policy of resetting the deployed recurrent state periodically.

The cheapest discriminating head study would keep the same parent/trunk, auxiliary coefficients and two-epoch schedule and alter only the starting recurrent history for a fixed subset of training examples. Pair each selected training sequence with one deterministic hash-selected offset in its first10,000rows, recompute raw-derived event features from that offset, reset hidden state, allow99new warm-up rows, and train the remaining suffix. Control must use the identical suffix target rows and optimizer step count but reconstruct the original prefix state before processing the suffix. Thus crop/target selection is shared and only history-origin exposure differs. No target-derived origin, train-time label carry between sequences, changes to inference resets or extra serving features. Exact offsets and allowed train chains are frozen before any score.

A small fixed screen on seed3/fold0 can reject catastrophic degradation cheaply, but promotion should require the paired seed3/4, fold0/1 serving ensemble estimand against corresponding matched suffix controls, then an unchanged full-history V1-like anchor. The hypothesis predicts better robustness when effective history is short or interrupted, not necessarily higher ordinary average score; such slice inspection remains secondary and cannot rescue a failed primary gate. Because its new matched suffix controls consume additional fits and context recomputation costs, allocate only if F/T and AUX02 finish early and root judges the remaining budget sufficient.

Falsifiers: divergence on identicalprefix replay indicates a bug; no difference with copied prefix state means intervention was not applied; gain only from different scored-row population invalidates attribution. A negative result closes this specific frozen-head origin study, not all recurrent-state augmentation. No physical clock or label-maturity claim is needed.
