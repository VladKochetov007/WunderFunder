## Static review and practical limits

No leakage or shape blocker was found in the frozen supplied recipe. Nuisance fitting reads only the four raw columns; the sequence loader supplies the same current/past feature rules during training. Final fold−1 fits may legally use all supplied validation chains. The original trainer's best-model bookkeeping sees a tagged zero placeholder, so its model.pt can be epoch0: **always grade/export model_ep1.pt explicitly**. The campaign spec already requires this; an implicit model.pt fallback would be a failure.

Two robustness omissions are nonblocking under the root's frozen commands: predict_adapter accepts arbitrary output paths rather than enforcing the campaign directory, and it derives the prediction fold from embedded source_fold without separately checking args.valid_fold. The root must enforce the declared output destination and check those fold fields agree in checkpoint provenance. The current trained adapter enforces nuisance/training-fold equality at launch. These omissions do not establish leakage in the provided workflow and do not authorize rewriting a frozen empirical recipe.

Native correctness and serving cost remain untested by this review. Preserve float64 nuisance values and update arithmetic, float32 detector thresholds/raw comparison semantics, invalid/neutral detector memory, and one reset per sequence. Score gains on exposed folds would still require native parity and runtime validation before packaging. Initial forecast equality and a strong nuisance-code predictor alone say nothing about price-score improvement.
