# TRAIN-AUX02 independent proposal, before other actor outputs

Question: does main-only two-epoch head adaptation improve the fixed four-member V1-like serving ensemble relative to otherwise identical auxiliary-supervised adaptation? This is a cheap training intervention, not a claim of generic auxiliary conflict: the last gradient audit found every combined-aux cosine positive and clipping inactive.

Freeze existing h128 parents `ref_f{0,1}` (seed3), `refs4_f{0,1}` (seed4), checkpoint `model_ep3.pt`. Reinitialize both arms from those exact parents, freeze `inp,gru`, retain the 23-output architecture and all constructor settings, batch16, two epochs, lr5e-4, OneCycle pct_start=.05/final_div_factor100, unchanged scored-row correlation surrogate pooled within each batch×chunk (not the exact full-dataset metric), auxiliary constructor weights .3/.3. Candidate multiplies both auxiliary loss terms by zero while keeping their computation and tensor gradients present; control multiplies by one. Fixed endpoint epoch1, never fold-selected model.pt. Preserve masks, random data order, full20k context and optimizer settings.

Build V1-like OOF anchors for distilled256 seed3 `d256_f{0,1}` and chunk500 seed3 `c500_f{0,1}`, parent epoch3, same two-epoch head recipe with their logged dimensions/chunk. Primary is replacing both ordinary h128 slots at fixed .25 weights alongside these unchanged partners. Baseline is the exact reconstructed two-epoch four-member ensemble. Secondary report every seed/slot and folds0/1, no weight/epoch/scale selection. Root owns final scorer and readout allocation.

Cheapest decisive test: four treatment fits plus eight control/anchor fits, no new parent. Hypothesis spending gate: combined serving pooled delta >=+.0003, each fold nonnegative; below gate stop this recipe. Two optimizer seeds represented, conditional whole-chain uncertainty on reused pure-chain OOF data does not provide a fresh holdout or hidden-test guarantee. Expected GPU cost 15–25min plus inference; no added inference state, width or compute. Exact runtime/checkpoint inventory follows separately.

Likely failure: auxiliary tasks regularize shared nonlinear head beneficially; absent auxiliaries may overfit true-mask validation training rows. Pooled corr is scale invariant standalone, so member/ensemble behavior can differ. Parameter count and zero-vs-None gradients must match. Existing head1 and V1 head2 are different schedules and cannot be continued/interchanged.

Distinct reserve TRAIN-ENS02: optimize one named h128 slot against three frozen matched partners with a pooled-correlation loss on the equal-weight ensemble. Include affine-only control; both are separate scientific configurations. In-sample partners during training can invite cancellation overfit; never use held-out targets or partner fits that include held-out fold. This addresses exact ensemble objective mismatch but is more implementation/data expensive, so prioritize AUX02 first. Do not repeat from-scratch wmse+gcorr, full-trunk FT, loss-normalization or broad aux-sign probes: prior history already found them inconsistent/negative in comparable serving blends.

No GPU job, candidate WP readout or original-server mutation is authorized to this actor; root allocates and launches after review. No GitHub/public submission.

Implementation clarification before execution: per-epoch validation evaluation is suppressed and consumes the same single CPU RNG draw as the original sequential DataLoader iterator, preserving paired second-epoch sampler order. Numeric zero in its metadata is explicitly marked as a placeholder, not a score. Only fixed endpoint model_ep1.pt is graded, and model.pt is saved from that final endpoint. New runs refuse to overwrite nonempty directories.
