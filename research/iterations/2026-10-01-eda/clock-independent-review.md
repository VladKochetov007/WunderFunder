# Independent review of CLOCK2 by the training/statistics branch

Reviewed the complete clock diagnostic and frozen plan before real execution. This review did not inspect audit labels, run real-data probes, or reproduce the author's synthetic tests.

The core statistics are coherent. Audit counts retain both transition identity and raw proxy bit, so rounded-key collisions with different proxy behavior do not silently change denominators. Known transitions use the learned zero/nonzero move; absent keys fall back to the original proxy in both fixed and bootstrapped tables. Events outside the union of learned/historical keys contribute to fallback denominators. Signed disagreement and binary mask disagreement remain distinct. Modal signed jump is selected before collapsing to zero/nonzero; chain resampling re-fits this discrete mode and retains the declared original tie order.

The conditional audit bootstrap and joint fit/audit bootstrap answer different questions and use whole chain counts. These intervals still condition on the sampled sets and the chosen partition, and cannot certify physical quote semantics. In particular, stable binary masks are not evidence that non-quote book updates carry no predictive information. The changed write policy remains a future matched intervention.

The strict-interior update count is correctly `cum[t-1]-cum[s]` between events `s<t`, including the gap1 empty-interior case. Gaps and prefix/tail censoring stay within sequence resets. Nonempty histories motivate the `h_t` versus `z_t` representation contrast but do not prove that `z_t` omits a sufficient predictive state. A no-event sequence can contribute both a full prefix and full censored-tail description; these are not disjoint durations to add together.

I identified one concrete denominator issue before execution: equal-chain summaries used zero-filled division for chains without proxy events/closed intervals, contrary to the plan's missing-denominator semantics. The author changed these to positive-denominator-only quantiles with explicit valid/undefined counts, guarded fit-only flip means, and made an undefined primary interval fail the descriptor. The earlier CI broadcasting/neither-known fixes were already present. The author reports the final code hash `a2e3d9931e01700f8c8ae5ef54c6c27f27867ea0d2b01483c4f2fbb3b2b18272` and successful synthetic tests; those tests are author-executed evidence, not my independent reproduction.

No remaining blocking statistical or causal-runtime issue was found in source review. Dense bootstrap storage scales with observed transition/outcome count; the registered resource limit remains the safeguard rather than a promise that arbitrary data fit. The final descriptor is an exploratory resource decision, not a repaired pass of the previous signed-key gate, a forecasting result or training authorization.
