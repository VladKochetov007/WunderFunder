# Warm child: match the head random stream without the warm parent epoch

Status: source-only design; no allocation, implementation, model/data reads or scoring. This is a mechanistic child of ordinary warm, not a new model family or production candidate.

The ordinary-warm experiment changed two things relative to historical H2: one all-layer parent adaptation epoch, and the CPU random stream entering H2 sampling. The latter comes from constructing and discarding `Linear(256,32)` then `GRU(33,64,batch_first=True)` after the ordinary RNN. The loaded head is not reinitialized: all ordinary tensors are overwritten by the parent checkpoint. Consequently these extra constructor draws change sampling randomness, not initial parameter values.

Fit exactly two seed3 heads, folds0/1, named `oct02_ow_rng_s3h2_f0` and `oct02_ow_rng_s3h2_f1`. Initialize strictly from the original `cont_sh256_s3_fF@ep3`, retain W's exact factory including both discarded constructors and parity check inside `fork_rng`, and freeze `inp,gru`; mean/std remain unchanged buffers. Train two epochs and read only `model_ep1.pt`. Use W's 103 resolved H2 arguments with only name/init changed: batch16, chunk2000, lr.0005, AdamW/OneCycle, gcorr minibatch surrogate, aux_next=.3, aux_struct=.3, aux_loss_scale=1, valid-only training outside held chain fold, scored mask, no teacher/EMA, no normalization refit. Keep the skipped-evaluation base-seed draw between epochs. No alternate seed, epoch, shuffle, objective or ensemble weights.

Let H be historical H2 (`cont_sh256_s3h2_fF`), R this random-stream-matched control, and W completed warm+H2 (`oct02_ow_s3h2_fF`). Every scored forecast is the equal-quarter replacement of the shallow slot with unchanged `on_ctl128s3`, `on_ctl128s4`, `on_ctlc500` anchors. Primary mechanistic contrast is W4−R4; explanatory contrast is R4−H4. Reuse H/W frozen sufficient statistics, the exact existing row/mask convention, pooled metric and paired whole-chain bootstrap; retain both-fold deltas. These contrasts partition the existing scalar W4−H4 difference; they are not independent discoveries. No historical or W forecasts need regeneration. Two new full-prefix, target-free OOF passes produce the usual full1873-row arrays, unselected rows NaN, with row-order/checkpoint receipts.

Interpretation is fixed before any new score. W4−R4 positive in both folds supports extra parent adaptation at this seed; an interval wholly above zero gives stronger evidence. R4−H4 positive in both folds with W4−R4 nonpositive in both falsifies attributing this seed's gain to the extra parent epoch. Mixed fold signs or an interval spanning zero leave attribution uncertain; do not rename the better arm or choose a seed. The parent adaptation changes both backbone and starting head, so even positive W4−R4 cannot identify a backbone-only improvement. Seed4 warm replication does not replace this seed3 mechanism check. This diagnostic does not change the production recipe or promotion rules.

W files cannot be reused unchanged with only new commands: `initialize_model` and the checkpoint validator both require H2's own parent job at ep0.
