## Causality and arithmetic

[`online_zscore_torch`](../../../src/wnn/features.py:148) computes, independently for each sequence and raw channel,
`mu_t = cumsum(x)_t/(t+1)`, `v_t = max(cumsum(x*x)_t/(t+1)-mu_t²,0)`, and `(x_t-mu_t)/sqrt(v_t+1e-4)`. The current row is observed and legitimately included. No target, scoring mask, other sequence or future row enters these features. Computing this on the full20k tensor does not make the mathematical prefix sums whole-sequence statistics.

The [frozen trainer](../2026-10-01-overnight/training/train_aux.py:880) calls `build_inputs(x)` on the complete float32 raw batch **before** the chunk loop and outside autocast. The [reader](../../../src/wnn/data.py:103) explicitly supplies float32 features; CudaPrefetcher transfers without changing dtype. Thus the prefix sums, squares, variance subtraction and epsilon operate in float32, while the downstream model forward uses the existing AMP policy. They must not be reinitialized at TBPTT chunks or silently changed to float16/Welford/float64 in this experiment.

At the first row the new feature is zero before the model's fixed normalization. Exact constant prefixes also give zero in real arithmetic. Float32 cumulative rounding and cancellation in `E[x²]-E[x]²` can leave small drift or a negative variance estimate; clamp prevents a negative square root, and epsilon bounds the denominator below by .01. This does **not** bound all z-scores near ±1: a rare change after a long constant prefix can approach `sqrt(t)` when epsilon is negligible. This is existing behavior, not a reason to introduce a new clip/variance estimator now. Native serving will eventually need an explicit numerical parity check for this exact323-input recipe; historical E183 does not automatically certify new weights or a differently ordered accumulator.

[`fit_normalization`](../2026-10-01-overnight/training/train_aux.py:350) additionally estimates fixed model mean/std from two batches of **training** sequences, subsampling every seventh row, with std floor1e-3. These are learned training constants, not test-sequence future normalization. H2 loads them from the parent via strict state_dict loading and does not refit them. The own-fold training-pool exclusion remains the existing trainer's responsibility.
