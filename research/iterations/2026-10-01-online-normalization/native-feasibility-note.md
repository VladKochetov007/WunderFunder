Numerical caution matters more than the structural change. GPU `online_zscore_torch` builds full-sequence float32 cumulative sum and sum-of-squares outside autocast, includes the current row, clamps variance at0 and adds `1e-4`. Native `OnlineNorm` uses sequential float32 sums/squares and the same operation order with function-level `fp-contract=off`; its build uses `-O3` without `-ffast-math`.
