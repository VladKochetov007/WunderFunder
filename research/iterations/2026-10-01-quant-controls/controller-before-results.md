## Prior and ordering are separate interventions

Under the old block shuffle, the transition predictor retained an empirical conditional prior learned on original-order sequences. Its adverse null performance combines changed transition statistics, prior mismatch and the finite-sample cost of learning separate contexts. PRIOR02 will fit the surrogate prior on corresponding surrogate fitting sequences, leaving the original prediction recipe unchanged. The opposite half remains the evaluation half. The primary contrast holds the evaluation surrogate fixed and changes only the fitted prior, so it can attribute that estimator-level difference to the declared prior intervention.

On original-order sequences, an independent transition prior (every row equals the marginal prior) separates some transferred transition information from the same online context adaptation. A static original transition prior separates the value of subsequent adaptation. These are complementary contrasts, not an additive causal decomposition: log loss, shrinkage and the online counts interact. An early or late difference does not identify where a real market mechanism originates. No context length, smoothing value, centers, event cap or sample will be chosen after seeing the new results.

The frequency baseline's complete-sequence probability is exchangeable: its product telescopes to a function of final category counts and fixed Dirichlet parameters. This explains equal full-sequence F loss under mark permutations. It does not make its event-by-event probabilities noncausal. For a raw-stream surrogate, emitted counts may change; F loss need no longer remain equal.

## A raw detector can create predictability

There is a useful exact toy counterexample. Suppose raw vectors take two distinct valid, nonneutral values independently at each row. Emit only on an exact raw change. Consecutive emitted raw values then alternate, even though raw rows were independent. If the two values map to different categories, previous emitted category predicts the next category perfectly after initialization. Neither a learned temporal market mechanism nor an inference leak is required.

More generally, for IID raw values with probabilities p(v), all valid and nonneutral, the next distinct raw value after v has probability p(u)/(1-p(v)) for u≠v. Thus compressing holds changes the observation process. This expression is an exact restricted toy calculation, **not a fitted model of these data**: neutral/invalid bridges, multiple raw vectors mapping to one category, drift and non-IID dynamics invalidate that simple formula here. In particular, distinct raw values can share a category, and an ignored neutral bridge can permit the same emitted raw value to recur. A categorical no-self-transition rule is therefore not a correct implementation of the actual detector.

DETECTOR02 must specify a counterfactual in raw four-vector space and apply the actual detector again. The surrogate must preserve each vector jointly rather than shuffling coordinates independently. What is preserved—run durations, validity/neutrality states, row distributions and coarse localization—must be stated exactly. If reordering creates equal neighbouring vectors, the detector must suppress that boundary naturally, without retrying the permutation to obtain a preferred event count. Event counts, category frequencies and capped row coverage can then change; their changes are outcomes to report, not nuisance discrepancies to conceal.

Matching nuisance priors to each surrogate avoids repeating the old prior mismatch. It does not turn a constrained permutation into a physically validated market simulator or a calibrated significance test. A reduced prediction gain would show dependence on structures disrupted by that particular intervention. A retained gain would show that the preserved representation/observation structure can reproduce part of the effect. Neither statement uniquely identifies market flow or a proportion of the original gain caused by the detector.

Surrogate methods require a stated null and careful interpretation of which properties are preserved; an arbitrary constrained generator cannot be interpreted as rejecting every simpler explanation. This general limitation is discussed in Schreiber and Schmitz, [Surrogate time series](https://arxiv.org/abs/chao-dyn/9909037). The concrete surrogate here is our declared diagnostic, not a reproduction of a method certified by that paper.

## Selection, uncertainty and the stopping rule

The 256 chain IDs, original diagnostic halves, centers and caps are inherited from QMARK-EDA-01. Same-chain reuse improves pairing but supplies no fresh evidence of transfer. Three fixed surrogate seeds are technical repetitions, not three training seeds or independent datasets. Chain intervals will pair conditions within half/class strata while holding nuisance fits fixed; that limitation remains explicit. Both original and surrogate estimands must declare how empty or short sequences enter denominators.

After the two fixed controls, report the complete contrasts and stop this allocation. A favorable result may justify a separately fixed feature ablation against the current raw-input GRU, with appropriate matched controls and ensemble contribution as the outcome. Physical interpretation is optional for competition utility; naming a representation feature honestly is mandatory. A disappointing mechanistic control does not establish that the representation is useless, and an interesting mechanistic result does not establish score improvement.
