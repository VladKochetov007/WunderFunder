## DETECTOR02 specification review

The declared128-raw-run-packet recipe addresses the initial specification concerns without claiming to preserve incompatible properties. It shuffles joint four-vector/length packets, permits adjacent merges, reruns the original detector, freezes the original centers and matches both pi and R on corresponding surrogate fitting chains. It explicitly allows changed emitted counts/marginals, gaps, first code and cap endpoint. The paired-complete-chain primary cohort and each condition's own event denominator are the appropriate declared estimands; undefined observations and exclusions must remain visible.

The preservation claim applies to packet/code-duration multisets, not reconstructed maximal run lengths or valid-pair topology. First-block permutation changes reset state by design. A matched raw-surrogate comparison is therefore between complete observation/prediction recipes with several changed features; it cannot identify a fraction of the original gain caused solely by the detector. The128-raw-run clock differs from PRIOR02's128-mark clock.

## DETECTOR02 completed-result review

The saved result completed in **36.880 seconds**. Every source, metadata, raw-preservation, original-mark/prior/loss reproduction and post-run identity check is true. All **256 chains**, with **32 in each of eight half/class strata**, contribute to the paired primary contrast; no chains or strata are excluded. Independently summing the saved per-chain losses and dividing by each condition's own counts reproduces all reported macro-chain and event-weighted gain points to less than `1e-12`. This review reads the saved result only, without querying source arrays or executing another empirical recipe.

The original macro-chain `F−T` is **+.7897566**; the mean of three raw-run surrogates is **−.0312842**, conditional 95% interval **[−.0337732,−.0285505]**. Their paired difference is **+.8210407 [.8121237,.8303417]**. Event-weighted values are respectively **+.7863654**, **−.0241179 [−.0271136,−.0207767]**, and **+.8104834 [.7987097,.8222976]**. Each surrogate seed, both halves and all four classes have negative whole-prefix surrogate gain. This is consistent across those declared slices, not independent replication on new chains.

The observation units genuinely differ: original retained events number **681,335**, versus **670,684 / 670,649 / 670,665** in the three surrogates, a **1.563%–1.568%** reduction. Counts change for **179 chains** in each draw, and the number capped at 4,096 falls from **80 to 77**. Before the cap, detection yields **826,511** original events versus **804,708 / 804,896 / 804,897**. The comparison is paired by source chain, not by emitted event or equal raw-prefix coverage. Preservation checks pass despite these intended changes; they must not be described as preserving event marginals, detector transitions or reconstructed maximal-run durations.

The cold-start qualification also survives this control: first16 surrogate gains are **+.025290 / +.024682 / +.028483**, while after16 gains are **−.031860 / −.032118 / −.032209**. Those are descriptive fixed-slice points; no new first16 uncertainty calculation was added. Saying that every trace of contextual advantage disappears at every horizon would be incorrect. Mean within-chain same-category rate changes from **.057936** original to about **.1817–.1824** under these surrogates. Consequently the detector does not impose a universal no-self-transition constraint on the encoded marks.

The defensible conclusion is narrow but useful: **this fixed surrogate's preserved joint raw values, attached durations and block occupancy, passed through the same detector/encoder and condition-matched priors, do not reproduce the original whole-prefix transition-estimator advantage**. The original ordered raw path contains structure destroyed by this operation. An ordered encoding/state-machine origin remains compatible with that result; market information, economic mechanisms and forecast utility remain unidentified. The original-minus-surrogate contrast exceeding the original gain reflects the negative surrogate gain, not more than 100% of a causal mechanism removed. Similar point estimates for the PRIOR02 matched-null and DETECTOR02 null do not by themselves establish equivalence between the null mechanisms.

No blocking numerical, denominator or implementation issue was found. The two controls clarify interpretation of existing mark predictability; they do not authorize a new forecasting model or a post-result search for a more favorable null.
