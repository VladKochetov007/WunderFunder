## Two corrections to the E527 brief

**Do not pad the 211-wide input as a cuDNN alignment fix.** In `scripts/train_rnn.py`, H230 constructs the default `RNNModel` with `n_in = 112 raw + 99 event features = 211` (before optional features). In [`src/wnn/nn.py`](../../../../src/wnn/nn.py), `RNNModel.inp` projects `n_in` to `hidden`; the recurrent module is declared as `nn.GRU(hidden, hidden, ...)`. H230's `--hidden 256` therefore gives the GRU an input width of 256, not 211. The E527 suggestion to test 211→216 padding is inapplicable as written. If the trace later shows recurrent-kernel dispatch as the bottleneck, inspect the actual cuDNN algorithm, tensor/weight layout and alignment for this 256-wide GRU, along with installed PyTorch/CUDA/cuDNN and GPU capability. Any layout or kernel variant is a new matched numerical/profile comparison.

**Do not require blanket disjointness between student fitting and teacher training.** Ordinary distillation may use an independently trained permitted teacher on the student's permitted fitting groups. The validity gate is that teacher ancestry is excluded from the scored/evaluated unit used to claim the student comparison, or that a documented cross-fit construction excludes each evaluated unit from the teacher that generated its targets. Bind the exact membership and ancestry to the recipe. The prior E527 selection wording was too broad; its original notes are preserved, and an independent review may refine the H230-specific split interpretation.

## Break-even record (leave numeric cells null until observed)
