## Priority and corrections before implementation

1. First competition experiment: H240 with an independently fitted feature projection/dynamics prior and a matched small readout. Fix the parent and fitting membership. Compare no adaptive features, fixed dynamics, warm-up-only updates, and continuous causal updates. Generate fitting states by the exact causal replay used at inference. The tested RLS reference does not yet include that fitting pipeline.
2. Prepare H241 as a separate higher-upside teacher path. Match no-write and learned-write controls. Scientific exploration may exceed final inference cost, with a compression or native-serving path explicitly measured later.
3. H244 is a cheap distinct dynamic-statistics control; H245 can use existing diverse own experts only where ancestry and row order permit. Do not make all other online arms wait for either control's result.
4. H242 needs task-aligned offline training. A failed untrained adapter or changed normalization plugged into a fixed checkpoint does not test the trained treatment and must not become a universal gate. Preserve the scout's original suggestions, but do not adopt its proposed naive-replay/one-step rejection as a required predecessor to meta-training.
5. H246 and H247 remain independent exploration/interaction proposals. H247 may be diagnosed before fitting; its failure closes the tested trigger, not adaptation itself.

Serving-cost limits apply to a submission route, not to a larger adaptive teacher's research eligibility. A slow Python reference motivates cost measurement and possible compression; it is not proof that the mechanism should be discarded.

Scout accounting correction: four float32 arrays for fast/slow location/scale over 112 features already require 1,792 bytes before counts, priors or scratch space. The report's “under 1 KB” estimate is too small; budget several KB and measure. Source availability of cumulative z-score or prefix adapters does not establish that the selected parent actually enables them: read its exact recipe before naming an active control.
