# All-data heterogeneous teacher-family replication audit

Status: source-grounded method audit and proposed independent implementation. This is not a frozen training contract, admission, or score claim.

## Evidence boundary

The reported endpoint is pinned to `origin/research/creative-iteration-20260930` commit `833aaf541155c591e930bbc6c640b8817e01a536`. The endpoint audit packet binds wave18 `full_teacher_lane.sh` (SHA256 `7ebd4318bd45c781306faf39e8f159638bbd1f29307663a3c714201f685e1c89`) and `prepare_full_teachers.py` (SHA256 `6af31d8a5b7aca818bbf45c4328fd02afe8ad2f1d99c0da075fa3cacfdecee4b`). The former only exports existing checkpoints: `fbc1k_s5@ep3`, `ffull_s3/s4@ep1`, and `frawW_s3ft/s4ft@ep1`. The latter only combines prediction arrays. Neither trains teachers.

The endpoint binds a 0.8125 unnormalized main-source sum: `0.30 strong fbc1k_s5 + 0.10 full_s3 + 0.10 full_s4 + 0.125 raw_s3 + 0.125 raw_s4 + 0.0625 orgbase`. Family targets are the strong source, the mean of the two full sources, the mean of the two raw sources, and the organizer baseline. Main labels and the main source sum are clipped to ±2 before the `.3/.7` mix; family targets are raw. Student details and ancestry gaps are recorded in `H230_MAXIM_RECIPE_AUDIT_V1_20261008.md`.

The endpoint snapshot does not bind teacher checkpoint hashes or complete parent/run receipts. Its all-data prediction lane is not proof of all-data teacher fitting. `ffull` has no training command in the inspected endpoint snapshot. `frawW` has a plausible predecessor training command, but the endpoint snapshot does not establish that this exact command produced the final `frawW_s3ft/s4ft` checkpoints. The `orgbase` checkpoint hash and origin receipt are absent. These remain explicit lineage gaps; no exact-teacher-reproduction claim is supportable from this source set.

## Independent all-data route

The full teacher recipe should be described as method-compatible, not as an exact replay. Fit and predict on the exact supplied target-index groups (10,606 train; 1,873 valid). The 468 exposed valid evaluation groups are included in fitting, so every teacher cache, student run, and metric from this endpoint is final-training/in-sample and has no held-out interpretation.

Use our own trainer/source and independently trained weights only. Preserve the endpoint's source coefficients, `.8125` mass, raw family targets, and `.3/.7` clipped main-target construction where a root-reviewed all-data source contract can bind each source. The own-baseline substitution, own source ancestry, parent choices, and any change to strong-teacher multiplicity must be explicit deltas. Seed-specific student parents must be separate own checkpoints; the previously completed shared seed-3-parent student pair does not satisfy that condition.

The current H230 V3 cache validator is intentionally strict for clean-fold teachers: it requires `teacher_training_scope=clean_fold_fit_only` and rejects direct ancestors outside the clean fit groups. The existing all-row H230 cache therefore remains a clean-fold-teacher cache, not an all-data teacher pool. A distinct V4 source/cache successor must retain V3 unchanged and accept an all-data teacher source only when (1) direct teacher fit groups exactly match the complete train and valid target indices, (2) every ancestor is identified by own checkpoint/source/terminal receipts and its fit rows are a subset of those supplied groups, (3) output sidecars bind each checkpoint, split, row-group order and array digest, and (4) metadata sets `heldout_claim=false` and has no evaluation groups. No scope boolean alone may bypass ancestry validation.

Before any cloud fit, freeze per-stage commands and checkpoint policy, source hashes, teacher membership, target coefficients, own-baseline treatment, cloud input/output identities, and bounded per-job runtime/resource budgets. Exact final-teacher ancestry not established by the pinned source must remain listed as a deviation rather than filled with guesses. The existing clean-fold cache and the V3 validator must not be modified or relabeled.
