## Concrete source and validity checks

- The source's loss section (ROGII lines 6–23) is a distribution over alignment hypotheses, with expected-path Huber and a probability-weighted GR-gap term. It is not evidence for clipping every Connectome training target to the official prediction interval, anchoring target values to an input channel, or penalizing prediction magnitude.
- The source's feature sections (lines 34–75) use explicit typewell/horizontal views and TVT coordinates. Causal per-channel calibration or sequence-index bins are reasonable new analogies to test, but are not direct reproductions.
- The source's particle filter section (lines 76–88) motivates rare jumps, coarse updates, smoothing, and diversity. For this project, all online state updates must use rows through t only and reset at sequence boundaries. Any model-derived training features need fold-excluded ancestry.
- The source's XY section (lines 89–122) depends on real XY and TVT/Z geometry and explicitly recounts a local-CV/public-metric disagreement. Do not synthesize XY from anonymous channels or treat the author's transfer diagnosis as a universal selection rule.
- The source's augmentation section (lines 123–156) has a domain-specific invariant and matched feature regeneration for its path-changing augmentation. The safe immediate port is narrower: test feature-only affine perturbation or audited-helper corruption with labels unchanged. Every transform needs an explicit statement of whether inputs, labels, or both change.
- The source's model and negative-result sections (lines 24–33, 170–195) concern one ROGII solution. Fixed average pooling is a plausible cheap A/B; its U-Net, normalization, precision, transformer, and scale observations are not universal results.

## Reusable candidates and deferred variants

Most source-grounded next candidates are: (1) a per-target soft distribution head with expectation decoding over a training-defined range; (2) a reset-safe forward rare-jump belief with delayed coarse updates and fold-excluded likelihood/features; (3) causal trailing quadratic summaries matched against existing windows; and (4) a fixed trailing-pool versus learned-stride test in the current causal TCN. Auxiliary-channel corruption is a small, directly motivated follow-up once the exact teacher channels and ancestry are frozen.

Keep calibration, a reliability-gated blend, and the proposed magnitude penalty as separate, lower-confidence recipes. Defer XY/pseudo-coordinate geometry, target-path bootstrap, reverse-path augmentation, and any claimed TVT+Z-like invariant until data structure and a valid paired generator are independently established. No ordering here is a score prior or experiment admission.
