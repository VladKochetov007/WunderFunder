3. Medium edge case: a fully collapsed student gets a positive RKD penalty but zero RKD gradient.
At `rkd_component.py:114-130`, if all sampled student outputs are identical, every `vector_norm` distance is zero and `student_mean` is clamped to `tiny`; normalized student distances stay zero. PyTorch's vector-norm derivative at exact zero is zero, and the clamped denominator has zero derivative, so this relation term cannot move a collapsed student. The tests cover a collapsed teacher (`tests/test_rkd_component.py:99-111`) but not a collapsed student. The ordinary label loss remains active, so this is an edge case rather than proof the whole training run is stuck; still, either define a deliberate student-collapse policy or add a test documenting the stationary point and finite-gradient behavior.
