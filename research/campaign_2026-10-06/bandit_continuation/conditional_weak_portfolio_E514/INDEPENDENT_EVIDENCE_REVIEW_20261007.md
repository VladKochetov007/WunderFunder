Reviewed the preregistration, pre-score amendments and failure receipts, source manifests v1–v3, frozen chain partition, notebook, final result/report, inference receipts, and official `global_wp_clip2_v1` implementation. I also ran a bounded read-only hash/partition check; I did not rerun scores or fit models.

**Verdict:** The final calculation supports lowering priority for this exact weak-pool screen. On the registered calibration procedure, every selected lambda is zero, so every once-held-out endpoint is exactly the C1 anchor. This is valid evidence that this pool and screen did not add to the anchor on the previously exposed fold-0 chains. It is not evidence that all weak models or other candidate pools have zero population value.

## Result and limits

All four non-anchor strategies chose lambda 0 in all three outer folds; the conditional-signal set was empty in each fold. The other strategies selected members, but `_blend` returns the anchor whenever lambda is zero (`E514_RESULT.json:149-238,241-352`; `weak_portfolio_eda.py:481-508,572-627`). Thus the equal outer-fold scores and concatenated OOF WP of `0.6832264634983685` exactly match the anchor; every reported delta is exactly zero (`E514_RESULT.json:4-18,70-96`).

The 2,000-replicate paired-chain bootstrap intervals `[0,0]` are correctly computed for these identical endpoints, conditional on the outer-fold choices (`E514_RESULT.json:97-145`; implementation `weak_portfolio_eda.py:663-697`). They are degenerate because each selected candidate prediction is the anchor, not a precise estimate that a nonzero weak-model blend has no effect. The interval excludes uncertainty from selection, training, candidate-pool choice, and public transfer (`E514_RESULT.json:3`; `REPORT.md:16-20`).

Standalone scores on the exposed fold are below the C1 pair: ab12 `0.604966`, ab48 `0.662881`, baseline at its registered ×0.25 scale `0.659408`, and C1 pair `0.683226` (`E514_RESULT.json:356-360`). The reported mean weighted prediction correlations are high for ab48 and the baseline (about `0.972` and `0.967` versus C1), while ab12 is about `0.887` (`E514_RESULT.json:28-61`; calculation `weak_portfolio_eda.py:699-745`). These are descriptive diagnostics; they do not establish useful residual signal or a population ranking. No public gain or submission readiness is claimed (`E514_RESULT.json:64,362-368`; `REPORT.md:3-4,18-20`).
