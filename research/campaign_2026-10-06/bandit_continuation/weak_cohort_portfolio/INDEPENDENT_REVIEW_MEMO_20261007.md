# Independent evidence review: E-513 and separate E-511 ancestry check

Review scope: read-only inspection and bounded CPU replay of the frozen E-513 prediction analysis and its source lineage. No model fitting, prediction generation, Kaggle execution, or edits to the original E-513 result were performed. This memo is a separate correction record.

## E-513 finding

**The E-513 numerical analysis is reproducible, but its preregistered out-of-fold (OOF) claim fails.** An untracked propensity-model ancestry used evaluation-fold `is_scored` masks for fit and/or checkpoint selection. The reported scores therefore remain descriptive, exposed-fold results; they do not establish a clean held-out comparison. This is a confirmed provenance error, not evidence that the weak-portfolio mechanism is false.

The preregistration says each prediction is on rows held out from member fit and requires calibration on one pure fold followed by evaluation on the other (`PRE_REGISTRATION.md:11-28`). The pre-score addendum says to stop if fold/ancestry checks fail (`PRE_SCORE_ADDENDUM_20261007.json:6-8,28`). The notebook's actual gate checks source hashes, row alignment, run-card fold fields, and TD5 config fields (`weak_cohort_eda.py:130-185,257-271`), but it does not trace propensity-model training or checkpoint-selection ancestry. The omitted check allowed a result to pass a gate whose stated ancestry requirement it did not satisfy.

The official metric implementation is appropriate: the EDA uses `is_scored AND step >= 99` (`weak_cohort_eda.py:61-89`), blends raw float32 predictions in float64 arithmetic before casting back to float32 (`weak_cohort_eda.py:300-304`), and delegates clipping and clipped-target-magnitude weighting to `global_wp_clip2_v1` (`src/wnn/gmetric.py:23-38,159-216`). The scorer clips both target and prediction to ±2 and weights by the absolute clipped target. Pure-chain exclusion is also implemented as preregistered: fold 0 has 432/468 groups (36 train-bridge groups excluded), fold 1 has 399/457 (58 excluded), with 123 and 126 pure chains/components respectively (`weak_cohort_eda.py:73-104`; `E513_RESULT.json:437-438,461,500-501,517-524`).

The four preregistered full-fold card checks pass the stated 0.000051 tolerance. My bounded streaming replay reproduced anchor f0/f1 at 0.676105497/0.662341800 and p2 f0/f1 at 0.673887558/0.659491402, within 0.0000055/0.0000418/0.0000124/0.0000086 of their rounded cards. I also rehashed all 40 pre-score manifest entries successfully and independently replayed the pure-fold blend and bootstrap outputs. The result documents the 40/40 and score-gate status (`REPORT.md:7-12`); legacy prediction arrays still have no row-order sidecars, so alignment remains reconstructed from the cache-order contract and score reproduction (`SOURCE_MANIFEST_E513.json:270-290`).

### Confirmed propensity ancestry failure

The propensity target is the validation `is_scored` mask, not the competition return target: `train_propensity.py:1-5,122-135`. In the generic path, `devA` is used for fitting and `devB` for AUC evaluation; checkpoint policy defaults to `best` and evaluation is enabled unless `--no-eval-during-fit` is supplied (`train_propensity.py:46-55,84-100,141-180`). The selector chooses by the devB AUC when using the default `best` policy (`train_rnn.py:872-884`).

The E-513 anchor cards use `prop_v1` (`research/runs/ab56_ema_e4_f0.md:14`; `research/runs/ab58_ema_e4_f1.md:14`), as do TD5 f0/f1 (`artifacts/logs/ab62_td5_f0.log:1`; corresponding f1 log). E-031 records that `prop_v1` fit devA masks and measured devB AUC (`research/experiments.jsonl:31`); its AUC increased through epoch 3 from 0.8731 to 0.9111 (`artifacts/runs/prop_v1/history.json:1-22`), so the best checkpoint was selected using devB mask labels. Reconstructed by row group against the frozen chain-fold map, 166/432 pure fold-0 groups and 155/399 pure fold-1 groups were in prop_v1's devA fit set; another 178/432 and 163/399, respectively, were in its devB checkpoint-selection set.

The p2 cards use `prop_v2_f0/f1` (`artifacts/logs/ab53_p2_f0.log:1`; corresponding f1 log). Their launcher fits each propensity model with `--heldout-fold k` and omits both a fixed checkpoint and `--no-eval-during-fit` (`artifacts/logs/q15.sh:8,11-14`). The same trainer's heldout-fold branch puts fold `k` in devB (`train_propensity.py:84-100`); devB AUC therefore used the exact fold later treated as p2 evaluation. The mask-selection AUC peaks at epoch 7 for both models: 0.9316 for f0 and 0.9307 for f1 (`artifacts/runs/prop_v2_f0/history.json:45-46`; `artifacts/runs/prop_v2_f1/history.json:45-46`). Thus all pure fold-0 and fold-1 groups used by E-513 were included in the corresponding p2 propensity checkpoint-selection set. The basehead logs show no propensity source (`artifacts/logs/ab48_basehead_f0.log:1`; f1 equivalent), but that does not rescue comparisons whose anchor and other cohort members inherit the exposed propensity source.

This is mask exposure through the propensity weights that shape model fitting, not direct exposure to held-out `y`. It still violates the pre-score fold/ancestry gate and the project requirement to keep propensity ancestry out of evaluated fitting groups. The original manifest contains the evaluated member checkpoints and run inputs but not the propensity model checkpoints/provenance or the propensity trainer/launcher needed to establish this lineage (`SOURCE_MANIFEST_E513.json:22-35,83-175`; confirmed by inventory). **Classify E-513's OOF gate as failed and its scores as contaminated for held-out inference.** Do not silently rewrite the original result.

### Numerical interpretation and seed correction

Conditional on the fixed predictions and selected lambda, the point deltas are small. Calibration 0 → evaluation 1 selects cohort λ=0.40 (single-basehead λ=0.15) and gives Δ vs anchor +0.000427532, with reported 95% chain-bootstrap interval [−0.000248764,+0.001145841] and `p_le_0=0.1105`; Δ vs single is −0.000030684, interval [−0.000432686,+0.000367747], `p_le_0=0.548` (`E513_RESULT.json:397-469`). Calibration 1 → evaluation 0 selects cohort λ=0.35 (single λ=0.20) and gives Δ vs anchor +0.000532990, interval [−0.000598075,+0.001531214], `p_le_0=0.169`; Δ vs single is +0.000212738, interval [−0.000340776,+0.000859168], `p_le_0=0.229` (`E513_RESULT.json:471-532`). Every interval spans zero, and the cohort's point delta versus the single-member control changes sign. The selected cohort values also sit at the top of the frozen grid, which is an exploratory signal and not authority to extend it on these folds (`REPORT.md:21-23`). The standalone equal weak mean exceeds the mean of member standalone scores descriptively, but that does not show it is a better anchor complement (`E513_RESULT.json:645-663`).

There is a confirmed bootstrap-seed protocol deviation. The preregistration specifies exactly `int(first 8 hex digits of source_manifest_sha256, 16)` (`PRE_SCORE_ADDENDUM_20261007.json:6-8`), yielding 4,001,575,692. The notebook adds `evaluation_fold` (`weak_cohort_eda.py:415-430`), so evaluation fold 1 used 4,001,575,693 (`E513_RESULT.json:416,441,458`). Replaying with the exact preregistered seed for 0 → 1 gives cohort-vs-anchor CI [−0.000231255892,+0.001124412625], `p_le_0=0.097`, and cohort-vs-single CI [−0.000443715205,+0.000371687611], `p_le_0=0.549`. For 1 → 0 the stored seed already equals the preregistered seed, and the intervals/fractions are the values above. `p_le_0` is a bootstrap sign fraction, not a formal hypothesis-test p-value. The seed deviation does not change the substantive interpretation.

The bootstrap resamples complete pure chains/components, is conditional on the calibration-selected lambda, and does not include training-seed or public-transfer uncertainty (`PRE_REGISTRATION.md:24-34`; `weak_cohort_eda.py:391-450`; `REPORT.md:38`). Both folds were previously inspected and used by E-177 and related scans, so they are not fresh confirmation (`PRE_REGISTRATION.md:34`; `E513_RESULT.json:538-555`). The bootstrap helper was not among the original 40 pre-score manifest entries, though its post-score code receipt exists and I replayed the outputs (`REPORT.md:40`).

**Allocation recommendation:** close the exact fixed-cohort/blend allocation and screen E-513 as insufficient evidence, with an explicit provenance/OOF-gate failure annotation. This recommendation follows both the failed ancestry gate and the unmet promotion rule: the cohort does not beat the single control in both directions and no paired interval excludes zero. Retain H-201 as insufficient evidence and keep weak-model averaging available as a broader mechanism for a materially different cohort, teacher target, or representation use; this result does not refute that family (`PRE_REGISTRATION.md:34`; `E513_RESULT.json:538-555`; `REPORT.md:34-40`).

Its exact job command requests the fold0 manifest, fixed epoch 3, and `--no-eval-during-fit` (`research/campaign_2026-09-29/jobs/c20260929_prop_f0.json:6-26`); the terminal log records `evaluation: withheld` at every epoch (`research/campaign_2026-09-29/logs/c20260929_prop_f0.log:1-5`). The source trainer fits `campaign_split.valid_groups`, constructs no test loader under no-eval, and records fixed-epoch selection (`scripts/train_propensity.py:64-100,109-115,139-180`). Its provenance confirms 1,405 fit-valid groups, the 468 fold-0 evaluation groups, `selected_epoch=3`, and checkpoint SHA-256 `b243b9d44a71bcaa02589be0a38b19477f448f9da98a02e16495af6730b517a7` (`artifacts/runs/c20260929_prop_f0/provenance.json:1-19,1427-1435,1897-1905`). No held-out mask or return score selected this propensity checkpoint.

C1 seed3 provenance uses the same manifest hash, 10,535 train + 1,405 valid fit groups, 468 evaluation groups, fixed epoch 1 with evaluation withheld, and the same propensity checkpoint hash (`artifacts/runs/bd26_c1v2_s3_f0/provenance.json:30-34,114,166,202-203,12149,12624-12625,12795`). This propensity ancestry does not undermine C1's fold-0 group holdout. It is still an exposed, previously inspected fold, not a fresh confirmatory holdout. Therefore no completed paired C1 conclusion should be implied for seed3/seed4 until seed4 terminates and the pair is graded.
