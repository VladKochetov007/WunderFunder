# Review of the quant literature cards

Root reviewed the source-bound Grok hypothesis cards while the history-swap
student was fitting. No proposed feature has been computed or scored in this
review. The cards remain untested mechanism proposals with null numerical priors.

The shared within-sequence circular shift by half the sequence length is not a
causal inference control: its wraparound sends future observations into earlier
rows. It can be an explicitly labeled diagnostic of alignment, but cannot be
treated as a valid served competitor or used to establish the gain of a causal
model. Keep the original cards unchanged and declare a successor before execution.

For a predictive screen, compare the unmodified input model with a model receiving
the proposed past-only state. If a timing control is useful, use a fixed past-only
delay of the added state, with a declared neutral initial state and explicit
warmup behavior. It does not preserve exactly the same marginals; report that
limitation rather than describe it as a matched circular-shift control. Keep
model capacity, training membership, step budget and official evaluation mask
fixed. An input-only static graph permutation is a separate control for peer
selection, not a control for temporal alignment.

The age/expiry, transient shock inventory, sparse peer gaps and conditional tail
expert mechanisms remain eligible. Start with one bounded streaming census of
feature updates and isolated shocks using already permitted training groups.
Store the source-qualified membership and descriptive plots in this lineage.
Do not make a nondegenerate census or a significant coefficient a universal
requirement for all descendants: it informs how this particular representation
should be defined. Keep large-model and interaction experiments eligible when
their own recipes provide a concrete mechanism and a bounded useful test.

Avoid conflating anonymous-coordinate analogies with identified market meaning.
Any teacher pair or conditional blend must be evaluated as a complete endpoint;
its weaker member's standalone score does not decide its conditional value.

Execution ownership is not assigned by this note. The active owners are delivering
the matched student, GRU384 and embedding predictions, and a real-data TPU gate.
Allocate the census to a freed CPU-analysis owner once the cloud packets are
running, without delaying those ready experiments.
