Johansen Procedure
The Johansen Test tests if multiple L(1) Time Series are cointegrated or not.
L(1) means that if you take the last value from your time series and subtract it from the
current value then your timeseries becomes stationary.
L(2) would mean you would have to do this twice for it to become stationary etc.

Greedy Search
So we have been looking at constructing a mean reverting portfolio but not a sparse one.
We want to find the most mean reverting portfolio in our entire universe that only
contains at most k assets. Let’s formulate this problem:

So we want to find the weights that minimize our portfolios predictability but the number
of non-zero elements in our weight vector should be smaller than or equal to k and the
weights should sum up to 1.
As usual you would do maximization for a momentum portfolio.
This problem is NP-Hard.
We need to get a suboptimal solution that is still good and fast enough.

One of them is called Greedy Search.
