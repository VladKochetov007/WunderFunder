## Allocation and update rules

Approach diversity precedes seed-only repetition. Leading lineages, independent exploration and confirmation/serving retain separate effort. Register combinations with explicit parents; a weaker parent can contribute conditionally. Execution failures record infrastructure evidence rather than negative scientific rewards. Untested priors remain null. Reassess at terminal results and approximately every two hours; replenish distinct source-grounded arms when useful admitted work is insufficient.

Every new proposal gets an append-only hypothesis card, a curated arm or explicit existing-node association, source notes and a bounded discriminating test. An actual run additionally gets an experiment/attempt record, frozen recipe and data/source/checkpoint/prediction provenance. Update the frontier and result notes only from terminal evidence; rebuild the tree/catalog after meaningful changes. Preserve superseded cards and decisions.
