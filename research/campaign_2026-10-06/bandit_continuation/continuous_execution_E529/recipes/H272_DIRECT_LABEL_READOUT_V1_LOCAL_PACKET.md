# H-272 direct-label readout packet

H-272 is a source-prepared target-objective comparison for the H-233 readouts. It keeps the frozen C1 seed-3 epoch-1 parent, all four rank-32 readout controls, fold-0 membership, propensity and true-mask weighting, seed, optimizer, two epochs, fixed epoch 1, batch size 8, and 2,000-row chunks. Its only scientific change is that the readouts target the original labels clipped to `[-2, 2]` instead of H-233's 30% label / 70% teacher blend.

The H-233 auxiliary teacher prediction inputs and their readout-fit ancestry checks are removed. The exact C1 parent checkpoint and provenance remain hash-pinned, including that parent's own E511 teacher-distillation lineage. The direct-label loader uses the same train and fit-development group tuples, concatenation order, seed-3 DataLoader generator, shuffle and `drop_last`; it passes `teachers=None` to the unchanged `Sequences` reader and rejects any target batch with columns beyond the original pair. This removes teacher-array reads without changing sequence or group order.

`direct_label_target()` is the production target function. The tiny fixture checks fixed clipped outputs and a hand-computed weighted loss of `2.5`; another fixture checks that teacher-appended targets fail closed. The readout architecture and weighted regression stay unchanged. The worker changes the target path, removes the auxiliary readout-teacher inputs, and adds H-272 identity and update-count guards. The source lock records all 55 included code/runtime members; 51 are byte-identical to the H-233 copy, three existing code/test files are changed, and one H-272 guard test is new.

The fitted cohort would contain 10,535 train groups plus 1,405 fit-development valid groups, with all 468 evaluation groups excluded. The frozen loader gives 1,492 batches per epoch and ten chunks per sequence, for 14,920 optimizer updates per epoch and 29,840 total. These counts are asserted in the worker and recipe. Fixed prediction export remains evaluation-mask-only under the unchanged official metric.

This is source preparation only. No profile, fit, export or score was run. The profile and fit argv, resource ceilings, and root-owned admission conditions are in [the profile/fit contract](H272_DIRECT_LABEL_PROFILE_FIT_CONTRACT_20261008_R1.json). The exact source and recipe bindings are in [the frozen recipe](H272_C1S3_DIRECT_LABEL_READOUT_V1.json) and [source lock](H272_DIRECT_LABEL_FROZEN_RUNTIME_SOURCE_LOCK_V1_20261008.json).
