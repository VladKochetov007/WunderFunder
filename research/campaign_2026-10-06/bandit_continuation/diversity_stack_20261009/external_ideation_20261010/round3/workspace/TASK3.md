## Task facts
- Data: 20,000-row sequences with 112 anonymous numeric columns per row. About 12,000 training sequences plus 1,873 validation sequences (4 component-disjoint folds). Two targets (t0, t1) per row, scored from row 99 on. Causal prediction only at serving (one CPU core, 60 min for ~39M rows, which limits the served model to roughly one 2-layer GRU of width 256); training-time resources and TEACHERS are unconstrained (GPUs, large models, offline lookahead are allowed for teachers as long as the served student is causal and trained only on supplied data).
- Metric: weighted Pearson, pooled over rows and both targets, weights |clip(y, -2, 2)|. Our best public score 0.6664 (validation folds 0.668 / 0.679 / 0.703 / 0.707; folds differ in difficulty). The population-optimal predictor under this metric is m(H) = E[w*y | H] / E[w | H] with w = |y|; our weighted-MSE loss is aligned with it.
- Labels are piecewise constant in time (mean constant-run length 9.5 rows). The label changes 3.2x more often on rows where a block of 22 'fast clock' columns updates (31% of rows). The 112 columns form blocks that update on identical rows (multi-rate clocks: blocks update on 31%, 40%, 15%, 12%, 69-71%, 100% of rows); no column is a small discrete alphabet. t0 and t1 change together only 2.3x more than chance.
- Four 'market classes' per sequence (defined from labels) are decodable from the first 500 rows of features with 99.4% accuracy. Class 3 holds half of the metric weight and has correlation 0.44 (classes 0-2: 0.74-0.81). Its weight sits in long label runs (73% of its weight in runs older than 16 rows).
- Error decomposition (validation fold 1, best student WP 0.669): with the true sign and the model's magnitude WP is 0.843; with the model's sign and the true magnitude WP is 0.685. Weighted sign accuracy 84.3% (class 3: 80.7%). Sign accuracy is 90-92% right after a label change and decays to 74% in runs older than 512 rows. Causal smoothing or sign-holding of the model output always lowers WP (the late sign flips carry information).
- Teacher side plateau (all within the 0.002 noise): GRU/LSTM/TCN/gated/two-clock variants, Muon vs AdamW (+0.004), width 384 (+0.0012 over 256 on four folds), 3 vs 4 epochs, schedules, dropout, losses (Huber and correlation losses harmful), class weights, EMA/SWA, wider models to 768 (worse), seed averaging (+0.0015-0.002 per ensemble, not servable), distilling wider/ensemble teachers into the width-256 student (no transfer), four teacher families (pairwise prediction correlation 0.95-0.99). Chunk length 500 vs 1000 rows of truncated back-propagation: +0.002 in one run (unreplicated).
- Class 3 is data-limited (half the data costs 0.009), class-3-only specialists are far worse (-0.14), synthetic labels hurt. Hidden-test loss relative to validation concentrates in class 3.
- Not yet tried: selective state-space teachers, exact bid/ask mirror decoding, mixture/quantile output heads, explicit change-point/regime-switching state, causal test-time adaptation, multi-rate hierarchical recurrence, learning from a future-aware (privileged) teacher, predicting label-change events instead of levels.
