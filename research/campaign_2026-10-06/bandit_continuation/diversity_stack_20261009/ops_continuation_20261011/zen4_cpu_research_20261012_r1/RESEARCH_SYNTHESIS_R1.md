## Proposed algorithmic arms and their falsifiers

Two explicitly requested independent Sol6.1/xhigh reviews were sealed before synthesis. They produced distinct proposals; agreement is not validation. Their [original reports](INDEX.md) remain separate.

- Fuse activation output and max-absolute reduction: remove a vector pass while preserving activation arithmetic, exceptional-value treatment and whole-vector quantization scale. It does not remove Q8 matrix traffic; profile its fraction before predicting a speedup.
- Fuse16-unit recurrent gate tiles: retain separate input/hidden scales, bias and nonlinear order; snapshot the old quantized hidden state until the complete layer finishes. A single combined input/hidden integer dot is incorrect when activation scales differ. Register pressure and full-trajectory parity are real gates.
- Test genuine256-bit EVEX AVX512VL-VNNI scheduling against ZMM: the current AVX2 widened-byte route is a different comparison. Zen4 code/assembly and actual-device timing are needed; Intel-only results cannot choose the scorer schedule conclusively.
- Reuse exact nonzero indices/counters only where current code repeats them. Generic widths, dense inputs and the already-shared feature path limit scope. Do not resurrect closed approximate duplicate-row predictions.
- Selective low-bit / mixed precision / faithful QAT: preserve sensitive recurrent/head blocks; grade the complete blend. Q5–Q7 storage may save ZIP bytes but needs a native decode path for compute gains. Existing weight-only Q8 fake quantization does not reproduce deployed dynamic activation-Q8 behavior automatically.
- Complete-ensemble distillation and low-rank factors: distinguish source signal from serving compression. For a3H×H matrix, FP32 factors use16Hr bytes versus3H² Q8 bytes; smaller traffic requires r<3H/16 (r<72 atH384), before metadata. A high rank is not free compression. Full Q4 H384 still exceeds1MiB once input projection and metadata are included; L2 fit is not established.

These are proposed arms, not a promise that all are dispatched. Admission requires a concrete treatment, unchanged control, bounded resources and actual artifacts. No HPO timer or paid spending path is reopened.
