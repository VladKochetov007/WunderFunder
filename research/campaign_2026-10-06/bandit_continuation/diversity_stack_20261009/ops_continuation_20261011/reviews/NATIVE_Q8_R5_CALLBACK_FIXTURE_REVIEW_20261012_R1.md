## Remaining limits before any full-fold admission

The fixture runner’s 180-second deadline is cooperative: checks occur after predictor construction and between callbacks, it cannot interrupt an in-progress sequence callback, and there is no final deadline check after the last callback. Its receipt does not itself establish an outer systemd `RuntimeMaxSec`. Any later full-fold attempt needs a fresh launch through the bounded `scripts/limited` wrapper with a hard runtime limit and observed cgroup membership/limits before data access; do not treat the fixture’s cgroup snapshot or four sequences as a full-fold profile. `torch_imported:false` is a literal receipt value rather than a dynamic `sys.modules` assertion.

The actual fixture was not rerun under this review: no model/predictor construction, parquet page reads, inference, fit, score, provider action, or package installation was performed. Callback/reset evidence can satisfy one precondition for later full-fold admission; it does not establish process-wide timing, full-fold memory/runtime, official metric validity, ZIP readiness, or a scientific result.
