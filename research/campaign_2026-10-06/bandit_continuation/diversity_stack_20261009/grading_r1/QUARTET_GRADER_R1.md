# Optimizer/loss quartet grader

`scripts/grade_optimizer_loss_quartet.py` implements the eleven endpoints and paired whole-component bootstrap declared in `FROZEN_D02_QUARTET_R1.json`. No endpoint or blend coefficient was selected using Muon results. The R3 log-path failure has no scientific score; the same comparison applies to a root-reviewed successor only after source/recipe equivalence and terminal provenance are verified.

The CLI requires the exact preregistration digest and an independently reviewed four-arm artifact-binding file. Each binding identifies terminal review, provenance, checkpoint, prediction and sidecar, plus initialization, normalization, sample order and schedule artifacts. The consumer checks all identities, matching fit membership, evaluation component exclusion, FP32 NPY shape and finiteness, and actual sidecars. Root terminal review must separately verify tensors, full update counters, the conditional device gates, producer receipt closure and source ancestry before creating that binding.

Raw predictions are averaged in FP64, rounded to FP32, and then passed to the unchanged official scorer. A clipping-order counterexample was checked. Every endpoint is checked against the independent centered metric formula to1e-12. All secondary contrasts are reported together as exposed-panel discovery evidence; the paired bootstrap conditions on these fitted models and does not include training variation or public transfer.

`QUARTET_GRADER_ACTUAL_D01_FIXTURE_R1.json` records successful consumer validation of the two real AdamW producer cells. It is not a completed quartet or a Muon score. Source formatting/static checks passed. A four-arm binding and final result remain pending the Muon terminal artifacts.
