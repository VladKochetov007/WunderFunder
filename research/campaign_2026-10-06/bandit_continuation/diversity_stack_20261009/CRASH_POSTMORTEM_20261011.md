## Lane b tick (controller.py tick) explained + fix, 2026-10-11 04:30
Tick = unattended 5-min safety/feeding loop: (1) pull finished jobs (scp 286 MB pred npy + checkpoints, bank row) (2) spend cap: at cap move pending jobs to held/ (3) auto-feed backlog when pending<3 (4) DESTROY the receipt-owned instance when drained (stops paying). Stuck because step 1 is serial and slow (scp ~300 MB per job over a slow link, backlog of ~10 files) and systemd oneshot blocks the next tick => no cap/feed/drain while pulling. Not needed for experiments to run or be scored (scored on host via score_prediction_remote.py; jobs live in the host refill daemon), but needed for cost-safety and local bank/ensemble analysis. Fix: `tick --no-pull` + separate `wnn-vast-pull-lane-b.timer` (10 min after previous finish, lock-serialised, 2G/7200s timeout). Data not lost: preds stay on host; pull is resumable (skips banked names).
