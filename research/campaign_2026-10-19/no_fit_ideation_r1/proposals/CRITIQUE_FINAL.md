# Final quick audit — learning information R2

I found one hard mathematical error and one optimizer-control claim that needs careful wording. The equal 20,000-row sequence length is consistent with the task specification. The file’s weighted-MSE identity is correctly presented as a population result, not a finite-model guarantee.

- **Card 2: preconditioning does not leave the expected update vector unchanged.** A covariance preconditioner `P` preserves the population risk being optimized, but the update becomes `−P g`; for non-identity `P`, its expectation is generally not `−E[g]`. If `P` is estimated from the same sequence-block gradients, dependence makes that equality even less plausible. Reword this as changing optimizer geometry while retaining the objective, and measure any update bias/benefit. Do not claim an unchanged expected update.
- **Card 6: uncertainty-based damping is adaptive, not an unbiased correction.** Multiplying the next step by a jackknife-dependent factor changes the expected step and can alter the finite-capacity solution, even though the official WP target and row weights remain fixed. Present it as a heuristic learning-rate policy; compare with a matched fixed-step schedule and do not imply that it only reduces estimator noise.
- **Card 4: applicability is conditional.** Prefix replay is useful only for chunk training that would otherwise initialize interior chunks incorrectly. If the actual trainer already carries/reconstructs the forward state, this card is inapplicable; the proposal correctly states that condition, so it should not become a “zero-reset fix” without checking the recipe.

The conditional-variance objective in card 3 has the stated weighted conditional-mean minimizer under its correctly specified/population assumptions; finite-capacity shared features may still couple the mean and variance heads. The other cards read as testable optimizer/model-bias hypotheses, not hard mathematical claims. No empirical result or numerical gain is established here.
