# Prior-overlap and invalid-inference audit

This audit follows the frozen independent note at `proposals/blind_critique.md`. It uses the prior 25-card portfolio and the 17 October R2 report/reflection only. It adds no data analysis or mechanism evidence. The novelty labels below are portfolio bookkeeping, not scientific verdicts.

## Prior exclusions that should constrain synthesis

- R2 measured the within/between sequence contribution: do not resubmit it as a new no-fit discovery, infer that between-sequence signal generalizes, or conclude that removing sequence-level context is beneficial. The raw-level bypass in H-RR17-06 remains a safeguard to test, not a result.
- R2’s frozen-output transforms (AND/OR holds, cadence, EMA, and blends) were negative on three fixed arrays. They did not freeze hidden state, certify producer provenance, or test whether every quiet-row input is useful. Therefore “smooth or hold because labels persist” is excluded; hidden-state interventions remain a separate, source-bound question.
- The rare target-clock exceptions have tiny aggregate scored weight and short offsets. They do not justify an exception-specific memory block, event-triggered serving rule, or generic “long quiet period” mechanism.
- Generic slow/event memory is not new: DualClock and related event/interval-memory work already exist. H-RR17-03 can only claim a narrower main-state write/decay intervention, with a matched same-information recurrent control. H-RR17-12 already captures a CUSUM-specific version.
- A future-label oracle’s improvement is retrospective and not a causal ceiling. Neither it nor the heterogeneous age-by-target-class tables establish a causal age gate; target-derived classes and label-run boundaries are unavailable online.
- The saved-output arrays in R2 are aligned to rows but not source-bound to the currently inspected checkpoints/preprocessing. Their transformations are evidence about those arrays only, not certified current-model causal effects.
- The moment/scale and occupancy pilots are small, exposed, short-budget evidence. They do not establish strict normalization, component equivariance, or latent transition topology; they do reduce novelty for generic “moments,” “scale,” and occupancy/dwell ideas.

## Criterion for a genuinely new mechanism

A candidate should name one exact causal intervention on the current row and permitted prefix, identify its nearest prior card or implementation, and state the one operator, information path, or optimization target that differs. It must make a prediction that the nearest prior control does not already make, and name a cheap falsifier that can reject the premise before a full comparison. For example, “main recurrent write gating” can remain distinct from an auxiliary event-memory branch only if the gate changes the main state transition and is compared with a recurrence that receives the same information. “Use more history,” “share across anonymous features,” “adapt to regimes,” or “preserve two-target balance” are families or diagnostics, not distinct mechanisms until this difference is specified. All checks and future treatments must remain causal, reset per sequence, and independent of targets, `seq_ix`, and hidden `is_scored` at inference.

No blind idea merits a new final-25 card in its present wording. Items 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, and 8 overlap named prior mechanisms or measured diagnostics; item 3 is an uncertainty that should not block empirical history tests; item 5 is an inference constraint, not a predictive mechanism. This audit can be revisited after the other independent proposals freeze.
