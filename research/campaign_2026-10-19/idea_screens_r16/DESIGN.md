## Estimator (FP64 NumPy, no Torch)

For each direction independently fit Xpast channel mean/std on its15904training rows; scale=max(population std,1e-6). Delta is divided by the same raw-input scale, then destination delta means are fitted and subtracted. Fit a three-component common PCA to normalized centered Xpast on the same training rows. Use the top3eigenvectors of empirical112x112 covariance, descending eigenvalue, with sign chosen by positive largest-absolute loading (first index for ties). Normalize PC scores by sqrt(max(eigenvalue,1e-12)). PCA is an explicit common-factor linear control here, not sparse factors, economic ancestry or an identified common cause. Record eigenvalues and finite/rank checks.

For each destination B, Z=[normalized Xpast_B, three normalized common scores]. Fit baseline Y_B=mu_delta_B+Z beta by ridge with exact objective sum squared errors +100*sum beta^2, intercept handled by fitted response mean and not penalized. No iteration/learning rate/optimizer or label clipping.

For each source A!=B: regress normalized Xpast_A on Z using the symmetric Moore-Penrose inverse of Z.T@Z with eigenvalue cutoff1e-10*lambda_max (for the symmetric Gram matrix reject eigenvalues < -tol where tol=1e-10*lambda_max; treat eigenvalues in [-tol,tol] as zero and invert only eigenvalues>tol). Let R_A=Xpast_A-Z gamma. Normalize residual columns by their training RMS floored1e-6. Fit a ridge100 correction to baseline training residuals using R_A. Freeze raw/PC/response means/scales, gamma, beta and correction. On held components predict base and base+correction with those fitting-only transforms. This stagewise linear residualization avoids claiming that a duplicated common-factor column supplied new information; it is one declared estimator, not nonparametric conditional independence.

## Primary outcome and spending gate

For each of12(pair,direction) evaluations record total baseline and corrected SSE across all destination channels/held rows, delta energy (sum squared standardized true delta), channel/row counts, and the same compact scalar sums per held component. Primary edge gain=(SSE_base-SSE_corrected)/SSE_base. A zero/near-zero baseline SSE<=1e-12 gives undefined gain and gate false, never row/channel replacement. If any component denominator is undefined, its edge median gate is false; do not drop that component from the median. This input-predictive measure is NOT competition WP or a Y-target score.

An edge is stable for this allocation screen iff relative SSE reduction>=.01 in BOTH L-to-R and R-to-L and the median of32component relative reductions is>0 in BOTH directions. Primary report: all six paired gains plus number of stable edges. Spending gate: at least one stable edge.
