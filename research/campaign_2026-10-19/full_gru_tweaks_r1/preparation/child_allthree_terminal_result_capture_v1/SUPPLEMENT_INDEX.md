# AllThree terminal result-capture supplement

This directory contains an inert, conditional metadata reader and its source/adaptation records. The independent reader review is `PASS_CONDITIONAL_SOURCE_ONLY`, but five actual support admission/launch byte, SHA, and invocation constants remain unbound; the reader therefore cannot query or capture a support unit yet. No actual capture, admission, or remote query is established, and no new fit or quality evidence is provided.

The preserved producer-schema negative is historical: it reviewed an older 51,884-byte producer draft whose full body was not archived and found missing `invocation_id` and `native_unit` fields. The current conditional producer-template review records those fields as corrected. Do not treat the older negative as a finding against the current template or as a remote failure.

## Files

- [Reader README](README.md) describes the five required support constants and the bounded, read-only capture contract.
- [Result-capture template](RESULT_CAPTURE_TEMPLATE.py) is the conditional reader source; it has no actual support-receipt bindings.
- [AST literal-adaptation record](AST_LITERAL_ADAPTATION_V1.json) records the NLL-to-AllThree literal adaptation and seven-code pin; its declared AST proof was not executed by the independent reviewer.
- [Conditional source review](SOURCE_REVIEW_V1.json) records `PASS_CONDITIONAL_SOURCE_ONLY`, with zero remote queries and no actual support receipts bound.
- [Historical producer-schema negative](SOURCE_REVIEW_V1_PRODUCER_SCHEMA_NEGATIVE.json) documents the unarchived older draft mismatch and its two missing launch keys; it is retained as history only.
