# AllThree terminal checker supplement

This local packet binds a seven-file checker closure, a source-only review, a root-owned preparation, and a narrow private-import boundary proof. The checker validates the already-finished AllThree fit's terminal evidence and integrity receipts; it does not fit or score a model. The root preparation is 20,096 bytes and pins seven code files plus 58 metadata/proof inputs, including the 14-entry ancestry map, profile/cache receipts, and the fixed path authority. The separate metadata review records a local-only audit of those pins.

The final private-import proof is the current proof pin. The two `_NONFINAL` proof files preserve earlier harness observations in place; those observations are not archived, are not current dependencies, and are not current pins. No checker or training entrypoint was imported or executed for the metadata review. This packet makes no new-fit, quality, B-staging, admission, or execution claim.
