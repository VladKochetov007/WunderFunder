# AllThree terminal verifier V3 receipt-source repair

V2's first remote preflight stopped before private-checker import, output-directory creation, or support-unit submission. It attempted to read five original fit receipts from the B-side native evidence directory. The follow-up read-only capture showed that only `ADMISSION.json` and `LAUNCH.json` exist there; `LAUNCH_ACCEPTANCE.json`, `ALLTHREE_LAUNCH_STDOUT.raw.json`, and `TRANSPORT.json` are root-provided pinned payloads.

V3 starts from the exact V2 body and changes only `check_fit_receipts`. All five embedded receipts must still match their fixed payload lengths and SHA-256 values. The two native receipts are also read from the existing native evidence directory and compared byte-for-byte with those payloads. The other three names are explicitly classified as root-only payloads and are never opened from, copied to, or recreated in the B-side native directory. Existing admission, acceptance, lineage, source, native-terminal, and fifteen-payload checks remain in the body.

[`V3_BINDING.json`](V3_BINDING.json) records the exact V2 failure, the read-only outcome capture, the source-only V2 review, the inherited root-preparation/native pins, and all fifteen unchanged payload identities. The narrow [local fixture](test_allthree_terminal_verifier_v3_receipt_sources.py) checks the exact inverse-byte relation, confirms that only `check_fit_receipts` differs at the AST level, and rejects incorrect or incomplete receipt-source classes. These checks parse source text only; they do not import or execute a worker or checker. [`LOCAL_CHECKS.json`](LOCAL_CHECKS.json) records the fixture and Ruff results, and [`REMOTE_SUBMISSION_V3.py`](REMOTE_SUBMISSION_V3.py) is the exact prepared body.

This is local preparation. The recorded V2 attempt was one failed preflight SSH and its follow-up was one read-only capture; neither created a support unit or admitted work. V3 still needs an independent exact-body review and root's matching stdin/transport binding. No new fit, quality score, staging, admission, or execution is claimed here.
