# Screen map for H-NF19-01–25

This maps the 25 proposals in [`IDEAS_25.md`](../no_fit_ideation_r1/IDEAS_25.md) onto the bounded screen defined in [`CONTRACT.md`](CONTRACT.md). It is a test plan and interpretation guide, not a report of measured results. At writing time, every row below is still pending execution or explicitly not tested.

## Evidence boundary

The screen may read at most 48 complete 20,000-row sequences from the existing exposed canonical F0 membership. Select whole connected components with target-blind seed 19019, record membership before reading arrays, and keep fitting and evaluation components disjoint (at most 32 fit and 16 evaluation groups). This is a small, already-exposed development panel, not CP30, a fresh holdout, or clean cross-validation. Report any score on the selected scored evaluation rows as a bounded allocation clue only.

The fixed target-operator check uses only fitting components, with its own component separation and the preregistered horizons 1, 8, 32, 128, and 512. Future input rows may be used there only to explain fit-side labels diagnostically. They must never enter predictive features. For predictive probes, build all histories causally, process full sequences before taking the frozen training-row stride (20 or 40), use train-only normalization, clipped labels and `abs(clipped_label)` weights, and fixed ridge 1.0. Freeze no more than ten feature views before evaluation; score every selected scored evaluation row with the official clipped weighted Pearson metric. Do not select features, horizons, rows, or configurations from evaluation outcomes.

The ridge is a small float64 CPU probe. It measures whether a fixed linear readout can use a declared representation on this panel. It does not measure a recurrent training recipe, optimizer behavior, hidden state, full-model quality, fresh-fold transfer, or public score. A feature view omitted from the frozen ten remains `not_tested`; post-hoc fit or evaluation changes do not turn it into a measured result.

Status terms below mean:

- **EDA candidate (pending):** an admissible descriptive check can screen an observable premise. It does not establish target relevance unless that relation is explicitly checked on fitting components.
- **Ridge candidate (pending/conditional):** a fixed feature-view contrast could measure incremental linear predictive utility if it is included in the predeclared limit of ten.
- **NOT_TESTED:** the defining mechanism requires a different experiment or a materially richer model. Correlations, residual plots, or a proxy score do not count as testing it.

## Overlap and accounting

- **History representation family:** #2 asks whether history adds information to the current row; #11 proposes a signed/absolute summary; #18 changes the filter basis. A positive ridge result across these views is not three independent sources of signal. Use shared controls and report the exact tested representation.
- **Same-row feature geometry:** #3, #4, and #7 all add deterministic relations while retaining raw inputs, but rely on different assumptions (positive composition, compatible within-row scales, and compatible trade/quote scales). Do not merge them into a generic “geometry works” conclusion.
- **Cross-instrument relation:** #6 is an input-only rolling relation; #19 is a supervised, internally cross-fitted predictor of labels from i1. They must remain separate in both construction and claims.
- **Factor and graph structure:** #9 is a static sparse measurement-factor proposal; #10 is a temporal conditional-association proposal. Low rank or stable correlations alone establish neither mechanism.
- **Target distribution and optimization:** #12 and #22 concern sign/amplitude or observed-label spread; #16 and #25 concern pooled metric optimization and moment-denominator uncertainty. They are not tested by the prescribed WMSE ridge, and #25 is explicitly dependent on #16.
- **Training dynamics:** #13, #15, #20, #21, #23, and the defining regularizer in #24 need recurrent internals, gradients, updates, or higher-order derivatives. They remain `not_tested` regardless of EDA or ridge outcomes.

Execution reporting should distinguish (1) `measured_descriptive`, (2) `measured_fixed_ridge` with the exact frozen view and split, (3) `not_tested_budget_or_scope`, and (4) `invalid_or_inconclusive` if a validity/resource failure prevents interpretation. Do not mark an omitted candidate as a negative result, count correlated feature variants as independent confirmations, call a selected-panel score a recipe improvement, or infer learning-state/gradient mechanisms from linear features.
