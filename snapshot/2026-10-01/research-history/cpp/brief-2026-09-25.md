# Research brief (2026-09-25) — Wunder Alpha Connectome

Task: stream 112 float32 features per row (sequences of 20,000 rows, two instruments i0/i1,
L11 order-book encodings + trades + 8 extra columns a0..a7); predict t0, t1 (future-price indicators
of i0) on every row from step 99. Score = GLOBAL clipped weighted Pearson: pool all scored rows of all
sequences, clip targets and predictions to [-2, 2], weight |y|, per target, average t0/t1. Only ~9.4% of
rows are scored (is_scored hidden at test; validation mask given). Limits: 1 vCPU, 60 min for whole test
(~37M rows => <= ~80 us/row incl. Python callback), 20 MB zip, Python 3.11, CPU only. Training allowed on
train.parquet (10,607 seqs) and valid.parquet (1,873 seqs). No external data.

Leaderboard (public test): organizer GRU baseline 0.5935; top 0.6651; cluster 0.65-0.66.
Ours: baseline x0.25 output scale 0.6503 (removes clip loss; valid 0.6728); our GRU h128 wMSE +
aux heads 0.6488 (valid clean 0.6721). Validation overstates test by ~0.022.

Established facts (validation):
- 4 market classes by target quantum (tick size / scale): 0.12543, 0.24133, 0.46854, 0.65687.
  Class 3 (large tick, ~37% of seqs, ~46% of metric weight) is the bottleneck: within-class WP ~0.42-0.44
  vs 0.72-0.79 for classes 0-2.
- Targets change only on rows where i0 bid and ask price codes change; constant between.
- Price columns are global deterministic nonlinear encodings of book shape (not depth levels); all 11
  bid columns change together. Features look rank-Gauss transformed; sentinel -5.19934 = missing.
- Target law (exact for class 3, approximate for 0-2): with spread sp (ticks) and U = F - bid for one
  common future price F: F inside [bid, ask]: t0 = U - sp, t1 = -U; F above ask: t0 = U - sp,
  t1 = -(sp+2); F below bid: t1 = -U, t0 = -(sp+2). Knowing which side F lands (3-way branch) gives WP
  0.968; magnitude adds 0.02. F is a near-future price (reached ~1 book event after it changes), likely a
  short real-time horizon over irregularly spaced rows; exact definition not found.
- Global pooling makes the per-class/per-sequence target level matter (18-20% of weighted variance);
  per-sequence correlation losses ignore it. |y|-weighted MSE beats Huber, corr losses and a softmax
  over outcome codes with analytic readout. Aux CE heads (spread, branch, class) help slightly.
- Class-3 branch direction is hard: AUC ~0.74 from our GRU; earlier work: ~0.55-0.60 far from a price
  change, ~0.83 within 20 rows.
- is_scored is predictable from features (AUC ~0.9); training weights p(scored|x)^2 helped earlier.
- Train and valid differ in class mix (classes 0/1: 24% of train vs 40% of valid); test likely a later
  period than valid.
