## Skeptical reading of the frontier

* The strongest current candidate is the **two independent EMA-4 GRUs**. E-176/E-178 support about +0.0011–0.0013 on two held-out chain folds over the old 0.65-GRU/0.35-baseline structure. E-182 adds +0.0008–0.0009 **per member** from stretch-age weights; E-184's two-member package improves the old holdout by +0.0009–0.0015 at λ=0.02. The since-weight pair is a candidate, not a measured 0.663+ leaderboard result. A partner has to retain near-anchor accuracy (E-177); extra decorrelation by itself does not suffice.
* The active chunk-500, online-normalization, and distillation queue is pursuing *member diversity or compression*, not an identified missing source of information. Its diversity estimates come from two-epoch runs (E-179); the EMA-4 transfer is unproven. Chunking changes optimizer steps and EMA horizon, so comparisons need the matched 0.99975 decay (retry panel). Online normalization was neutral as a single at E-118; convolution's two-epoch win vanished under EMA-4 (E-168 → E-181). Distillation already failed on the final recipe (E-116) and same-teacher TD labels cost −0.0031 (E-174). Compression may still help runtime, but cannot be credited with an accuracy gain in advance.
* The biggest identifiable blind spot is **information timing in dense-scored, quiet i0 stretches**. Dense sequences carry 53.5% of score weight (E-139); class-3 dense WP is 0.385 versus 0.547 sparse, and 77% of that class-3 gap is the mix of *future* distances to the next i0 move (physicist F1). Future distance is an analysis coordinate only. At stretch midpoints the GRU and simple queue proxies are near chance (F4), whereas the GRU already knows the next-move side immediately before the move. More generic book capacity is thus a weak bet.
* The adversarial train/valid AUC ≈0.99 (E-094) does **not** establish unseen instrument identity. The target tick quantum is uniform by class across splits, and valid is time-blocked/interleaved with train (audit §3). Chain grouping prevents direct neighbor leakage (E-095/E-096) but short chains can still share a market period across folds. The 368-sequence holdout is explicitly chain-leaky. A two-fold/holdout gain can therefore overstate transfer to a fresh period. Conversely, the earlier claim that valid fine-tuning transfers 3× better because test resembles valid was refuted by same-pair analysis (skeptic panel). Do not treat a fitted λ≈0.02 from four leaderboard levels as a measured test-mask rate; shift and mask softness are confounded.
* Selection is not a future-mask leak: future distances barely improve mask AUC (physicist F6), and label-aware mask features improved AUC by only 0.0022 (E-103). `is_scored` on a future test row must never be used as a feature or gate. The reported perfect target law for class 3 (E-079) is a *future-path identity*, useful to derive training labels and diagnostics, not an online predictor.

## Ranked hypotheses and cheap falsifiers

All proposed screens use existing cached validation/prediction data or a bounded scan of validation rows. They are read-only, one CPU core and under 2 GB when implemented with memory maps and episode aggregates; none is a request to run them now. Score every change as the clipped ±2, pooled, per-target |clipped y|-weighted Pearson, with true-mask λ=0 primary and λ=0.02/0.05 sensitivity. Use chain-grouped held-out predictions and bootstrap **chains or i0 price episodes**, never rows. Pre-register the feature, cut points, and two fold signs before any later model fit.

### 1. Exogenous i1 innovation during an i0 price standstill — highest upside, genuinely different mechanism

**Mechanism.** A book-only GRU has little information before i0's queue race is decided. During a long i0 quote standstill, a directional i1 move may reveal common information before i0 reprices. Use the *change in decoded i1 mid since the last observed i0 price-code event*, together with i1 event count and elapsed real time; gate by the **causal** number of i0-quiet rows (for example 32 and 128), never by rows until a future move. Fit the i1 code dictionary on training data only. The output is a small residual correction or auxiliary head on the existing two-GRU pair, especially for class 3; do not spend a member slot on a weaker standalone model.

**Exact cheap falsifier.** On existing OOS EMA-4 predictions, aggregate one observation at the midpoint of each *completed* i0-quiet episode of at least 64 rows, using only the i1 displacement available at that midpoint. On training chains fit a sign-constrained, ridge-regularized 2-feature residual model (`i1 displacement`, `displacement × causal age≥32`); score on each held-out chain fold. Require the same coefficient sign on both folds, ≥0.10 event-level partial correlation with the next i0 move, and ≥+0.0003 pooled WP when the frozen correction is applied to the two-GRU pair; report class-3 dense far-row WP separately. Unknown i1 code transitions must be an explicit third bin, not dropped. A zero/negative fold or gain only under future-distance gating kills it.

**Likely gain / risk.** *Forecast* +0.0005–0.0020 LB if the conditional effect survives; this is the only single mechanism here plausibly capable of taking a large part of the remaining gap. The physicist's F5 saw +0.14 ±0.07 partial event correlation on about 205 long stretches, but E-149 found only +0.02–0.04 at full scale. The local signal is small-sample and may be decodable-code or regime bias. **Overlap:** tree B3, physicist H-P2, opus_micro H3; this proposal narrows them to causal standstill-conditioned *innovation* and frozen-pair residual lift. General i1 lead is already low priority (E-149).

### 2. Episode-equalized training weights for repeated future outcomes

**Mechanism.** In class 3 a long quiet stretch repeats the same future target over many scored rows. Dense sequences score more such rows yet have only ~27–31 effective target episodes per sequence (physicist F3). Ordinary row loss, even after causal stretch-age down-weighting, can spend many gradients on one outcome and favor noisy long episodes. Weight each *training* row by the inverse total metric weight of its **completed target-constant episode**, with a cap and a blend toward current weights. This changes estimation variance, while retaining the original metric for selection. Episode membership can be derived from targets only in offline training; at inference there is no episode feature or future observation.

**Exact cheap falsifier.** Before training, count weighted effective episodes and class shares under baseline, E-182 since-weight, and a capped inverse-episode scheme (`min(4, median episode weight / episode weight)`), by train and valid period. Then reuse cached OOS residuals as a diagnostic: within each episode compute the mean weighted prediction error; check whether high-multiplicity episodes have significantly more between-chain error variance or worse held-out WP after conditioning on causal age, class, and |y|. If the excess disappears after the E-182 weights, or the candidate weight profile halves effective score mass in class 3, stop. A later EMA-4 model would need **both members** improved on separate held-out chain folds, ≥+0.0007 pooled over the current since-weight pair.

**Likely gain / risk.** *Forecast* +0.0003–0.0012 LB. The exact inverse scheme optimizes an episode average rather than the contest's row-weighted objective and may underfit valuable long stretches; cap and interpolate. **Overlap:** E-182 stretch-age is the nearest successful result, but age is a causal proxy and does not equal an episode's realized multiplicity. Label-uniqueness is still open in tree D2/L8. Do not revive ordinary per-class weighting: E-104 failed.

### 3. Distinguish *intensity of depletion* from static queue imbalance

**Mechanism.** A static bid/ask depth proxy asks which queue is shorter. A race depends also on which side is being depleted by trades/cancellations and refilled. Estimate a few causally updated bid/ask queue-change intensities and use **asymmetric depletion velocity**, conditional on spread and current depth, to predict the next i0 price move. This is a targeted class-3 far-row cue and could complement the GRU where static volume features have little residual value. The [queue-reactive model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0563) motivates state-dependent order-flow intensities, while [Gould and Bonart](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03492) establish next-move directional information in queue imbalance; neither paper proves a gain in this anonymized contest.

**Exact cheap falsifier.** On a validation-only, chain-held-out screen, build two signed rolling counts from observed **past** i0 book/volume changes at horizons 16 and 64 i0 events. Fit one ridge/logistic next-move model on train chains using static ICA queue proxies alone, then add velocity and its interaction with depth. Evaluate one record per completed i0 episode at its causal age 32/64 snapshot. Require ≥+0.03 direction AUC and ≥+0.08 partial direction correlation on long episodes in each of two chain folds before considering a residual model. A gain confined to the last 1–3 rows before the move is redundant with the GRU and fails this hypothesis.

**Likely gain / risk.** *Forecast* 0–0.001 LB. Anonymized volume is mixed (E-090); simple queue proxies have AUC only ~0.62 and were unstable far from moves (physicist F4). **Overlap:** tree B2, prior queue-race H-P1, generic heuristic E-088/E-089 and MO split E-112 (failed); this test specifically isolates *directional depletion velocity at an earlier causal snapshot*. It should be killed cheaply if the event-level signal is absent.

### 4. Audit period leakage before trusting a +0.001 candidate

**Mechanism.** The mask and output head can specialize to an activity regime shared by short chains even when the exact stream is held out. The current fold variance estimate may thus be optimistic. This is an evaluation hypothesis with zero direct leaderboard gain but can prevent spending the remaining gap on a false positive, especially online norm and true-mask LP-FT.

**Exact cheap falsifier.** Build coarse period clusters using only sequence-level causal feature distributions and adjacency links; never use target or score to cluster. Recompute **existing cached** OOS pair deltas for EMA-4, since-weight and any queued candidate by leave-one-period-cluster-out aggregation, compared with chain-bootstrap deltas. Report effective cluster count and the largest cluster's score-weight share. If every fold's positive sign remains and period-bootstrap intervals overlap the chain intervals, period leakage is not material. If only 1–2 periods dominate, abstain from claims of hidden-period transfer; no leaderboard probe is justified.

**Likely gain / risk.** 0 direct; high decision value. Clustering can itself manufacture regimes, so predefine the feature list and number of clusters and compare with a simple chronological/adjacency grouping. **Overlap:** E-095/E-096 fixed direct chain-neighbor leakage; audit §3 and skeptic panel already question period independence. This tests the remaining, broader correlation.

### 5. Conditional scoring selection beyond propensity, used only for loss allocation

**Mechanism.** On valid, scored rows are more predictable than unscored rows even within a propensity bin (physicist F6); prop_v1 is not a sufficient summary of the scorer's causal state. A cross-fitted selector using only current/past activity, queue state and the *frozen* GRU's output magnitude may concentrate training on the right states. This is a loss-weight hypothesis, not a permission to infer or use the hidden test mask. It is lower priority because prop_v2 gave nothing under EMA-4 (E-170), and floor+aux already captured much of the gain.

**Exact cheap falsifier.** On existing OOS valid predictions, fit a shallow, monotone calibration table for `P(scored | prop_v1 bin, |two-GRU prediction| bin, causal activity bin)` on half the chain groups; evaluate mask log loss and calibration on the other half. Require ≥0.01 AUC *and* ≥0.01 absolute calibration reduction in the top propensity quintile in both fold directions, with stable weighted class shares. E-103's label-aware model gained only +0.0022 AUC, so a result of that size kills the route before training. A later EMA-4 comparison needs ≥+0.001 pooled WP over E-182 and no λ=0.05 loss beyond one bootstrap SE.

**Likely gain / risk.** *Forecast* 0–0.0007 LB. Selection fitted on valid masks can overfit the period or the GRU's own errors. **Overlap:** E-103, E-150/E-155/E-170, game_changers O4/prop_v3. This is a stringent revisit, not a new claim that the scorer depends on future labels.

## Decision

If one exploratory branch is available after the active queue, prioritize **#1**. Its signal is tied to the exact regime holding the most metric weight and proposes an information source the current book-based GRU may see too weakly. Treat #2 as a variance companion only if its episode diagnostic adds something beyond E-182. Use #4 to calibrate confidence in *all* positive fold deltas. None of these has been shown to close +0.0033; combining the existing since-weight pair's forecast with one independent, replicated information gain is the realistic path.
