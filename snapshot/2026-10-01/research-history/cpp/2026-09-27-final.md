## Prior winners and what transferred

- The [Optiver Trading at the Close first-place writeup](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/optiver-trading-at-the-close/writeups/hyd-1st-place-solution) and [DRW Crypto first-place writeup](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/drw-crypto-market-prediction/writeups/drw-solution-1st) both favor blending strong, complementary predictors. Here the strong-member gate selects the independent GRU over tree, baseline, chunk, and online-normalization partners; Optiver's cross-stock and online-label features are unavailable in this API.
- The [Jane Street Market Prediction winner](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/jane-street-market-prediction/writeups/cats-trading-yirun-s-solution-1st-place-training-s) used fold-local auxiliary supervision and seed averaging. The closest local transfers are the existing next-change auxiliary training task and independent EMA seed. Reading its unused auxiliary logit into the final pair failed at exact-score level (about −0.00002), so another head needs a distinct residual signal.
- The closer [Jane Street Real-Time 8th-place writeup](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/jane-street-real-time-market-data-forecasting/writeups/evgeniia-grigoreva-private-lb-8th-solution), with [author code](https://github.com/evgeniavolkova/kagglejanestreet), reports about +0.001 on *its* validation/leaderboard metric from several auxiliary responder horizons, alongside gains from seed averaging and a period-gap check. Our earlier next-2/4-change auxiliary test (E-107) was flat, so this is a low-priority analogue; a direct component-label construction would have to differ materially. Its largest online-learning gain used released lagged targets. Connectome supplies no target feedback; an earlier causal reconstruction recovered about 90.5% of mature class-3 labels, but per-sequence online recalibration **hurt badly** (E-080). Its score deltas do not forecast ours.
- The [2012 Kaggle Algorithmic Trading Challenge winner paper](https://www.ms.k.u-tokyo.ac.jp/sugi/2013/Kaggle.pdf) split *future response horizons* and averaged models fitted on different samples. The causal elapsed-event phase analogue here gained only +0.00005 pooled and failed its gate. A chain-resampled member is a possible future variance/diversity experiment, but its value must be tested as a partner.
- [Optiver Realized Volatility first place](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/writeups/nyanp-1st-place-solution-nearest-neighbors) gained from recovered time order and neighbor features. Neighbors from future sequences cannot be used in this strict row stream. Its transferable point is careful validation under period shift; the six-epoch gain is positive in all three precomputed train/valid shift terciles.
- [G-Research's competition recap](https://www.gresearch.com/news/wrapping-up-the-g-research-crypto-forecasting-competition/) highlights feature engineering in winning crypto models. Our broad feature and raw volume-shock additions failed; only a causally decoded signal that survives an event-level residual gate merits another model fit. [Queue-reactive modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0563) and [queue imbalance](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03492) motivate a concrete depletion/refill hypothesis, not a proven gain here.

## Decisions and ranked next experiments

1. **Keep the six-epoch gain where it is replicated.** Seed 3's pure-chain paired gain is +0.00121±0.00051 at λ=.02. Extending seed 4 was positive on folds 0/1 (+0.000571/+0.000191 at λ=.02), but the pooled gain over the mixed pair was only +0.000388±0.000317; λ=0 gave +0.000276±0.000334. Both miss the predeclared +0.0005 gate. The reserved holdout is inconclusive: both-six versus mixed is −0.000255±0.000709 on all 368 sequences but +0.002472±0.001976 on its 48 chain-isolated sequences. Keep the mixed pair ahead on OOF score evidence; the both-six package is a faster hedge whose score difference is uncertain. A class-specific switch is unsupported: class-3 six-epoch deltas reverse sign between the two seed-3 folds.
2. **Validation-period repetition helped the single, then failed the champion gate.** The `--valid-repeat 2` screen repeated existing eligible validation sequences and added about 12% optimizer steps. On fold 0 it beat the four-epoch parent by +0.0020±0.0009 at λ=.02 (+0.00211±0.00105 on 432 pure chains), yet adding it to the selected mixed pair gave only +0.000406±0.000929 on those pure chains, below the fixed +0.0005 paired gate. It is stopped before fold 1. Its single-model advantage over seed-3 six epochs was +0.00138±0.00183, too uncertain for a fallback switch. The gain over the old four-epoch single cannot be attributed solely to period weighting without an equal-step control.
3. **The shallower strong-member screen was positive but too small.** The Jane Street Real-Time author's [solution document](https://github.com/evgeniavolkova/kagglejanestreet/blob/master/solution.md) compares a one-layer GRU with a two-layer readout against a deeper GRU and keeps both in the ensemble. Our one-layer EMA+since seed-4 candidate beat the two-layer OOF proxy alone by about +0.0005 on fold 0, but replacing that proxy in the pair gained only **+0.000359±0.000522** at λ=0 and **+0.000352±0.000482** at λ=.02 on 432 pure chains. Both miss the fixed +0.0005 paired gate, so stop before fold 1 or final fitting. The proxy lacks since-weighting, so the small positive result cannot be attributed solely to depth. A runtime benefit remains plausible but unmeasured.
4. **Look for genuinely new class-3 information only after an event-level falsifier.** The hard, dense-scored class-3 regime carries a large fraction of weight. Both i1 and i0 raw 4/16-event volume-code displacement probes *reduced* held-out long-interval direction AUC on both folds. A future attempt should decode physical side/depth changes on train only and test asymmetric refill/depletion against the frozen pair at one causal snapshot per target episode, requiring +0.03 AUC and +0.08 partial residual correlation in both folds before GPU training. This is a speculative, high-effort branch.
5. **Audit period transfer before interpreting another +0.001 local gain.** The train/valid discriminator is strong; source-time and mask shift could alter hidden ranking. Use label-free period proxies and exact cached-score sums, with pure-chain and λ sensitivity, before trying period reweighting. Do not use full-sequence future statistics as inference features.
6. **Consider direct class-3 event-component supervision only after a label diagnostic.** E-079 approximates the target as a rounded mean across the next four book-price events. E-107 already tried 2/4-th *target-change* direction/time auxiliaries on an earlier two-epoch recipe and got −0.0001 to +0.0003, so a simple repeat has low prior. A genuinely different task would supervise the decoded mid-price component at events 1, 2 and 4, then reconstruct or jointly predict the main target. First quantify distinct component information and stable event decoding; only a fixed-coefficient fold-0 fit beating the current pair by +0.0005 would justify fold 1. This remains untested and speculative.
7. **If more GPU time becomes available, predeclare one additional independent seed.** Seed diversity has already improved the pair, and six-epoch gains differ materially between seeds 3 and 4. A fixed seed-5 EMA+since fit could replace the weaker member only if its *paired* gain clears +0.0005 on pure-chain folds 0/1 at both mask settings, followed by untouched folds 2/3 before choosing among multiple seeds. This follows the sample/seed averaging pattern in the [Jane Street](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/jane-street-real-time-market-data-forecasting/writeups/evgeniia-grigoreva-private-lb-8th-solution) and [2012 trading challenge](https://www.ms.k.u-tokyo.ac.jp/sugi/2013/Kaggle.pdf) writeups, but further seed search has a substantial winner's-curse and GPU cost.

## Failed screens that should stay closed

The four-epoch capped class-3 episode factor lost **−0.00083** on fold 0 at λ=.02, despite 23–31% more effective episodes; a different four-event uniqueness definition would require a new diagnostic. Weighting EMA teacher consistency by clipped `|y|` lost **−0.0001** against its parent on fold 0 and **−0.00079±0.00091** as a pair member against the selected mixed champion on 432 pure chains at λ=.02; it failed both predeclared gates and was stopped before fold 1. [Mean Teacher](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.01780) motivates consistency training but does not imply this weighting helps. A first-99-row causal regime correction lost **−0.00009** pooled. An activity-gated blend weight added **+0.00003**, below its +0.0005 gate. The unused auxiliary logit added **−0.00002**. i1 and i0 raw flow shocks lost about **0.01–0.04 AUC** on long class-3 midpoint samples. These are tests of specific implementations, not proofs that all possible selection or flow models fail.
