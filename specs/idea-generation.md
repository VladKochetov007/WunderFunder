# Idea generation and allocation

Use a research tree: objective → mechanism family → direction → hypothesis → experiment. Give interactions explicit nodes with multiple parent links. Record what changed from each parent and evaluate the actual combination; never add parent score gains arithmetically.

Generate diversity through different LLM families, coding harnesses, sources, contexts and creativity lenses. Assign both directed literature for a known bottleneck and random literature from the user's source library. Record the source passage, selection procedure/seed, actual provider/model, prompt and public output. A model catalog entry is not a successful call. Provider failure is transport evidence, not failed science.

Ask for a real attempt to transfer at least one component, including an unconventional idea. The headline method may be irrelevant while its sampling rule, decomposition, state representation, normalization, diagnostic, approximation or control is useful. Require the correspondence, assumptions, predicted observable, cheapest useful test, falsifier and estimated cost. Do not fabricate a connection to satisfy an idea quota.

Keep initial proposals independent: commit them before sharing competing proposals. Give a critic concrete assumptions to attack and checks to perform. Agreement between similar agents is weak evidence. Route actual implementations to capable coding agents; use deterministic checks and measurements to decide validity and gain.

Allocate by mechanism lineage and expected long-term value, including information gained and artifacts reusable by descendants. Start unknown arms with qualitative priorities/null numeric priors. Fill measured costs/effects only from relevant actual experiments. Preserve an exploration and confirmation/serving reserve while deepening useful leading branches.

Avoid low-value loops. After repeated uninformative allocations, move that recipe out of the active context, preserve its archive, and ask a fresh-context agent to reframe the task using different literature or assumptions. Do not feed the rejected framing back into the new agent as a mandatory explanation. Reopen a branch when a new representation, interaction or contradictory observation justifies a new experiment. Preserving knowledge does not require placing every old idea in every prompt.

The leading runnable experiment must have an owner and a short path to measurement. Broad ideation cannot become a dependency for obtaining that result. A weak standalone model can remain useful as a teacher or blend member; assess its declared contribution rather than rejecting it on standalone rank.
