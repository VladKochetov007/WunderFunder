# ADR-0001: Optimize long-term winning

Status: Accepted. Date: 2026-10-07.

## Context and evidence

The user explicitly prioritizes fundamental research, novel directions, useful submissions and long-term winning over short-term convenience. Repeating one narrow idea can miss gains from new representations or combinations.

## Decision

Use expected long-term competitive value, information gain and reusable artifacts to allocate effort. Deepen promising lineages while preserving exploration and confirmation/serving reserves. Keep the user's ambitious milestone separate from evidence-supported forecasts. [Specification](../research-objective.md).

## Alternatives and consequences

A pure greedy hill-climb is simple but can lock the task framing. Unbounded random ideation is diverse but can fail to produce results. Use the research tree to combine directed deepening with bounded independent exploration. Standalone losses do not universally reject useful teachers, residuals or combinations.

## Revisit when

The user changes the objective or measured costs show the current allocation produces insufficient information/results across distinct families.
