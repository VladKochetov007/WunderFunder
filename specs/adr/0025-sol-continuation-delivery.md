# ADR0025: bounded Sol continuation with independent Luna verification

Status: accepted, 2026-10-08. Basis: user's explicit continuation implementation plan and conduct-creative-research invocation.

Model exception superseded by [ADR0026](0026-luna-only-unattended-execution.md): the user withdrew premium-agent authorization; the Sol lead was stopped and its context transferred to Luna/max. Other experiment, resource and delivery requirements below remain binding.

The user authorizes one `gpt-6.1-sol` continuation lead at medium reasoning. Root retains admission, spending and synthesis; independent grader/packager and scoped builder use `gpt-6-luna` at max. Subsequent cost guidance reinforces Luna/max as the usual default. The available provider route does not name a separate Luna6.1 model. No additional Sol/Astra role is authorized by this exception.

The lead owes evaluated artifacts and submission delivery, including recovery, rather than source preparation alone. Isolated writer scopes and one serialized bounded support process protect the healthy local queue under five CPU cores, 20GB RAM and 5GB observed VRAM. Existing local v3 source is not hot-patched. Fixes are reviewed and applied through identified successors.

Exact Z3 fold0 recipe evidence is required before calling Z3 an expected improvement. H230 finishing comparisons answer a separate question. Fold1 checks measure robustness with exposure recorded; they are not automatically fresh confirmation. Public upload remains with the user.

Vast r5 is a sequential GRU512/768 Muon capacity cohort, ten-hour hard stop and at most $5 all-in reservation within existing credit and $20 cumulative authority. Laptop staging must total less than 100MB, including source and auxiliary inputs. Large target caches must have verified signed downloads or a checked remote reconstruction with new identities. Startup deadline and transfer budgets are checked before rental; insufficient download runway cannot silently cause a large laptop upload. HOLD covers bounded useful successor preparation, then teardown when useful work is exhausted.

Kaggle mounts are tested independently with executable boot markers before smoke v3 and disjoint two-T4 work. Empty logs alone do not prove no execution. Runner gates cover missing artifacts, dependencies, interrupted recovery, live heartbeat and sealed startup. Parsed-data loading and OS-cache reuse are different measurements.

The [continuation contract](../../research/campaign_2026-10-06/bandit_continuation/controller_claude_20261008/SOL_CONTINUATION_CONTRACT_20261008_R1.md) binds ownership and resource scope; the append-only continuation log and original receipts preserve prior failures and authorization. Other decisions, including independent code/weights and ADR0015 ownership/spending limits, remain binding.
