# Research portfolio control

Read the project's current objective, frontier, latest allocation receipts and
effective user constraints. In this repository start with
[the pipeline](../../../specs/research-pipeline.md) and
[research methods](../../../research/RESEARCH_METHODS.md). Older stop snapshots,
provider errors and numerical priors are not current authorization or results.

## Choose work by mechanism and information

Maintain objective → mechanism family → direction → hypothesis → experiment.
Keep separate allocation lanes for deepening leading mechanisms, independent
exploration and complete-endpoint confirmation/serving. Their proportions are
controller choices, updated from actual evidence and remaining compute; no fixed
percentage is universal. Seeds estimate training variation; they are not a
substitute for mechanism diversity.

For the next bounded allocation, write a short receipt with:

- The uncertainty to resolve, why now, and the expected reusable artifact.
- The candidate/control endpoint, actual ancestry, exposure and measured cost
  basis; leave unknown numerical effects null.
- Owner, admitted budget, exact next runnable action and a successor or repair.
- Conditions to deepen, combine, defer or reframe after the evidence lands.

An individually weak component can improve a teacher, residual representation or
served combination. Give an interaction its own node and parent links. Freeze the
weights/combining rule and compare the complete endpoint; parent gains do not add.
Use fitting-only or group-cross-fitted diagnostics to select complementary sources
where appropriate. Prediction disagreement alone does not establish useful signal.
Avoid testing every pair: use residual association, regime coverage and mechanism
differences to choose a few informative combinations.

## Refresh the framing

When repeated work produces little new information, inspect whether the bottleneck
is scientific or execution-related before replacing the direction. Missing data,
guard failures and queued hardware cannot disprove a mechanism. Defer the exact
low-value recipe with its evidence and reopening condition; preserve its archive.

Use `research-reframe` for a fresh source/lens/context assignment. Give the real
task, binding constraints and relevant observations, omitting stale explanations
that the agent is supposed to challenge. This is context hygiene, not hiding
contradictory evidence from a grader. Commit initial proposals before sharing
competing proposals. Later restore ancestor evidence during comparison/audit.

Use different actual LLM providers and coding harnesses when authorized. Mix
directed literature with recorded random passages from the user's library. Ask
for a serious attempt to extract at least one transferable component, including
an unconventional one: a sampling rule, representation, normalization, diagnostic
or state update can transfer even when the headline method does not. Require the
structural correspondence and falsifier; do not fabricate applicability.

Each proposed card owes a mechanism, assumptions, predicted observable, cheapest
discriminating test, likely failure mode, cost and descendant/combination path.
Deduplicate mechanisms after independent proposals, not before they can differ.

After first commitment (so ideation stays independent), add a triage block before
spending fits: (1) **ceiling** — best case computable from existing predictions or
checkpoints (oracle/seed-ensemble blend, error budget, information audit) and its
source; (2) **mechanism presence** — a zero-fit probe that the deficit exists in the
artifact we actually ship, not only in a cold screen; (3) **transfer path** — the
stages between the screen and the served endpoint and any measured multiplier;
(4) **detectability** — ceiling versus panel standard error and public noise;
(5) **decision** — the action each outcome changes. Order checks analytic →
zero-fit → small screen → stage-matched transfer. The block sets priors and order;
it is not a kill switch (ADR0009). See the
[triage reflection](../../../research/unified/methods/IDEA_TRIAGE_REFLECTION_20261029_R1.md).
Use the current model preference and provider-access checks from AGENTS.md; a
catalog entry or launched CLI is not a delivered proposal.

## Integrate outcomes

Keep validity, scientific interpretation and compute allocation separate. A
confidence interval crossing zero is not a universal rejection rule. Bayesian
learning-curve forecasts are conditional on their recipe/priors, never a ceiling
on future representations. Allocate a bounded follow-up when it can resolve a
useful uncertainty; preserve full-budget tests alongside cheap screens.

Before promotion, use independent `research-evidence-review`; before expensive
replication or recipe changes, use `research-recipe-audit`. Every runnable fit needs
an end-to-end owner using `research-experiment-delivery`. Do not let ideation,
criticism or a queue replace measured results.

Append outcomes and lineage decisions to the scientific ledgers and tree journal;
update the compact frontier and regenerate navigation after meaningful evidence.
Commit public source prompts, code, measured results and decisions in meaningful
steps. Preserve raw negatives and superseded recipes. Do not archive credentials
or private reasoning transcripts.
