# Fresh-context research reframing

Receive the actual objective, hard constraints, evaluator semantics and a small set of decisive observations. Keep the stale explanation and other agents' proposals out of the initial packet unless necessary to avoid duplicating a completed test. Preserve old evidence in the archive; removing an idea from active context does not delete it or make exposed data fresh.

Use different model families, coding harnesses, sources and creativity lenses when actually available. Record exact provider/model/access outcome. A requested model, catalog listing or transport failure is not an executed ideation result. Rotate roles where useful; keep initial proposals independent until committed.

Assign directed literature around a concrete bottleneck and random books/articles/passages from the authorized library/source pool. Record selection procedure/seed and exact passages. Ask for a real attempt to find at least one transferable component, including an unconventional route: sampling, decomposition, state, geometry, normalization, approximation, constraint, diagnostic or control. A paper's headline need not transfer. Do not fabricate applicability if attempts find no defensible correspondence.

For each distinct proposal return: source component, structural analogy, intervention, assumptions, predicted observable/direction, cheapest informative test, falsifier, approximate cost, reusable artifact and mechanism-family label. Cluster semantic duplicates. Distinguish speculative explanation from source-established fact.

Create new tree arms or interaction children without invented numerical priors. An earlier loser can become a useful teacher, residual or component; specify the actual combined comparison. Do not add ancestor score gains or universally reject a family from one failed recipe. Prioritize expected long-term utility and information gain under the current allocation.

Hand off a bounded implementation/test task with explicit result ownership. Broad ideation must not hold a leading runnable experiment hostage. Use `conduct-creative-research` for overall orchestration and claim-appropriate grading/reproduction; this skill supplies fresh hypotheses, not approval to launch unbudgeted experiments.
